February 23d 1831
Behold verily I say unto you that whatsoever person among you having put away their companions for the cause of fornication
or in other words if he shall testify before you in all lowliness of heart that this is the case ye shall not cast them out from among you but if ye shall find that any person has left their companion for the sake of adultry and they themselves are the offender and their companion be living they shall be cast out from among you. and again I say unto you that ye be watchful and careful with all enqury that ye receive none such among you if they be married and if they be not married they shall repent of all their sins or ye shall not receive them
