Hyram [Hiram] Portage County Ohio Febry. 27th. 1832
Behold thus saith the Lord unto you my servants that I have chosen Lincoln [Haskins] to be a servant unto me wherefore verily I say unto you lit [let] him be ordained and receive the articles and covenants which I have givin unto you and some of the commandments that he may go forth and proclaim my gospel whithersover I will send him in the congregations of the wicked and in asmuch as he is faithful I will prosper him even so Amen
