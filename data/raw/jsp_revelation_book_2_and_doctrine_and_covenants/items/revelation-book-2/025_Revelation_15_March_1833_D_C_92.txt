Kirtland 15th March 1833—
Verely thus saith the Lord I give unto the united firm organized agreeable to the commandment previously given a revelation & commandment concerning my servant Frederick [G. Williams] that ye shall receive him into the firm what I say unto one I say unto all. and again I say unto you my servent Frederick thou shalt be a lively member in this firm and inas much as thou art faithful in keeping all former commandments thou shalt be blessed for ever Amen — — — —
