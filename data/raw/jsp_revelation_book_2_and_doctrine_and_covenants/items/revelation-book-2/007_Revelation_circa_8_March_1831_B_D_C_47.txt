Hiram Portage Co Ohio March 7th. 1831
Behold it is expedient in me that my servent John Whitmor [Whitmer] should write and keep a regular history and assist my servant Joseph in translating all things which shall be given him, and again verily I say unto you that ye can also lift up your voice in meetings when ever it shall be expedient and again I say unto you that it shall be
appointed unto you to keep the church record and history continaly for Oliver [Cowdery] I have appointed to an other office wherefore it shall be given thee by the comforter to write these things even so Amen
