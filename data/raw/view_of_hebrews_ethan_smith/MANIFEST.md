# Ethan Smith, *View of the Hebrews* (Poultney, VT: Smith & Shute)
Argues that Native Americans descend from the lost tribes of Israel. It is the usual candidate source for the BoM's framing idea, and Oliver Cowdery's family attended Ethan Smith's Poultney congregation. Topic and period are matched, but it is not KJV pastiche.
