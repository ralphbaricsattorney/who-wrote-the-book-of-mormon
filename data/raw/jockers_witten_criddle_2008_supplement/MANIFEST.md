# Jockers, Witten & Criddle (2008) — public supplementary data

- **Source:** Stanford Digital Repository, https://purl.stanford.edu/rs276tc2764 ("Supplementary data for 'Reassessing authorship of the Book of Mormon using delta and nearest shrunken centroid classification'", *Literary and Linguistic Computing* 23 (2008), doi:10.1093/llc/fqn040). License **CC BY 3.0**; all files world-downloadable. Files fetched 2026-11-12 from https://stacks.stanford.edu/file/druid:rs276tc2764/<file>.
- **Files:**
  - `purl_metadata.json` — the repository's JSON record (description incl. the Jan 2009 tokenisation-error note: Alma 52 flips from Rigdon to Spalding after correction).
  - `bom.xml` — the authors' Book of Mormon text split into **239 chapter units** (`<book><author>Unknown</author>…<bookName>x1ne1</bookName><text>…`), 1.5 MB. Edition appears to be the modern (1981-style chaptering, modern punctuation) LDS text, not the 1830.
  - `dataMatrix.txt` — tab-separated **word-frequency matrix**: 456 rows (text samples) × 19,993 word types. Row labels / sample counts: Barlow 12, Cowdrey 19, Isaiah 66, Longfellow 23, Malachi 4, Pratt 53, Rigdon 23, Spaulding 17, Unknown (BoM chapters) 239. **Counts only — the candidates' raw texts (their Rigdon, Cowdery, Spalding, Pratt samples) were NOT published.**
  - `cluster1-3.jpg` — dendrograms from the paper's supplement (50 19th-c. novels, 15 authors).
- **Schaalje, Fields, Roper & Snow (2011)** "Extended nearest shrunken centroid classification" (*LLC* 26): **no public corpus or data found** (web search, 2026-11-12). Their text samples apparently derive from the Jockers et al. matrix plus additional Smith samples; would need to be requested from the authors.
