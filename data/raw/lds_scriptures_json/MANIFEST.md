# LDS standard works as JSON (bcbooks/scriptures-json)
Source: https://github.com/bcbooks/scriptures-json (see README.md here). Verse-level JSON, modern (2013) LDS text.
- book-of-mormon.json: modern chapter/verse BoM. Good for alignment and indexing, but it carries the 1837/1840/1981 edits. Use the 1830 text or the manuscripts for style.
- doctrine-and-covenants.json: modern D&C. Sections 3–40 or so are the 1828–1831 revelations: the same dictator, often the same scribe (Cowdery), and the same period. JSP Revelation Book 1 (if downloaded in `jsp_*`) is the earlier text.
- pearl-of-great-price.json: Book of Moses (JST Genesis, 1830–31) and Book of Abraham (1835–42). These are other Smith "translations" in KJV register.
- old-testament.json / new-testament.json: KJV with verse structure. Use for Isaiah (BoM quotes Isa 2–14, 29, 48–54) and Malachi 3–4 (3 Nephi 24–25), and as a register baseline.
