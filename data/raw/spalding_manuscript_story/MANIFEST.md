# Solomon Spalding, "Manuscript Story" (the Oberlin/Honolulu manuscript)
This is the only surviving Spalding fiction: written c. 1812, found in Honolulu in 1884 and held at Oberlin College. It is not the hypothesized "Manuscript Found." The Spalding–Rigdon theory posits a lost second manuscript.
