# David Whitmer, *An Address to All Believers in Christ* (Richmond, Mo., 1887)

- **Source:** https://archive.org/details/addresstoallbeli00whit (BYU Harold B. Lee Library copy; scan sponsor BYU).
- **Fetched:** 2026-11-12 from archive.org (metadata API + `_djvu.txt` OCR download) via `scripts/ia_get.sh` (or direct curl for items whose OCR filename contains spaces). Generic client.
- **Cleaning:** `*.clean.txt` made by `scripts/build_cowdery_and_ocr.py`: de-hyphenation across line breaks and joining of wrapped lines only. OCR errors, running heads, page numbers and library stamps are NOT removed. Raw OCR kept as `*.djvu.txt`.
- **Author:** David Whitmer (1805–1888), one of the Three Witnesses; printed pamphlet, 75 pp.
- **Files:** `addresstoallbeli00whit.djvu.txt` (raw OCR, ~45,500 words), `addresstoallbeli00whit.clean.txt` (~45,200 words), `.metadata.json`.
- **Caveats:** Whitmer wrote at age ~82, possibly with editorial help from family/associates in the Church of Christ (Whitmerite); contains long quotations from the Book of Mormon, Book of Commandments and D&C (mask before stylometry). Eyewitness account of the translation (seer stone in hat) is in Part First, ch. 2. OCR quality good but imperfect (front-matter stamps garbled).
