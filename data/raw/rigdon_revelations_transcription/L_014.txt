and thou hast not up till this time honored thy calling, but hast treated it lightly, and I have called upon thee now to arise and shake thyself from the dust of Babylon, that thou mayest deliver thyself from her sins and her plagues.– I the Lord know thee, there is not a thought of thine heart but is present with me, you cannot deceive him with whom you have to do, for I the Lord your God is not man that he can be imposed upon, therefore I send this my word to you for the last time.

And verily, verily I say unto thee, that if thou dost not awake from the sleep of spiritual death and do as I require, I swear in my wrath thou shalt never enter into my rest, I will hurl thee down at a time when thou thinkest not, and thou shalt be cast out as salt that lost its savor, and I the Lord will shew thee no mercy in the day of thy calamity, neither will I hear thy prayrs in the day when judgment and wrath shall overtake thee, thy name shall be blotted out from the Book of rememberance before me saith the Lord, and thou shall wail in tribulation, and lament in dispair, I the Lord say this in mine anger, and unless thou hearest, and comest forth with thine whole heart before me saith the Lord, calling upon my name continually for Grace to enable you to do my will, verily saith the Lord my wrath shall burn against thee until thou shall raise thy voice in howling lamentation when hope has forever perished.
