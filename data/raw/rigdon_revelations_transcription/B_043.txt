And again saith the Lord it is of vast importance that the people who are appointed to redeem Zion and gather Israel should understand the true condition of things pertaining to both themselves and Israel. It is not possible that Israel could be gathered under the ruling of the present law of life as pertains to man "three score and ten" During this period of mans life there would not be time for the true priesthood to qualify itself for the vast duties pertaining to that work, there must be added a longer lease of life. The prophets of former years who had inquired into these things, saw and understood this great fact, & in their prophecies made mention of it. Let the children of Zion read those prophecies and they will see these facts set forth unmistakeably."

Before closing I wish to say that these facts have been in our possession for upwards of 40 years. The promise made to us about the renewal of our age was made by Joseph Smith Sen. who was the first patriarch. Its renewal took place many years afterwards by one who had succeeded to the patriarchate; But this one knew nothing of the promise having been made by his predecessor: But declared the same things of his own knowledge of them. The confirmation also was a length of time after the giving & renewal of the promise. In this respect it is like the promise made to Abraham. This whole matter now is put on paper by the requirement of the Lord, for purposes known to himself to be manifested in due time of the Lord. When & how this greatest of All promises will be fulfilled, will be manifested in the due time of the Lord. But it is his will that all those who receive his gospel, or have received it should all understand this the greatest of all facts pertaining to the gathering of Israel.
