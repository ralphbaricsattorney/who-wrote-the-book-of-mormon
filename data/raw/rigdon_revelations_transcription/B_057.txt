## heading: Minutes of a conference of the church of Jesus Christ of the Children of Zion held on the 4th day of July 1877 held at the house of Stephen Post Province of Manitoba Canada on Lot No 31 Township 2 range 5[?]. E.

The conference was organized by calling Br. Stephen Post to preside & Br Thomas Sellers as vice president who were Elected Unanimously Br A. H. Black was called to act as Clerk. The worthy president gave a most instructive & scriptural lecture on the present position of Zion. After which a child named James belonging to AH & Jemima Black was blessed under the hands of prest. Br S. Post & vice prest. T. Sellers. Conference adjourned at 12:30 for refreshments.

Conference met again at 3,30 P M When the worthy prest again addressed the meeting relative to the duties of the respective officers urging them to live up to the rule laid down, after which a debate was held to see if it was necessary to elect additional officers which was considered unnecessary at present except one who was nominated for a priest Also a lengthened debate was held on the necessity of sending out ministers to preach the gospel but it was considered impossible at present on account of so much water all over the country By the unanimous consent of the church Sister Celestia Hildreth was nominated for a priestess & ordained under the hands of Stephen & Jane Post & blessed by Jane Post. This concluded the business of the Conference at present. Conference adjourned till the first sabbath in Oct. 1877

## signed: Stephen Post prest. A. H. Black Clerk
