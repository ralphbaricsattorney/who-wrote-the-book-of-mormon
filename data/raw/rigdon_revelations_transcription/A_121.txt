## heading: Section 71st
## date: Oct 10th 1872
To my servants Stephen and Israel.

Behold saith the Lord I require that all those who have been ordained to holy offices in my Zion will arise and slumber no more but go forth in my name and preach my gospel whereever[?] there is a door opened. And I the Lord say that they shall seek diligently to obtain open doors where they can preach my gospel, and if they do this faithfully I the Lord insure to them a reward; but if they do not this, I will afflict them and scourge them until they shall be laid waste and shall perish from the land of the living; for they will learn and shall know that the pestilence is in my hand and I will send it abroad for their destruction.

And verily saith the Lord I am angry with my servant Israel he desired an authority in my kingdom and I the Lord bestowed it on him and have blessed his family: but be astonished O ye powers of righteousness! he shews more regard for the things of this world than for the duties of his holy calling. Now I the Lord have sworn and will not repent that unless my servant Israel leaves off his worldly cares, which are a snare to his soul, and will be a curse instead of a blessing, I the Lord his God will lay my hand upon him and his money and property which he has been trying to lay up for his family. I the Lord swear in my wrath that his seed after him shall never inherit it. I say to my servant Israel as I say to all, I the Lord your God require all you have and are to be used for the building of Zion. There are those living through whose sacrifice of all they had brought the gospel out of darkness and it was and is proclaimed to you, And had they not have done so you and your family would have been cast down to hell, and were it not now for their exertions you never can enter the "rest of God" therefore seeing salvation has come to you and yours through their sacrifice — and such a sacrifice as none living but themselves know. — I the Lord require that you should see that they are crowned with blessing at your hand and that not sparingly but bountifully. Know O all ye Children of Zion that to the heavens it is well known what you are doing and what you can do, and I the Lord will have no excuses I require obedience at your hand & if I the Lord can not have this, Behold & lo! & fear & tremble!! for I will take the priesthood away from you, and consign you to perish and be damned with the Gentiles. Let every soul then who has received a priesthood according to the law of Zion, Arise trim their lamps and commence immediately to prepare to thrust in the sickle and reap lest judgment overtakes you at an hour when you think not. Amen.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon.
