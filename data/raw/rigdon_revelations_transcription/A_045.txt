Thus proving to the principalities & powers above that they had powers to judge between the things of God & the cunning of the devil & having done so they have obtained before the Lord their God the judgment. I the Lord say to the Children of Zion not only those that now are but ever shall be that had Noah Daniel & Job Moses and Samuel been placed where those five were they could have done no more to save Zion from an entire overthrow than did these five Saviors who have come up on the mountain of Zion to save Zion & judge the mountain of Esau; for all those five could have done would have been to have maintained the truth in righteousness before me as the five Saviors seen by Obadiah did.

And again inasmuch as these five judges have had power through their faith to judge all things pertaining to the kingdom of heaven on earth they shall judge the world in the last day. All that now live or shall live to the end of time, for in the great and final day of judgment they shall stand as Obadiah saw them, appearing as gold tried and purified by fire, and through them shall the world be judged. Men may now reject the word of salvation which goes through them for the redemption of Zion but the judgment of God that will go through them at the last day they shall not escape but it shall come upon them to condemnation saith the Lord your God. And now behold saith the Lord concerning those who receive my word at the hand of my saviors & judges as they are the servants and handmaidens of light and my messengers to the nations they must be great in my sight, yea, very great, for of necessity in order that they may do my will they must be partakers with the saviors & judges in all the endowments, powers & visitations of the kingdom of heaven, that they may be the messengers of light to the nations in deed in truth & in power. Behold saith the Lord they also have been seen thousands of years before they were born, in the visions of heaven by the prophets who have gone before them, and have lived at the time when those ancient prophets did. They saw them at the great and glorious work of gathering Israel & beheld them as they are & will know them again when they meet them in another world. Those ancient prophets were inspired to foretell what would take place in relation to Israel till the time of their restitution. And to understand these things they devoted all their energies before the Lord their God. And they saw in the visions of heaven the great events that were to transpire between the time they lived & the time when Israel would be gathered, yea, they beheld in the visions of heaven the way by which the dispersed tribes of Israel would be brought back to the lands and possessions which by covenant had been promised to their fathers. And now behold saith the Lord the Zion that now is & the messengers that will go from her midst are the ones they saw going forth among all people nations tongues Languages & kindreds
