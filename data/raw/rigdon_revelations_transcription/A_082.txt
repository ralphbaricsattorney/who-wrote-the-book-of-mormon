## heading: Section 48th. On missions to Europe Canada west & Southern states.

Behold saith the living God to the authorities of a living Zion You will cause to be separated unto the work whereunto I the Lord call them of one of the 12 and one of the 70 to be sent on a mission to preach the gospel to the colony of coloured people in the country which is called among you Canada West. Let them go thither trusting in me saith the Lord that their labour may not be in vain. Though in the first instance this mission is essentially to the colony it is not exclusively so for any of the white race who may believe you will minister to them also. And again saith the Lord let another mission be organized of two of the 12ve and four of the 70 to be sent to the southern states, and let them preach to both races as opportunity may serve. And let there be another mission organized before me saith the Lord to consist of three of the 12 and six of the 70 to be sent to Europe. Let this mission be prepared before they go so that they take with them the books & papers that may be necessary for a successful discharge of their duties. They will take with them the appeal to the Latter Day Saints, My servant Stephen's letter to Joseph Smith, There must also be taken with them the history of the melchisidean priesthood as well as the history of the two servants spoken of in the 24th Chap. of Matthew. I the Lord also require that my servant Stephen Post write a treatise upon the callings of God, to be taken with them. In addition my servant John A. Forgeus shall write to go with them a document on polygamy, prepare all these things for this mission that they may take them with them. And if through their faith & diligence their mission is a successful one through which they can get means for that purpose let all these things be printed in a book to be had both in Europe and this country.

My servant J. H. Newton will also write a paper on the 20 years exile of my servant Sidney shewing the cause as set forth in the D. C. (sec[?] 85[?] par 3d.) to be for perfecting his priesthood. for the salvation of all Zion, Jew & Gentile and that after the priesthood was perfected he was first to go to the Gentiles (as is now doing) & after that is done then comes the gathering of Israel, when the arm of the Lord is to be revealed in power. Let him shew that the perfecting of his priesthood was essential to the salvation of Zion, as well as to that of the Gentiles & it was also essential to the gathering of Israel.
