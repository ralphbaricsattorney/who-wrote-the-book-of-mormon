Thus saith the Lord, after full trial, being subject to all the devices of Satan, and yet overcome through the spirit of truth that is in them, and change not from their adherence to the Lord, but seek counsel from him, though all should forsake the way of truth and go after unrighteousness. Let Father and mother if they will, wife and children turn away till they are left alone and none to comfort them, yet with all this they move not neither are they shaken, but adhere to the Lord and the word of his grace; and though life itself should be taken, their purpose can never be changed, nor their determination yielded. They set their faces Zion ward & will reach its glories either in life or death.

Hunger, destitution or nakedness may be their lot in time; they will strengthen themselves in the Lord, and stay themselves on the God of their salvation, until deliverance comes by the mercy of their God. Of such the Lord chooses his servants and handmaidens, and though such will and must suffer great affliction, and go up through great tribulation, yet, they will wash their robes and make them white in the blood of the Lamb. Let none be mistaken, for if in this any are mistaken it will be to them a fatal error an irrecoverable mistake. The Lord is no respecter of persons, his plan is the same to all, And when the number of the eternally glorified are made up and all received, it will then be seen that all in all ages who have entered into the glory of paradise, have entered there according to the same law. Be ye therefore wise and understand what the will of the Lord is.

The glory of paradise into which those enter immediately after death, or at the time of death. For as soon as their spirits are separated from their bodies, Angels bear them off to their glory. This is the place your ancient brethren called Abrahams Bosom. This glory is the greatest glory that disembodied spirits can reach. It is the great seat where all the records of heaven[?] are kept. In this seat of glory is the great recording angels[?] seat, the greatest of all spirits, who has not as yet passed through the flesh. In this glorious pavillion are all the records of heaven kept. There, are all things recorded pertaining to the entire history of creation. There will the redeemed spirits learn all things pertaining to their existence.
