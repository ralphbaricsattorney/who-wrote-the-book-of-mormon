How then did Zion ever get out of that darkness see the 98th sec. par. 11th and there you will see how alone that could be done. Had there no persons at that time have done as there required Zion would nor could not ever have existed. But there were those who seeing the law there written, commenced the struggle before the Lord, and would not, and did not cease till they had obtained the victory. Not only day after day, & night after night were they before the Lord but year after year. They ceased not until they had prevailed, and Zion now appears & thus it was she came.

Here was a long struggle for Zion that none others will ever know but those who were engaged in it But we will here close

There is a lesson for you which belongs to the prophetic school. Take the prophecies of Isaiah, you will there read that the first part of that prophecy shews an entire departure of all the world from the living God: But Isaiah saw the Lord high and lifted up, and the whole world in obedience to him. Now the whole prophecy of Isaiah, gives the prophetic history of what would take place from the time when "all" had departed from God till the time came, till the earth should be full of his knowledge. Take this subject and make it your own. But I am tired.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## heading: Section 91st
## date: 1874 Dec 24

My Sister Post: I have a message from the Lord to you & all the sisters, he says they must go to reading the revelation of John, When reading you must be particular when you come to the name of a person, or of an angel, spoken of separately you will note them down and the place where they will be found, and also the earthquakes mentioned, note them down and the places where they will be found, and be particular and read closely and do this work perfect as the Lord has required of you!!

The Lord intends for all to be engaged in his work that we may perfect our priesthood, and make our salvation sure. The time has come that every one must be at work in doing what our heavenly Father requires of his children
