## heading: Section 10th.
## date: 1864 Aug 15th
## note: Item via J. H. Newton.

"I have obtained through the spirit that you and the brethren who operated with you in warning the members of the old church, and calling their attention to the written word of God, has been done in accordance with his (the Fathers) will, that he accepts the work at your hands, and releases you from any further obligations in the case. And that he now requires of you to turn your whole attention to getting the priesthood settled in the place appointed for that purpose. Let the united prayers of every brother and sister there and elsewhere be with united voice sent up to this end, that this great object may be obtained. Let this be the unceasing desire of all not to pray for every thing but for this one thing with the Assurance that if you all do so, your prayers shall be answered."

## heading: Section 11th.
## date: 1864 Sept 19th

The word of the Lord concerning the priesthood of Zion and her children. Thus saith the Lord to his servant Stephen lift up thy head and rejoice and be exceeding glad, for all the principalities and powers above are rejoicing when they see thee going forth to found Zion in everlasting strength no more to be thrown down but to continue forever by the priesthood that have now come forth out of the midst of darkness as seen by Lehi in his great vision, and are bringing to light my word saith the Lord. Fear not therefore neither be dismayed, for the angels have charge over you to shield you in the day of trouble. Go thy way with joy in thine heart and songs of praise upon thy lips. I the Lord send thee on this great Mission to prepare the way before the face of him whom I have placed at the head of Zion. To this work thou wast called from the beginning, but thou knewest it not, but behold in due time I the Lord have called thee because thou wast sufficient and able to do this work, to be a mouth piece for my servant Sidney. Therefore treasure up in thine heart my word, saith the Lord, that out of that treasury the spirit of holiness may bring forth things both new & old, that thou mayst shew thyself a workman before the Lord, that need not be ashamed, being able rightly to divide the word of truth & give to all their portion of meat in due season. I the Lord say unto thee again and not unto thee only but unto all thy brethren of the priesthood, let them also unite with thee in songs of joy praise & thanksgiving casting off all fear and gloom, and let them be clothed with the garments of praise. For behold saith the Lord
