Verily verily saith the Lord there are of those who have sinned unto death for whom there is no forgiveness in this world nor in the world to come. Therefore let the high council of my church be organized as speedily as possible so that offenders may be brought to trial as soon as it can be done, for it is the determination of the Lord your God to deal with such who have despised my word and insulted and slandered my servant through whom my word was sent into Zion as their crimes deserve both in this world and the world to come; and I the Lord begin with their judgment in this world, that their judgment in the next may speedily follow. There are those who are troubling you, that I the Lord have marked to be removed from the tribunals of this world to that of the next, and that speedily for their days are numbered and the time for their eternal judgment is at hand. I the Lord demand of my people that John A. Forgeus be brought before the council of my kingdom with as little delay as possible and inasmuch as he has rejected & despised my word that I the Lord sent to him for his salvation; let that word be the standard by which he shall be tried.

A person may believe all the books that I the Lord have caused to be written, or at least profess to do so; but if after their belief they reject my word which I send to them to bring them to understanding, that rejection will damn them, and do it eternally too, and the word that such have rejected shall be the word by which they shall be tried and condemned, both in this world and in the next. Therefore after John A. Forgeus has been tried and condemned by the revelation he has rejected through you, he shall be called from hence into the world of spirits where his disembodied spirit shall be brought into judgment, and there he will to his horror and dismay be confronted with the same revelation. It will there be to his spirit as a flaming fire, as of a heated furnace, under the influence of which he will writhe in eternal torments. I the Lord have given you this that you may understand upon what principles the future judgment will be conducted.

Those who have rejected the book of Mormon will at the future judgment be confronted with that book. So my saints may see that when they condemn a person by the law of heaven & cut them off when the spirit of that person is separated from the body
