Again saith the Lord it will require great prudence in those who go abroad in the Land to build Zion on the foundation where the Lord has appointed.

Faith patience with endurance and determination must be unceasing; and be careful that none of you assume the place of the devil and become accusers of one another. Let each one accuse himself before the Lord, and all will be well with you. Let each one be certain that his own heart is right before the Lord before he ventures to accuse any one.

Know assuredly that the devil is by way of preeminence[?], called the accuser of the brethren, and if one is found too much engaged in so doing, know that he has been familiar with Satan and let his case be enquired after and let such an one be rebuked by the presidency, and if he refuse the correction of the authorities let them cease to be of your number. Beware of trifling with your privilege before the Lord.

Br & Sister in their petition to the Lord as sent to us was verily guilty of this, and the Lord requires that we should rebuke them in his name for it. It was in asking for information on matters which are antiquated and belong to a people which have long since ceased. This was coupled with the question of tithing. Zion has nothing to do with Horeb or Sinai. Zion has a bishop to attend to the tithing and she needs nothing else, And the Lord says forbid them in my name for forever doing so again. As to the tithing asked for it is for you as you progress to do it yourselves.

Thus have I the Lord given you information eternal
