I hope you will all see the importance of this work it is for our salvation that the Lord has required it of us, go to work with willing hearts and ready hands to accomplish the work in righteousness before our heavenly Father. It is the will of the Father that Zion shall be built up by the law governing the school of the prophets, & all must be under that law and taught according thereto. So let all look for lessons, and let them strive to excel each other in learning them.

Your brother in Christ Jesus.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon.

## heading: Section 92nd
## date: 1875 Feb. 24th

"To Stephen, my spokesman in Zion, whose surname is Post, I the Lord dedicate this my will to the children of Zion, concerning the redemption of my kingdom saith the Lord.

From days of old and before you were, I was arranging the affairs of my house to bring about the triumph of Zion. And I the Lord move that cause from time to time as there are those who can stand against the wiles of the adversary, and not be shaken from their purpose: but through all the gloom which surrounds them, and all the darkness which closes them in, they still maintain their steadfastness: And though all others are seduced from under the power and influence of the word of the Lord, they are found holding on to the rod of iron, neither taking power to themselves, nor following after the influence of the designs of deceivers; but waiting on the Lord their God with steadfastness and full purpose of heart. And though all others turn away from the counsel of the Lord; they say, where shall we go? for safety can only be found under the direction of the revelations of heaven.
