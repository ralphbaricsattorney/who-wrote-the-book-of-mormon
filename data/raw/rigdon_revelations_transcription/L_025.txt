you desired of me to know my will concerning you have I not made this known to you have I not said it is my will that ye should organize the church according to the order of the Holy priesthood. This order is recorded in the book of doctrin[?] and covenants given through my servant Joseph in the beginning this is your pattern Saith God Now therefore set my house in order as fast as you can and prune my vineyard within the appointed time, for a sufficient time is given you and inasmuch as ye do it within the time blessed are ye, it shall be said of you blessed are ye enter into the joys of thy Lord, and if ye will not do my will within the appointed time I will call upon another people for I will have a people to serve me Saith God I am not to be mocked at in these last days even the dispensation of the fulness of times, therefore cast from you your much laughter and light mindedness and suffer not the spirit of contention to enter least my spirit withdraws from you Now you will say who has the right to preside, who is appointed of me, whose right was it heretofore, have I wrested it from him nay verily nay I know his afflictions & were it not for my power he could not stand I will uphold him until the deliverer comes if he is faithful, to me saith God now therefore cast away all your selfishness and learn of me who is meek and lowly of long suffering & kind & will teach you from time to time as a father teaches his sons and daughters for ye are sons & daughters begotten anew to a lively hope of eternal life even so Amen
