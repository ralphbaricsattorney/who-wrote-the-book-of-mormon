## heading: Section 15th
## date: Oct 1864

Thus saith the Lord to his handmaiden Sarah. When thou wast devoted to me by sacrifice. I the Lord dissolved all old covenants by which thou wast bound, that thou mightest be wholly mine, for I the Lord thy God art thy husband, to the exclusion of all others, that thou mightest do my will, and stand before me for thyself, that I the Lord might organise a quorum of female prophets for the benefit of Zion. Yea, I say more, for the salvation of Zion. It is my will saith the Lord that, that quorum, should be free from all earthly authorities, that I the Lord might be their ruler without men or the sons of men having any claim to them by virtue of any Gentile covenant. I the Lord say unto thee, that this quorum ceases to be self disposing, and shall do as I their husband and Redeemer direct. For it is chosen of me that it might be fulfilled which was typified in the visions of the prophets. My servant John saw in his great vision what he called a great wonder. A woman clothed upon with the sun, the moon under her feet, and a crown of 12 stars on her head. Thus signifying that all the authorities of Zion should give heed to her voice. The quorum of female prophets which I the Lord am prepareing was what the woman in the vision represented. John saw the woman that she brought into the world a man child who was caught up into heaven, and the woman went into the wilderness there for to continue for a certain time; this man child was to rule the world with a rod of iron, or the word of God. Verily saith the Lord this man child is Zion which is now prepareing for his rule in his strength, & as the man child is coming forth, the woman must come with him arrayed in her glory. I the Lord will make her to Zion what mount Gerizzim was to ancient Israel. For as the tribes of Israel went up to mount Gerizzim to get their blessings, so shall the priesthoods and the children of Zion go up to this queenly quorum to get theirs. For this prophetic quorum is appointed for the purpose of putting blessings upon the heads of the priesthoods and Children of Zion: and from them shall their blessings flow upon them, and I the Lord say that those whom they bless shall be blessed; for they shall bless and not curse: for it is not for them to curse, but to warn & to bless. And verily, verily saith the Lord there is no priesthood so high in Zion but what their warning voice shall reach it. Yea let all hear and give heed to the voice of their warning, from him saith the Lord whom I have chosen and ordained to be prophet, seer revelator and expounder through all the departments of the priesthood, lest evil come upon them. For when I the Lord send forth the voice of warning through them let Zion attend and bow with deference. And again I the Lord say unto my Queenly quorum when there is a warning voice put into your hearts, fail not to give it, lest satan take advantage of you
