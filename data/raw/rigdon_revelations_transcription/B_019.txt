And let my servant Israel appoint assistant Bishops and present them to the church or some branch of the Children of Zion, and should they approve of them, ordain them & let them take charge as he directs: And let Israel also know that he is a member of the first presidency and he can do nothing without their consent. Of necessity there have to be a great deal trusted to their judgment they will have to provide places to have the tithings preserved so that nothing be lost and in all this thing they will have to have judgment wisdom and prudence exercised.

If need be you will have a book one[?] to record the tithings, but all this will have to be a matter of judgment."

Now I the Lord say to you of myself in case you do not get tithings enough to supply at any time the needs of him who is at your head you must borrow; for his needs, must be supplied or Zion must fail. He bore Zion on his shoulders when the rest of you were doing nothing for it. And it was after he had got its principles brought to light, which he did alone, that any of you were notified of the fact. And now it was that I the Lord made the discovery that there were very wise men in the world. "There were of them who desired to have the principles through which Zion was brought to light changed and practices conformed to the customs of Babylon: for such had learned from "Babylon" all they knew on the subject; for I the Lord had never said one word to any of them. But the Devil was determined to get something of his introduced; and could he have gotten one thing introduced by one whom I the Lord had not inspired he would have let you seen what kind of a Zion you would have had. The eternal and ever memorable case of Eden would have been acted over again and he would have held his foothold as he has in the world, till Zion would have been laid in ruins.
