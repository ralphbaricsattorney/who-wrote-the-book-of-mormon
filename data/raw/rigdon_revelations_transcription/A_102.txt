## heading: Section 63d. On organization.
## date: Oct. 1871

Br. Post: Your letter came to hand last evening, the contents of which are satisfactory. By it I learn that you had received the letter calling on you to organize and prepare for what is hastening on the earth, as the days of those who professed to know the Lord but knew him not and blasphemed him in his own house are numbered and the judgments of God are about to fall on them at which time, according to the prophets, Zion was to begin to move forward. In that letter you ask me a number of questions. To your enquiry concerning myself the Lord claims the right of answering himself.

Behold saith the Lord your God concerning my servant Sidney. It is not my will that he should go there at present on his own account that he may escape persecution which will fall on him if he goes there at present certainly. When the blasphemers are hurled down to rise no more forever, if my servant sidney should be there the Devil would stir up a furious persecution on him, when all the elements of persecution are disturbed and excited, for the purpose of destroying him. For the Devil would be sure that if he were destroyed that would finish the work of redeeming Zion and there it would end. Therefore I the Lord your God have kept him, and must keep him away until the storm is passed. Again I say concerning him he can do all that is necessary to enable you to move the cause of Zion as at present, and be here and thus escape persecution.

And now I the Lord say let my servant Ebenezer Page be chosen for the presidency of the 12ve for he has proven himself before me. And also let my servant Robert Buhn be numbered with the 12ve that they may have power to have the Canada mission prosecuted, for I the Lord require of them that they see to it that the Canada mission fail not. Therefore let them counsel with my servant Stephen Post in all matters pertaining to their high and holy calling, that they may take the duties of their calling upon them as becometh the saints of the Most High, being discouraged at no power nor devices of the adversary, but in all things rejoicing before the Lord with thanksgiving that their sins are forgiven them, and they are redeemed from the condemnation which is resting upon the whole world except those who have been redeemed through their obedience to the word of the Lord.
