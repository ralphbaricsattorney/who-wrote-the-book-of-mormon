This the Lord shews us could not be done by merely gathering many people together for this had been done and proved a failure.

After all these abominations all could see wasted themselves, and only fitted the actors therein for the perdition of ungodly men, the few who had withstood the whole flood of hells wrath and fury, had instruction given how they should proceed to establish "Zion"; You will find the instructions given in the 98th sec. of D & C. & the 11th par. The counsel & instruction there given were designed for us, as certainly, as directly, as though we had been called by name. We make this application by authority to myself and all even as many as are about to engage in another attempt to redeem "Zion".

The Lord gives this because it is necessary, that the hearts of his people should be sanctified before him, as, that they should have his word. Both these will be accomplished by observing the rules that are given.

By no other means can the[?] "Zion" be raised from the dust and her light shine forth.

The Lord says concerning you that inasmuch as he has through the suffering, the ceaseless exertions, and undying faithfulness of another, (and I the Lord will here say that such was the purpose of his heart in redeeming "Zion" that nothing could change the purpose of his heart,) when all had turned away and the emmissaries of the Devil had robbed him of all he possessed, he by ceaseless prayer and supplication, his voice in connection with that of his companion was heard in the pavilion when none other voices came but theirs. These the Angels of the pavilion heard And thus I the Lord proved them before the Angels. and brought "Zion" to your doors. He requires you to bring the people he has sent there[?] to the knowledge of the gospel of Christ. yea he commands you to do so;
