After this all had taken place the Lord would send one of his servants to gather up the residue which would contain the strength of the Lords house, a something which would overthrow and not be overthrown There comes the strength of the Lords house.

All the tumblings[?] and corruptions of the old overthrown abominations left the strength of the Lords house where it was not moved.

But we are told that in order that the strength of the Lords house might come forth there would arise "another" servant not the old "Corrupters", but another servant and to him the requirement would be to go and gather up the remnant, "the strength of the Lords house." Till this time comes the strength of the Lords house will not come forth. Let what may be done, that will not be done. Now when this servant enters upon his work where will he do it,? by going back into the midst of old abominations,? or will his place of labour be in accordance with the work he is going to do.— Here then lays the faith and patience of the saints; to wait from one of these points to the other; that is, from the downfall of the old abomination, till the Lord gives order for the other servant to go and gather up the strength of his house. And blessed will they be who are found ready to arise at the voice of this commandment, & go forth to do the[?] Lord bidding & his work.

I have sent to you these great solemn & awful facts, for such indeed they are. It will be found when that day comes, that the time given was none too long for each one that will find acceptance to make themselves ready, after having devoted their entire time to the work.
