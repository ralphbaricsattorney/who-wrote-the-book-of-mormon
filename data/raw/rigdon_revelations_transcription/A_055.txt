## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## date: Addition June 23d

"We will here say a few things concerning our genealogy that has been made known to us since we sent the word of the Lord on the subject. Stephen the Martyr was a descendant of Samuel the prophet making br S. P." (Stephen Post) "of the same lineage with us, all of the Loins of Joseph of Egypt. thus it seems all the spokesmen the Lord appoints are of Joseph of Egypt from the days of Stephen the Martyr till the present time.

Our two sisters whom the Lord has placed in the priesthood are descendants from a sister of Mary's[?] the Mother of the Lord There were but two sisters in that family namely Sarah & Mary Sarah was the older of the two and it is in her that the geneology of our two sisters unite & from that back are the same".

## heading: Section 29th Object of book of Mormon.
## date: Sept 1867

"The Lords message of instruction to Zion.

Behold saith the Lord to his servant Sidney in order that the Children of Zion may have understanding in the things that pertain to their relation with me saith the Lord it is needful that they should comprehend those events which have transpired as necessary to get Zion into existence.

I the Lord commenced the work for the bringing of Zion out of the wilderness by bringing forth the fulness of the gospel. The book of Mormon in which is the fulness of the gospel, contained the manner of teaching by the Nephite people whose record it was. The object of bringing forth this book was to enable the priesthood of the days in which it should appear in the world to redeem my word from under the power of darkness and the dominion of Satan as necessary to the lifting up of Zion from out of the wilderness that the work of truth and righteousness might again commence to redeem the world from its lost condition. For as long as the word of truth which is the basis of salvation remained in darkness hid from the understanding of men, so long would the Devil hold dominion over the world and rule in its midst.
