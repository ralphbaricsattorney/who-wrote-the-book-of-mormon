There are places to be occupied by my servants Wm. Richards and Wm. Stanley the duties of which shall be made manifest hereafter. That of my servant Wm. Stanley, to be head of the agricultural, manufacturing & mechanical interests of Zion. And that of my servant Wm. Richards to have charge of all things pertaining to the service of bishops & their duties. Thus do I your Lord and law giver appoint each one his place in Zion. And now verily saith the Lord to you his servants, fear not little flock for I have placed you to rule over my Zion as directed by the spirit of holiness. Do as I the Lord your God council and direct, And I the Lord through you will send forth judgment unto victory, and not unto destruction. Even so Amen.

In the mean time it is the will of the Lord that you should all assist his servant in sending forth his appeal among the people.

I send you this as required by the spirit.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## heading: Section 4th Rev. to Abraham Burtis

"Thus saith the Lord unto his servant Abraham. "When a man is called & ordained to an office in Zion, I the Lord require of him that he should discharge the duty of that office faithfully: so that he may not fall under condemnation, and bring destruction on himself. You have been ordained to a high and holy office in Zion and you are bound by the law of that calling to arise and shake yourself from the dust of Babylon and labour for Zion with your whole heart might mind and strength: that Zion which is about to be called forth from the corruptions and abominations of Babylon, may awake as one out of sleep, and separate herself from the powers of wickedness which rule the earth, and stand before me the Lord your God as a separate people that I may lead her out of the midst of Babylon the Great that she may escape her sins, her plagues and her judgments: And that I the Lord may build up Zion in holiness before my face. This can only be done by the faithfulness and diligence of her priesthood before me saith the Lord. I have had my eye upon thee[?] from the time you were ordained to the high and holy calling, to stand in the highest priesthood known to Zion: but you knew it not.
