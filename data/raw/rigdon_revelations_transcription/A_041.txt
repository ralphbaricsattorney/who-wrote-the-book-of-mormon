Therefore let such arise from the corruption of Babylon, and stand before me the Lord their God, in holiness, in righteousness, & in truth. And let them be valiant for the truth, seeking wisdom & counsel from my word. And let them treat my word lightly no more saith the Lord that their salvation may be certain & their triumph sure.

And now saith the Lord the time has come for the Children of Zion to begin to understand their duty in their relations to one another. Zion is an organization of her own, and their relations to one another are in accordance with the great object to be accomplished through her - Zion is the order of release for all her children. And I the Lord alone will rule by such agencies as I in my wisdom may choose in her midst. Let no man therefore exercise authority over a woman, & let not a woman act unseemly to a man; but let the man treat the woman as a companion and the woman in like manner treat the man. If a man thinks he sees error in a woman, let him correct that error by admonition with prayer & supplication, and let his admonition be given in a meek and humble spirit & let the woman do likewise. Instead of Authority let there be prayer to obtain the right spirit by which admonition can be given to profit withall All the Children of Zion ought to pray continually & faint not, remembering the widow and the unjust judge, for they shall obtain if they faint not. Let each one both man and woman strive for peace that within the courts of Zion harmony and love may dwell and not confusion and disorder. If any are afflicted let them pray, and if any are merry let them sing Psalms praising the Lord, and not pollute their souls with the wild corrupting poetry of the Gentiles. I the Lord require of the Children of Zion that they should use industry, economy & prudence, that their habitation may be the dwelling place of the Holy spirit & that the labor of their hands may be blessed. Banish from Zion all evil speaking, purge out hypocrisy, speak truth one to another, and as much as in you lays live peaceably with all men, walking in the fear of the Lord giving none occasion to the adversary to speak reproachfully. Let all your conversation be according to Godliness and the peace & the good pleasure of the Lord will be with you: Cease not to study my word that you may be able at all times to give an answer to those who enquire of you the reason for the faith & hope which dwelleth in you. Spend no time in idleness & vanity as do the Gentiles, but devote all the time you have apart from your labors, to lay up in your hearts a treasure of knowledge & wisdom from the words of eternal life as written in my word.
