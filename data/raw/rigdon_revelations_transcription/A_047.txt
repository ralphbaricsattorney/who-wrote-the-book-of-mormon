He rejected the law of the priesthood and trampled under foot the authority of the Lord as taught by his grandfather Abraham, and also by his father Isaac. "Behold therefore saith the Lord let the Children of Zion understand that the mountain of Esau, the Gog & Magog of Ezekiel, Babylon the great the mother of harlots and the abominations of the earth; the false prophet of the revelations of John that was to be taken & cast alive into a lake of fire burning with brimstone, And the great and abominable church of the Nephites all relates to the same people, and that people are the nations and kingdoms of the earth which now rule the Gentile world saith the Lord your God. And know ye "Children of Zion" that when you read of the judgments and destructions that are awaiting them you are reading of what shall befal the Gentile nations & kingdoms that now rule the earth.

Again saith the Lord when those ancient prophets wrote; the history of the world lay before their minds in two periods. The first one was from the time they wrote till the coming of the book of Mormon, (for they knew of the coming of this book and prophecied of it. The second period was from the coming forth, of the book of Mormon till I their Redeemer came in my glory.

When they prophecied they spoke of both these periods at the same time and the same prophecy related to both. In their prophecies against the different nations or against individuals who lived in their day they knew that the same judgments would fall on the heads of the wicked and rebellious of the second period. Let the "Children of Zion" understand that when they read those prophecies it matters not whether the judgments were pronounced against Egypt or Tyre or Esau or Judah, Moab or Ammon, the same symbolized the judgments that should come on the despisers of the second period. Therefore know ye "Children of Zion" when ye read these judgments you are reading the judgments that shall come on the nations now living. The same holds good in the judgments written in the book of Mormon including the wars there recorded."
