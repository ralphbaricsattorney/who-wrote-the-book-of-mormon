Newton for his transgressions before me; hereafter I will hold you responsible for his conduct and his sins shall be answered on your head, for from henceforth it shall be unto him according to what you shall say." You cannot avoid seeing that this places us in most peculiar circumstances, we surely have sins enough to answer of our own without having his to answer for. Based upon this we here give you the law by which he must be governed without liberty to depart from it in the smallest degree. He shall not teach any thing publickly or in private to any person whatever, only the lessons which have been and will be given by the female priesthood. To the study of these lessons he shall devote himself until he has delivered himself from the fiends of hell that are leading him down, and that swiftly to the perdition of ungodly men. He shall not judge of any case which requires a spiritual decision until the spirit of the Lord shall get possession of his heart. He will cease to act as a member of the quorum of the first presidency. He can if his health permits go out and preach the gospel. He may join any of the missions now prepareing to go out if he chooses.

This law with regard to him You and Sister N. will see that it is carried out to the very letter, otherwise I will say to you as the Lord said to me his sins shall be answered on your head.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon, Phebe Rigdon

## heading: Section 53d
## date: Apr 22nd 1869

Verily saith the Lord to his servant Stephen: Let the branch of Zion at Attica be called together in obedience to this call, and if it is in accordance with their will let mine handmaiden Jane Post be ordained to the prophetic office so that the requirements of the Lord through his handmaiden Phebe may be carried into effect in Zion Amen
## signed: Sidney Rigdon, Phebe Rigdon

P.S. None but yourself (S. Post) can ordain to that office.
