The word of the Lord to his servant Stephen Post concerning things whereof he has inquired. I the Lord say unto my servant Stephen that the system of Poligamy as had among a people who were called after my name was not of me saith the Lord. I the Lord your Redeemer disavow it. I never gave to Joseph Smith nor any other man authority to introduce in my name that system as had among that people in any of its forms, as a pretended spiritual relation or otherwise, and you shall not suffer it to exist in Zion. Yea you shall not suffer it to make its appearance in Zion nor among her children in any of its forms, for in so doing you will bring it before my face. And it is before me saith the Lord an abomination. I forbid it among the Nephites, and as you are properly classed as of the Nephites I the Lord therefore forbid its appearance among you. Therefore if there come any to you who have been partakers in that abomination you shall require a baptism of them & it shall be between you and such a baptism of renunciation. They shall renounce that system to you before me; and their baptism shall be a pledge to you in my presence saith the Lord that in their hearts they have renounced the system. And should it appear afterwards by word or deed that they in their baptism had acted deceitfully you shall cause their names to be blotted from among the names of the Children of Zion for their abomination before me and their hypocrisy to you. But as I the Lord did to the Nephites reserve the right to dispose of my people as seemed me good so do I the same to you, I am God and I search the hearts and try the reins of my people and will require what I will & choose when I will do it, & of whom I will do it, and wo be to them should such refuse to obey me. I the Lord your God, am a jealous God, and my requirement shall be kept among the Children of Zion.

When I the Lord raise up to myself a holy priesthood I set them free to do my will, but let them be careful that they do it, lest my anger be kindled against them, and in my wrath I cut them off: For if any of them should use their freedom to introduce any thing amongst my people to gratify their lustful and carnal desires, as I have done to others I will do to you I will cut you off. But when you do as I the Lord require you shall be sustained though men & devils howl at you. In my own due time I will bring you off triumphant until the gainsayers shall be left without my word to waste their time in fruitless effort & either remain in a lifeless situation, or in mingling with Babylon not knowing what to do or where to go till judgments cut them off and then wo be to them.

As to those who have not polluted themselves with polygamy
