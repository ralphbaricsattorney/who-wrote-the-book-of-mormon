Lord having risen from the dead my disciples stole me away. So do all the church of the Devil Instead of believing that I the Lord brought forth the book of Mormon out of the ground where it was buried they profess to believe that my servant Sidney made it, and thus they are damned at his expense. They go to hell belying him.

And verily the Children of Zion do see that when any turn away from the truth they excuse themselves by preferring complaints against him. And so it is that salvation & damnation are with him. This peculiar situation belongs to him, and to him only on the earth. The condition of things with which he is thus surrounded was created because he was separated entirely from the church of the devil and became the head of the church of the Lamb of God. By reason of this fact & this only he has become the object of the Devils hatred, & he stirred up every soul over whom he has influence against him.

Furthermore that I the Lord may shew unto the Children of Zion the peculiar condition under which he exists, & has to exist The condition of things here described does not belong to him only individually considered but they attach to his children also. They from early life have had to share with him the persecution & violence hurled against him so that they have never known what it was to enjoy the Common privileges of life. The vengeance of the Devil was hurled at them even when of tender years. All this the devil through his emmissaries falsely and cruelly did. The Devil & his children here sought through these means to turn him away from the faith: but it belonged to him to sustain the faith or die in attempting to do so. And all the Children of Zion are witnesses of his triumph.

And now saith the Lord inasmuch as he and his family have been marked out as victims. And the Devil because of his regard for the cause of righteousness has subjected them to all manner of abuse far beyond others, it is for the Lord himself who knew all these things would transpire from the beginning, to manifest the things which were and are had in the eternal world in relation thereto Therefore let all the Children of Zion know assuredly and let them ponder it in their hearts the great mystery which I the Lord now reveal to them. Behold saith the Lord the principalities above established a law, and also entered into a covenant in due time to be fulfilled on the head of those who would be thus situated, and this covenant was shadowed forth to my servant John saith the Lord at the time of his great vision when he saw the "Man child" that the woman brought forth caught up to heaven.
