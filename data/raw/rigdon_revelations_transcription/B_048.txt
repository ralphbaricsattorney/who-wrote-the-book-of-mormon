## heading: Copy of the Letter accompanying the Rev appt a Bishp
## heading: Friendship July 23d 1868. Br. Post.

"As the spirit presses closely concerning the affairs of Zion we find it important that you should see I mean the whole presidency there that there are no idlers among the priesthoods. All who have been ordained must go into the field as much as is possible for them to do it. The presiding authorities must look well to this matter that every one is found faithful in his calling, and to this end each ordained authority should be called on to give to the presiding officer a regular report of their labours[?] in the vineyard at least once in every three months, And the presiding officer to whom such report is made, should forward the reports thus made to the presidency at Attica. So that it may be known by the presidency the state and condition of Zion generally and such extracts from these reports in a condensed form to be forwarded when considered necessary to us here. We want you to distinctly understand that all matters & things which are forwarded for our consideration and action must reach us in that way, for it is out of the question that there can be an open door for every one that chooses to worry us with their notions and opinions, & interminable questions when they choose. Each one making his report shall report to the presiding officer over the quorum to which he belongs, and that presiding officer will make the report to Attica. Through this way it will be known the faithful and spiritual laborer from the carnal and negligent; For the spiritual and faithful laborer will return bringing his sheaves with him. Thus let Zion begin to move onward. Another important thing is, that all ordained authorities must be organized under a presiding officer. There can be no teachers belonging to Zion wandering about on their own authority. all must have a quorum to which they belong and a presiding officer to whom they must make their reports. To this end there must be chosen an elder to preside over the quorum of elders, for all those elders who do not belong to the qorum of 70s. under whom they must be directed, and they must approve themselves to the quorum to which they belong. Each and all of these presiding officers must look well to their respective quorums over which they preside & must be themselves an ensample to them in all righteousness. And they must study the word of of the Lord and the law of their calling so as to be able to instruct their quorum in all things pertaining to their duty, and they must see that there is no idleness with the members of their respective quorums You will find the law of the organization in the 3d section of the D&C. Read, study, and understand that all things may be done according to the will of the Lord.

I have received the following from the Lord. "Behold saith the Lord" &c
