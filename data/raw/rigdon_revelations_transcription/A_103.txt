continued study in perfecting his acquaintance with the word of the Lord so that he can shew himself a workman that need not be ashamed being able to give to all their portion of meet in due season, for it is according to my purpose saith the Lord to exalt him on high, and to make him mighty in word and in deed, to hurl the powers of darkness at his feet and triumph over them.

To mine handmaiden Jane saith the Lord, whom I love, the redeemed above are rejoicing over her at her perseverance in searching after the wisdom of heaven that she may fulfil the whole law of her priesthood. Fear not mine handmaiden your work of faith and labour of love in the Lord cannot fail of bringing with them the reward of eternal glory. Therefore seek wisdom, work righteousness, and walk humbly before your God, and the reward of eternal glory is yours. But what shall I say of and to mine handmaiden Phebe, the light of the world, the Lords lamp in Zion. The first born daughter of the holy priesthood who alone first taught the knowledge of the true righteousness of Zion. The saved of the nations and the redeemed Israel will have her name in remembrance forever with honors on her head. There is no glory in heaven too great for her to possess. There will be no song of triumph among the redeemed but what will have her name and deeds of righteousness remembered in it. Through her mission the Lord will raise her female followers to the true dignity of their character and to the highest felicities of Heaven with her. She is the first born of woman there were none like her before her day, and there will be none after equal to her. she can bear honour without pride, and indignity without shame or mortification, knowing in whom she has believed, and can trust the keeping of her redemption in his hand till the day of her deliverance comes. It is thus that the Lord your God looks upon his handmaiden Phebe. Amen.

I the Lord speak to all the Children of Zion calling none such but those who learn righteousness through the instruction imparted through mine handmaiden Phebe. I the Lord say to all the Children of Zion that it matters not what has been taught in other periods and under other dispensations as duties transfer them into Zion as it now exists, and they will fail to establish the righteousness of the Children of Zion. For Zion is based on the book of Mormon and the righteousness therein revealed is what constitutes the righteousness of the Children of Zion.

And now behold saith the God of Zion I the Lord have not inspired any other than she who stands at the head of the female priesthood to bring forth the righteousness of that book, and all who will be saved have to learn the law of my kingdom from the instruction which this mine handmaiden sends
