The voice of the bride and of the bridegroom shall be heard in their land no more at all. The sound of the grinding shall be heard no more, neither shall the voice of him who ploweth, nor of him who soweth, nor of him who gathereth into barns: for famine sword and pestilence shall waste and destroy till there be nothing left. Their houses that are left shall be dwellings for the fowls of heaven, whither they shall congregate and build nests and raise their young. There shall the beasts of the forest make their lair, & the serpents shall lay in their bed chambers. I the Lord have decreed it and none can change my purpose. For I the Lord who sought to save them & they would have none of my counsel nor listen to my reproofs, will laugh at their calamity and mock when their fear cometh. Neither shall any who are found in their midst escape the destruction but shall perish with them.

Therefore I the Lord your God say unto you prepare your way that you may escape both the sins and the plagues which shall make this eastern land a howling wilderness. Warn your brethren and as one who whispereth in their ears let them hear the voice of their Redeemer, saying, go ye out of Babylon. Again I the Lord say unto you that it is only in the west where your lives or that of any others can be saved: for the decree of utter destruction which has been decreed by the priesthoods on high as far as this country is concerned is bounded as has been said.

Therefore let my servants Joseph H. Newton, Wm. Stanley, Stephen Post, Abraham Burtis & Samuel Reeves see that the requirements herein made, be attended to faithfully and finish the work in the east which has been required as speedily as possible that all things may be done as required. And it is the will of him who thus sheweth mercy to you that you my servants should prepare the way of him through whom you receive this voice of escape that he also may be in your midst, for on him you are dependant for your deliverance as I the Lord have made him dependant on you so that you can be equal sharers in each others benefits. And I would further say unto you my servants that it will be to your advantage when you leave to seek a home that you go as far west as the place known as Council Bluffs in Iowa. I the Lord say unto you that this is a pearl which you must not cast to swine lest they turn and rend you. Amen. I send you this as the spirit has required the fulfilling of it belongs to you.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon
