Joseph whose surname is Newton, and my servant Stephen whose surname is Post, when the powers of darkness had thrown their shades of gloom over the people that were called after my name and all were in confusion and they had cast my word behind their back and transgressed my laws and were following after the imaginations of their own hearts, seeking to make prophets and leaders for themselves and groping like blind men for the wall all in darkness and confusion. They were walking in light having taken my word for their guide and counsellor walking before me according to my commandment and waiting for the Salvation of Zion and when they were called for they arose and trimmed their lamps for they had oil in their vessels with their lamps and they went forth to prepare the way for the coming of the bridegroom. My fear saith the Lord which dwelt in their hearts, and their faith in my word withstood the powers of darkness that they could not lead them astray. And this is the blessing wherewith I the Lord their God bless them. The covenant of my peace is with them, and the words of life and Salvation shall go forth through them, and all that are redeemed with Zion, will have to receive the word of truth through their ministration, for I the Lord have deposited it with none others. Inasmuch as they walked in light when all the world were in darkness: Their names are written in heaven on tables of light, And they are known in the church of the first born in heaven, as the sons of light, and the powers and principalities above are waiting till their work is done on earth, and the day of Awards come, to crown them chiefs among the Redeemed to lead forth of the host of the glorified in the realms of eternal duration.

Behold saith the Lord, that the priesthood which I have placed at the head of Zion are before me day and night with their hearts overflowing with mercy and forgiveness for those who trespassed against them evily entreated them & reviled them in the days of their greatest tribulation & humiliation, crying Father, Father forgive them for they knew not what they did. And though justice and judgment are the habitation of my throne, and righteousness and truth go before my face yet mercy and forgiveness are with me. And shall it be said that a priesthood which I the Lord endued and qualified myself are more merciful and forgiving than the God who raised them up. Verily this can never be said in the courts of Zion, Therefore verily verily, saith the Lord to all those who receive the word of their salvation at the hand of my servants whom I have chosen that it shall as far as the record of heaven is concerned be the beginning of life to them.
