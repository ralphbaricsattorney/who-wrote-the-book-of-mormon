that you should devote all your energies in studying my word which I caused to come forth through my servant Joseph Smith even the book of Mormon & Doctrine & covenants that you may be qualified to assist to do the great work which is about to come forth, the marvellous work of fulfilling the covenant made with the house of Israel. You are called to assist in the work which is now commenced of prepareing[?] the way to fulfil the covenant made with the Fathers of Israel, which work consists in calling upon those who profess faith in the book of Mormon & in the book of doctrine & covenants to cease to treat lightly the things which I the Lord have caused to be written for their salvation; But to remember them and to say and to do according to that which is written.

I the Lord your God chose, from the beginning yea while Israel was a child & before he was a nation, two priesthoods through[?] I who speak unto you even your Redeemer intended to lead the tribes of Jacob not only to final deliverance but to endless glory. A knowledge of these two priesthoods you have received through the book of Mormon & doctrine & covenants.

Two, Moses and his spokesman were to lead, and lead Israel out of bondage. But I the Lord knew that they would rebel against my requirements as given by Moses, unto their overthrow, Appointed unto them another priesthood, namely, Joseph and his spokesman through whom I their Redeemer would gather them to triumph, glory & everlasting rest. Israel did rebel and in consequence were scattered as you see at this day. And the second priesthood is now called upon to lead that people to the promised deliverance. The entire salvation of Israel depended on the work of these two priesthoods. For I the Lord never appointed but the two, and these appointments were both made known to the children of Jacob at the same time by Joseph who was sold into Egypt as you read in the book of Mormon but as in the case of the first so it has happened to the second. When Moses appointed his spokesman, Cora, Dathan & Abiram rebelled against him & many others with them, but they all perished in their gainsayings. So it has happened to the second. When Joseph appointed his spokesman, Brigham James & Joseph have rebelled as Cora Dathan & Abiram did in the wilderness against the appointment through Moses. James, whose sir name was Strang has also perished in his gainsayings and as I live saith the Lord the others shall perish in the gainsaying of Cora as I have said by mine apostle Jude unless they repent. For I the Lord will not suffer my word to fail neither shall men trample it under foot.
