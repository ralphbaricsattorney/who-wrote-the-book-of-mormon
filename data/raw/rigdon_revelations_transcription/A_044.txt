And so minutely did he mark the expression of countenance of each and their peculiar features that he will know them when he sees them in the eternal world. And after he had heard and seen all these things, and understood how the kingdom of heaven should be established by which Israel should be gathered and the covenants made with the fathers fulfilled he wrote a part of the vision and you have it with you.

And now behold & lo! saith the Lord the Redeemer of Israel, he who conversed with Obadiah at the time of his great vision, let all the Children of Zion that now live, or shall ever live hereafter know that the vision which Obadiah saw concerning the Saviors coming up on Mt Zion is fulfilled. For verily verily saith the Lord that those who stood steadfast holding the truth in righteousness before me when Satan had triumphed over the people that were called by my name are the same persons seen by Obadiah in his great vision, and they are they whom he will know in the eternal world, namely my servant Sidney Rigdon and mine handmaiden Phebe Rigdon, My servant Joseph H. Newton & mine handmaiden Sarah Newton and my servant Stephen Post, they were they who stood immovable in the great day of trial each one for himself and herself neither having influence over the others waiting individually for the salvation of Zion.

When the powers of darkness raged & the waves of corruption lashed in fury, laying waste the order of the kingdom of heaven & hurling it into fragments, they were with my word in their hands seeking & searching after what I the Lord had caused to be written, while all others had cast my word behind their back and were running after the imaginations of their own hearts. I the Lord saw them each one alone holding fast to the words of eternal life, and by these means were able to judge the mountain of Esau (as the Jews called it) or the Babylon of corruption & darkness. They had to judge all things pertaining to the kingdom of heaven, not only the mountain of Esau but they had to try those who said they were apostles and prophets but were not, and found them liars. Satan put forth all his powers, he left nothing undone that he could do. All his devices artifices & subtilities were used by him to overthrow Zion forever.
