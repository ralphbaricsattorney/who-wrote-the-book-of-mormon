## heading: Section 59th
## date: July 1865

I the Lord your God say unto my servants in Attica. that my servant has sinned grievously before me and stands under condemnation in my sight, because he has broken his pledge of faith before me. He bound himself before the heavens by his prayers that he would remember the new covenant the Book of Mormon and the commandments which I the Lord his Redeemer had given, not only to say but to do according to that which was written, and instead thereof he took strength & power to himself and attempted to do that which I the Lord had forbidden him to do; in that he proposed to appoint a president over the Children of Zion & by so doing sought to reject him whom I the Lord had chosen to preside over Zion: & had he succeeded and the Children of Zion had done as he said Satan would through him have overthrown the Children of Zion. And they would have been driven out of the place that I the Lord had chosen for them, and where I had placed them; for if those who had gone before you were not spared, when they did not as I the Lord commanded them, should you expect to escape when you do the same things; the more especially when you had them for an ensample before your eyes. This can not be done unless the Lord is a respecter of persons. Therefore let my servant Stephen repent before me of this great wickedness, for it is against me his Lord he has sinned putting me to shame in giving him power and influence among the Children of Zion by appointing him to the office he now holds, and instead of his doing as he was commanded used his office and influence for the entire overthrow of Zion.
