## heading: Section 12th.
## date: Oct 5th 1864

Br. Newton: I received yours of the 30th of Sept. I answer by return mail. The revelation the Lord gave me without my seeking for it. It came to me as to Jeremiah. Indeed the appt's. of you and Br. Post were made without my seeking for them on my part. I always supposed the Lord would appoint two presidencies to fill the quorum of the first presidency. but not feeling myself capable of judging of any mans faith nor abilities, I waited till the Lord designated them himself. This he has now done and with that designation pointed out to each their duties * and I see he has put you both under the same law under which he placed myself. He required of me to accomplish certain objects before him and that I must do for the Lord would accept no excuse at my hand.

It did appear to me that Satan surrounded me with every opposing circumstance that satanic ingenuity could invent, still the duty I must perform or as far as I was concerned all was lost. I had to travel a road where there were none to direct I had to do it alone. Many times I got strength from what is said in the book of Mormon about Nephi's getting the plates, & building the ship, and my heart would say keep the commandments of God, & triumph gloriously. I did so solitary and alone in the midst of poverty & necessity year after year for 16 years, and the result of this gloomy conflict is to be the Salvation of a world.
