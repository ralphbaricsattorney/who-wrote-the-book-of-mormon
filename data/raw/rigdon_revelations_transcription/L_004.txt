The Lord gives this because it is necessary that the hearts of his people should be sanctified before him as that they should have his word. Both these will be accomplished by observing the rules that are given. By no other means can "Zion" be raised from the dust and her light to[?] shine forth

The Lord says concerning you that in as much as he has through the suffering the ceaseless exertions and undying faithfulness of an other; and the Lord will here say that such was the purpose of his heart in redeeming "Zion" that nothing could change the purpose of his heart when all had turned away and the emmissaries[?] of the Devil had robbed him of all he possessed he by ceaseless prayer and supplication his voice in connection with that of his companion was heard in the pavillion when none others came but theirs, This the angels of the pavillion heard and thus I the Lord proved them before the angels, &

brought "Zion" to your doors he requires you to bring the people[?] he has sent there to the knowledge of the gospel of Christ yea he commands you to do so he will take nothing less at your hand; if you fail you fail to your condemnation, for I the God of "Zion" will take your priesthoods from you and cast you down to rise no more Therefore see to it that you fail not but continue to pray and to intercede before the Lord as the poor widow before the unjust Judge till you do prevail (See Luke 18c[?] 1— 8 v[?]) Let all the daughters of "Zion" also have their solemn assemblies of prayer before the Lord that while the priesthoods are in the feild of labor teaching and proclaiming, the sisters may be sending up their prayers and supplications into the pavillion for the success of those who are in the field of labor

Thus let all engage and cease not untill the people I the Lord have sent to you will bow to the word of eternal life.
