Smith had entered into you was going to prevail with God and man. You knew you lied when you made that pretence. You ignorance was such that you did not know that there were those living who knew that there never was, is, nor will be, such a metempsychosis on this earth as you wickedly and heaven daringly pretended had taken place with you. Those in former times who, assisted by the Devil, were attempting to establish idolatry made such pretentions to accomplish that object; but the revelations of heaven stamped it with its true character as a hellish lie. But sir the day of your reconning[?] is at hand and the hour of your judgment is fast closing on you, when you will learn that God is not to be mocked, neither will he suffer his word to fail. He called a priesthood to gather Israel but it was not you. You will learn this if never before, when you open your eyes in hell being in torment. A period which is perhaps nearer your door than perhaps you are apprised of. Know then that your wicked corrupt and senseless career is shortly to close with the entire overthrow of you and all that you have or are. Amen.

## heading: Section 62nd. On solemn assembly.
## date: Oct 1/71

Br Post. We were in hopes of receiving a letter from you before we wrote this but in this we are disappointed and as necessity is laid upon us to write we this Sunday morning commence it.

According, as we are notified, we say to you and through you to all who love the truth and particularly to the members of the first presidency that it is the mind of the Lord, and his will most expressively expressed, that all the sons and daughters of the Lord — for the Lord acknowledges all who believe and love the truth as his sons and daughters; & the time has come when the Lord calls upon woman not as subjected to the rule and authority of man; but as standing independently for herself before God, to hear and do his will — should arise and trim their lamps and see that they have oil in their vessels with their lamps, for behold the day is near at hand when they shall be called upon yea all of them to forsake all other things dissolve all Gentile connection, & appear before the Lord stripped for the race and harnessed for the battle; for great and awful is the conflict which is coming; for behold the Lord is drawing his sword for blood and none but those who are sanctified shall be able to stand, and all among you who obey not shall perish.

The time has now come when the Lord is prepareing[?] to fulfil the word he had caused to be written in an early day in the work of the last days, when he would enter into judgment with the world, beginnining[?] at his house with those who professed to know him but knew him
