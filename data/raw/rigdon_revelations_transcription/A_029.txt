## date: Dec 1864

"Behold saith the Lord Satan is trying to overthrow the Children of Zion by my word through you.

I the Lord have gotten for the place of my habitation a goodly land yea a delightsome place and my servant Stephen has gotten possession of it. But Zion is not there, neither can it ever be there till my holy priesthood to which I have given Zion is taken there and established according to what I have said. The Children of Zion must take it there & raise it up out of all its cares and afflictions, or else it will be vain for them to go there. They may have houses and lands and live on; but Zion will not be in their midst. For I the Lord have made my holy priesthood to the Children of Zion, what the ark of the covenant was to the children of Israel, and their salvation depends on having it with them Therefore I the Lord say let the Children of Zion understand that unless they do as I the Lord their God require Zion can never be there, for in this thing will I the Lord their God prove them and they herein show to the Heavens what powers rule their hearts, whether it is my word or something else, for if my word saith the Lord do not rule in their hearts, no land to which they can go will save them,— Zion is not there saith the Lord God of Zion, but in the heart of my holy priesthood. The Children of Zion may say, let us get out of Babylon, but going to the goodly land which I the Lord have chosen will not put them out of Babylon. It is taking my holy priesthood saith the Lord and establishing it there, that will make Zion of it. Do that and then the Children of Zion can say, and that truly, let us go out of Babylon. For when they gather round my holy priesthood they will be in Zion, and not till then, and if this is not done then I the Lord will keep Zion to myself and in my own due time do as was said by the prophet Joseph. It shall go and gather up the residue, and Zion shall there flourish but not to the salvation of the children of Zion on whom I the Lord now call."
