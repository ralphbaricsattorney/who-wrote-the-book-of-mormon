I the Lord say in organizing the Zion of God that there can none be received only such as believe and will put into practice with faithfulness and diligence the duties here required, & all those who declare that their faith is founded on these facts and manifest a desire to put them into practice must be received when they apply for admission.

There need then be no darkness on your minds in bringing the kingdom of heaven into order and teaching the things which pertain to salvation. It is the design of the Lord your God to so present the house of his kingdom that no confusion can dwell in it putting it into your power in all cases to have an appeal unto the books through the right use of which all contention must cease.

Let my servant Israel consider duly the relation he bears to this second administration under the ruling of the book of Mormon of which mention has been made (see 85th. Sec. 3d. par.) for all must see the fate of the first for behold destruction and desolation layeth them waste, and unless those of the second administration take heed to what they receive they will also perish. Therefore let my servant Israel who is troubled with much serving arise & come to the assistance of his brethren in bringing into existence this second administration, and if he fulfils the duties of his high and holy priesthood he will press into the service of his God who has called him with a ready mind and a willing heart before the Lord his God, calling on the Lord day and night for the spirit of wisdom to aid him that he fail not.

Let him be found at his post as one of the presidents of this the second adminisistration[?], teaching and admonishing in all meekness and lowliness of heart with all firmness of purpose that is not to be changed, that he may secure unto himself the reward of his holy priesthood, for if he do not these things he like those who have gone before him will fail. To the Children of Zion I the Lord say concerning my servant Sidney making allowances for the weakness of man in the flesh which belongs to all. I have found him perfect before me; faithful in the discharge of his duties or of the duties which belong to the Holy order of the Universal priesthood, to which order he was ordained. In his heart I the Lord have seen neither Guile nor deception. In this respect he has been surpassed by none who ever stood before me. He received the book of Mormon when I sent it to him in the honest integrity of his heart, and he has sustained himself before me in that character to this day. had it not have been so he would not have been in the flesh
