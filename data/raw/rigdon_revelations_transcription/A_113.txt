he who offends in one point is guilty of the whole (Jas. 2: 10th) I the Lord warn you my servant Jesse to beware of transgressing any part of the law of your holy calling otherwise you will fall as your predecessor has done. Behold saith the Lord because punishment does not follow immediately after transgression, transgressors seem to think it is a light thing to set at naught the law of their holy calling: But remember and stand in fear for lo! the judgment of God is prepareing for such when they shall find themselves in the blackness of darkness, where, "There is weeping and wailing & gnashing of teeth" I the Lord say unto all those who receive the holy priesthood that if they fail to maintain it in purity, their doom is to be cast into hell from whence there is no return; But there remains for all such a fearful looking for of judgment and fiery indignation.

Behold I the Lord say unto you that Zion is founded & is before my face. The word which was written from days of old and brought to light in these last times, and those words which were had amongst men from early times have been made to run together and become one in the hand of the holy priesthood which I the Lord had elected for this purpose before the world was & made him manifest in the last times. Before he was made manifest I sent my word in all the civelized world as is named among you, and called upon all to hear and obey my voice. Thousands pretended to hear and they ran together. But now be astonished O ye heavens! And lament O earth! for out of the many thousands who came together there was found one and only one who took my word for his guide; and while the others yea all of them were falling into transgression and prostituting the power which they had received through my word to worldly purposes and the gratification of sensual desires he alone was seen before my face with my word in his hand, with much prayer and unceasing struggles that he might understand my word, and be able to solve all the mysteries of the divine scheme of eternal salvation. Satan seeing the progress he was making in bringing into[?] subjection those who had professed to walk in righteousness before me, the Lord, And seeing my servant bent on understanding the deep things of the kingdom of heaven, and knowing if S Rigdon my servant succeeded in obtaining the knowledge after which he was seeking that though he could overthrow all the rest, that through my servant Zion would be raised out of the dust and overthrow his kingdom sought with all the fury of his nature to destroy
