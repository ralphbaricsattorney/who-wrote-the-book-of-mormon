mount Gerizzim was to ancient Israel, for as the tribes of Israel went up to mount gerizzim to[?] get their blessings, so shall the priesthoods and the children of Zion go up to this queenly quorum to get theirs. For this prophetic quorum is appointed for the purpose of putting blessings upon the heads of the priesthoods and Children of Zion and from them shall their blessings flow upon them and I the Lord say that those whom they bless shall be blessed; for they shall bless and not curse, it is not for them to curse, but to warn and to bless. And verily verily saith the Lord there is no priesthood so high in Zion but what their warning voice shall reach it Yea let all hear and give heed to the voice of their warning from him saith the Lord whom I have chosen and ordained to be prophet, seer, revelator and expounder through all the departments of the priesthood, lest evil come upon them For when I the Lord send forth the voice of warning through them let Zion attend and bow with deference. And again I the Lord say unto my queenly quorum, when there is a warning voice put into your hearts, fail not to give it, lest satan take advantage of your fear and negligence, and you fall into trouble. In all ages of the world where I the Lord ruled, profound respect and obedience was rendered to mothers, so let all Zion render the same respect to the voice of the matronly authority. Thus saith the Lord I have given to the priesthoods and children of Zion A help mate, as was said to man at the beginning behold saith the Lord this order is in accordance with the faith and prayers of your father Adam before me, for he has the same respect for his daughters that he has for his sons, and it was his prayer that when the celestial organization of the Ancient of days was established, that his daughters should have a voice in that council. and I the Lord have appointed this prophetic quorum in obedience to his will to form part of the celestial Council, and their voice must be heard in it. And verily, verily, saith the Lord, let all the daughters of Zion lift up their heads and shout for joy and render praise and thanksgiving to the most high, for the daughters of heaven lift up their voices in praise and thanksgiving when they see this Celestial order coming forth. Amen."

Therefore I the Lord appoint you and ordain you to stand with them in the holy priesthood to be Ministers to Zion in the sacred things, which I the Lord reveal to them.
