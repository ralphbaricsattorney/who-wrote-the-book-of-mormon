## heading: Section 3d. Friendship N Y.

The word of the Lord concerning Zion. Thus saith the Lord to his servant Joseph H Newton. I have looked upon thee and heard thy prayers and being well pleased with thy faith and steadfastness in the truth for such the Lord loves. I the Lord make known my will to those whom I love, that they may abound in the works of righteousness, and be established in the truth to the salvation of their souls. I, the Lord, have sifted Zion as with a tempest, and have fanned her as by a whirlwind; that I might bring to light those in whose hearts dwelt the spirit of the prophets & the strength of the priesthood: for Zion cannot come forth in deed and in truth, and in power only as her rulers abound in the truth, and are filled with the light of the Lord, that they may be able to stand against all the wiles of the adversary and overcome the powers of darkness, being filled with the knowledge of God, so that neither height nor depths, principalities nor powers, things present nor things to come. Angels nor any other creature can separate them from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus. — During the time of darkness, when Zion was tossed upon the ocean of confusion, and Satan ruled and triumphed in the midst of her people, hundreds, yea, thousands fell from their steadfastness before me, and are now laying under condemnation in my sight: But I the Lord out of the midst of darkness, have brought forth five men, in whose hearts I found dwelling the spirit of the prophets who had power to lay hold on the words and testimony of the prophets, and thereby deliver themselves from under the otherwise triumphant fury of the mighty rush of the powers of Satan against the infant Zion, of the Lord your God. — Verily, verily saith the Lord your Redeemer, had you dwelt in Sodom at the time of her destruction, for your sake she would have been saved, and by the reason of your faith it is that this nation with all her people has not been devoted to entire destruction and their name and place perished before me forever. I found in her five righteous but in Sodom I did not. Therefore I have ordained that through you I will revive my Zion in her midst. — I the Lord do not say this to flatter you for I know your weakness and your sins in my sight; but that I your Lord may place you in a position to do my work; because I have found in you the spirit of the prophets which spirit you have inherited from your fathers: for you are the seed of the prophets and the lineal descendants of Abraham, and in bestowing on you the priesthood that shall rule Zion I fulfil the covenant made with Abraham that in him and his seed all the families of the earth
