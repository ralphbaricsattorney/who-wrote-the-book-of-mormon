I write now to the Lords spokesman confirmed to me as such through the recording angel in connection with the highest and holiest council known to the powers and principalities above, in whose hands is the "Lambs book of life."

In the name of all these powers I call on you to commence the work of spokesman in earnest, for until this time your soul has been straightened by reason of Gentile morality which has tightened your soul up; but now the principalities and powers above will require most positively that these things depart from you forever.

Now hereafter when your soul gets girt with some supposed principle of morality, Pause to inquire from whence you got it, from the, or through the grand High Council above or some fragment of Gentileism, which you have obtained from Old Babylon.

Let me warn you against that most arch and subtle abomination, the devil has wound into the old abomination of Polygamy & Monogamy. The basis that the devil has got for a contention. Satan has got them to believe that all that is necessary for a church, to build one you must either deny polygamy or profess to believe it. "That is all." *

## signed: Sidney Rigdon, P Rigdon

P.S. Read till you see clearly all the things alluded to in the communication here sent. You will find that Israel is to be gathered by power, and that necessary to that end all the languages of the world are to be spoken. But all this power comes by taking of Zion away from the old abomination."
