In this high and grand council of the court of Heaven were chosen the most powerful angel in the whole court, one to be the recording angel, and attend to the records of that court. There were also assistants appointed to help carry into effect the great work pertaining to this great council of heaven. The work of the mighty council thus organized in the dominions and in the presence of Jehovah was to keep the records of the most high in his court above, and to see that a correct history and full one, was kept of the world that was coming into existence through death. — The great council was organized before the world existed which was to be the theatre of its operations. — The world that was coming into being was one entirely of disembodied spirits that had been separated from their "bodies" through death. For the purpose of keeping a history of the spirit world through the whole period of its course, was the organized order in connection with the recording angel arranged. Through this organization there was an order of prophets who foretold the history of all the events that would transpire during the time of the worlds continuance. So as it was with the world in the flesh it might be in the spirit world, or world of spirits, a prophetic history of it given.

If any desire to see what is the order of heaven as established in the spirit world let such a one closely study the government as is found established in the world of those in the flesh, and there will be seen the order of things had among the spirits. So the two worlds are moving and will continue to move till they both cease to exist and give place to another order. As the world in the flesh has regulations through which they are associated, and combined into compacts under certain rules &c. So it is in the world of spirits, they are associated under certain regulations and governed by certain laws which are adapted to their nature as disembodied spirits. The source of all authority with them is the order established under the council pertaining to the recording angel. This recording Angel is one that never passed through the flesh. He is the nearest to God of any angel in the whole realms of heaven.

As men pass through the flesh there are certain ones who become identified with this eternal council.
