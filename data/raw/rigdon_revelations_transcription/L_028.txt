## note: a neat, even, rounded cursive hand closely resembling the copyist's hand of the bound volumes; ruled sheet with no ruled left margin and no margin labels (only a small mark of three short strokes at the left edge beside the line beginning "Adam"); the heading line at the top is a docket in smaller, lighter writing
## heading: Rev Con of Oct 1864

It is saith the Lord your Redeemer the order of Zion It is through this order that cometh the salvation of Zion and of the Gentiles as many as will believe, and of the house of Joseph or the Lamanites and lo and behold of the whole house of Israel, yea, of every son and daughter of Adam that will bow to the requirements of heaven. Therefore the first becomes last also. And again I the Lord say unto you that this is the order of things through which the hearts of the fathers are turned to the children and the hearts of the children are turned to the fathers, and the whole earth saved from being smitten with a curse, for behold through this order, all the covenants which I the Lord made with the fathers, and also the promises given to them, can be fulfilled; from your father Adam down to Jacob the son of Isaac who was the son of Abraham. And also all the covenants made with, and promises given to Lehi and Nephi and their seed can by this order be fulfilled. Hence the hearts of the fathers by it, yea, all of them, are turned to the children; therefore need I the Lord tell you that the heavens rejoice over you surely not, for had you not come forth and bowed to the requirements of the Most high, and held on to the principles of righteousness until you had obtained the holy priesthood of the restitution, the hearts of the fathers could not have been turned to the children because the covenants made with them could not be fulfilled. Therefore they would have to have seen the whole earth smitten with a curse, but through you they see that all can be fulfilled and therefore they rejoice over you.
