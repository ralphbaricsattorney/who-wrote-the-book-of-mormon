## heading: Section 4th 82
## date: 1873 Nov 3d

A letter by wisdom: Br. Post:

I write this letter by reason of one received from Br. Kisby[?] he is anxious to know some things about the removal. I want you to take the books and give them another reading, in view of putting yourself in possession so as to be able to satisfy all who may seek information on the subject.

To me it is a matter of great satisfaction to know that after upwards of 40 years struggling to reach the gathering of Israel the Lord has at last began to direct in relation to it directly.

Had we not have reached this point our labor would have been in vain and we ourselves cut off.

The whole work of bringing forth the book of Mormon as well as all the rest of the work, was in view of the gathering of Israel. In order to qualify ourselves for this the greatest and last of all the Lords work, we were called, and the writer of this spent upwards of 40 years before the Lord, regarding not the follies of men, neither their notions nor opinions, their love nor their hatred, their good will nor their condemnation: but untiring and unceasing he continued before the Lord, till the Lord has notified him to begin to prepare for the gathering of Israel, at an age like that of Moses 80 years; a something we had expected from the beginning but the day begins to dawn.

You are the spokesman acknoweledged[?] so by the high council of the court of heaven. This is the first case of the speaking of the grand council of the living God and it was you whose name was called in question. The first thing I heard from a strange voice which was not that of the Lord, for the manner of communication was different much nearer an audible voice, and the speaker spoke excellent English, I paused — "Who is it;" the answer was the recording angel. Thus came your commission in the order of gathering Israel.

Now follows you[?] work, see to it that you fail not. It is for you to bring to light from the revelations
