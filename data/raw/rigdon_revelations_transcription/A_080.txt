There have been feelings in the heart of that quorum to know which of its members are greatest in consequence there have been misgivings, heart burnings and discordant feelings all of which are violations of the law of unity & gross transgressions of the law under which the quorum was organized. That law required that what one said all should say, what one believed all should believe, what one taught all should teach. The quorum was required to inspire each other with confidence so that your yea might be yea and your nay, nay & what was different from that was of evil. I the Lord say unto you that mine eyes do not see that union neither do mine ears hear it. Therefore until that is the case sin and not righteousness dwells with you And you never can build the Zion of your God. For without that harmony the spirit of holiness without which Zion is nothing never can pass through you to the Children of Zion. The consequence is that whatever you build that thing will not be Zion. The matter which devolves upon you is to restore that confidence and that unity otherwise your work is at an end saith the Lord your God.

Behold saith the Lord I cannot look on the condition in which I the Lord have to see you with any allowance because you were placed there with a law as plain and as explicit as it could be made so that you need not err and your error is charged against you as gross & heaven daring iniquity, not because you had not the light, but because you treated lightly the knowledge the Lord your God had given you and ran greedily after the corrupt desires of your own hearts. For crimes thus committed there can be no allowance because you knew better and if you did not it was your sin and not your excuse.

Therefore saith the Lord go back and do your first work over again lest the Lord your God come and take your candlestick out of its place and you be left to howl and bewail your condition in darkness & dismay.

Return then saith the Lord and that speedily too and restore the confidence in each other you have lost. Let this be your business until that required confidence be perfected. Let your solemn meeting be observed the last day of every month, and let each search their heart by the spirit that is in such an one according to the power of that spirit. and the power to do that is perfect and let nothing be withheld from each other, let the revelation be perfect & thus restore the confidence and keep it so forever for if you fail in that thing
