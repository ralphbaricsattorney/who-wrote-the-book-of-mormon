it is my will saith the Lord that you shall understand those things in his prophecy which belong immediately and directly to you & the chapters to which I the Lord have directed your attention belong as directly to you as if the prophet had spoken to you personally. Therefore O ye saints of the living God lift your hearts on high, and place your faith on the glory which lays before you, and grovel not in the dust but acquit yourselves like those who behold eternal triumph before their eyes.

It is my will also saith the Lord that you should see Zion at the beginning as I have directed you to look at its triumph. When I your Redeemer was here in the flesh, I spake a parable to my disciples concerning Zion or the kingdom of heaven wherein the kingdom was likened to a man who planted a grain of mustard in his garden which grew to be a great tree so that the fowls of heaven lodged in its branches. I spake this parable concerning Zion as it was to come forth in the second prophetic period. Now saith the Lord I say unto you that the book of Mormon was that mustard seed. I said that it was the least of all seeds. This was said in view of the light in which the world would view that book, as the meanest the most degraded, & despisable of all books; The least of all books. The book came in due time as prophecied of & it was had many years among men but the seed was not planted. Those of the old church planted many seeds of their own, but the mustard seed they did not plant, and they continued planting seeds until my word had no place in their hearts nor in their practice and in consequence they were removed out of their place & sent away. For while they continued in their place the mustard seed could not be planted. After they were removed the mustard seed was planted by one priesthood & that priesthood was in exile when they did it, solitary and alone & of the people there were none with them, & I the Lord say of them as I have said of Obadiah that such were their faith desires & determination that it was not possible to prevent them from planting that mustard seed. It was not with them, what shall we eat or what shall we drink or wherewithal shall we be clothed: but it was, what is the word of the Lord concerning the salvation of Zion. The doctrine and principles of the book of Mormon were established in the world by that priesthood; this is what was meant by planting the seed. After the seed was planted there were a few who were prepared to commence watering the plant with my word saith the Lord, that it might grow, and it did commence to grow and continues to grow for it is my word only that can make it grow & flourish. Where my word is not there the mustard seed has not been planted, for the word of truth will make that plant grow but it will make nothing else grow.
