You will now be able to direct the church where to go. But let the elders that are among you not go to the Land only by preaching by the way for the Lord will not bless any neither going nor after they get there unless they do so.

We have a duty[?] for the duty of the department of the first presidency there. Sister Evva Adams is included in this commission. When you read the book of D. & C. you will find that the saints after they were scattered, were to be driven from place to place until there would be but few who would stand to get an inheritance. It is for you to take the book, and bring this whole out of it so that you can have it all at command, for the time has come now that you will have to use it.

Your elder brother & sister
## signed: Sidney Rigdon, Phebe Rigdon.

## heading: Section 90ty.
## date: Friendship Nov 23 1874

Br Post

Yours of 13th recd x x x I find that you had received the letter I wrote to Attica while you were gone. That letter I wrote as directed that it might form the first or beginning of the history of Zion, for the Lord dates the history of Zion at the commencement of your mission. He required me to write it as the first moving of Zion. All that had taken place since the coming of the book of Mormon as far as they were represented or spoken of as Zion, were only Zion in embryo, that was, a condition of things out of which Zion would come: but Zion in proper form had not, nor did not move till your mission. The fact can be easily seen, for in all other movements the full order of priesthood did not exist, and Zion could not move till the whole order of priesthood was complete. Such being the case Zion had to struggle in darkness till the entire order of priesthood was ordained in her; and then and not till then could she move.
