He has not only brought salvation to thee but with it eternal glory Behold saith the Lord your God My servant Israel, I present this case before you as it is before me and it is a peculiar case such as is not in existence but itself where one man was placed in such a relation to another that he raised him on high to the Highest glory without any exertion on the part of the one exalted. My servant Israel how will you requite him? All the wealth you could give him would be no compensation for the glory you have received. Verily verily saith the Lord it is under such peculiar circumstances as the bishop of Zion which you have been raised to, that you will see that my servant Sidney and mine handmaiden Phebe be your special charge, And I the Lord your God place them in that relation to you & shall require at your hand the discharge of all the duties growing out of that relation. See to it then that the voice of necessity does not from henceforth come up into the ears of the Lord your God from that place Amen.

## heading: Section 47th. The word of the Lord to the presidency of Zion.
## date: Oct 1868
## note: Presidency meet monthly

Verily, verily saith the Lord before whose eyes all things are open, who comprehendeth all things, and from whom no secret thing can be concealed. The thoughts & purposes of the hearts of all living are as present before him as are the lights which illuminate the earth: And let the ruling priesthood of Zion know that the eyes of the Lord are specially fixed upon them. He beholds them in their secret chambers and in their public walks. Their words he hears & their thoughts breathe into his ears. Now behold saith the Lord this quorum of priesthood (The first presidency of Zion had a law given unto them, a law through a perfect obedience to which they could discharge the duties of their high and holy calling and without which perfect obedience their high and holy calling would prove a curse to Zion and a scourge to themselves and the authority of that priesthood would have to depart from them.
