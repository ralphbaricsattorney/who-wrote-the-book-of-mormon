The first presidency which I the Lord had organized for the government of my kingdom fell like all the rest through the artifices of Satan & were entirely overthrown. The triumph of the devil over that whole church was before me saith your God the fall of the entire world. For my word had gone forth either directly or indirectly into all the world among all the inhabitants thereof; and when the former church thus gathered fell, the whole world being represented in it fell before me: and such was the character of that fall that a first or ruling presidency could not be organized until after the devil had exerted his whole power for destruction. But behold saith the Lord after the devil had done as he desired, and the time had come that there must be a beginning to prepare to fulfil the covenant made with the house of Israel that they in the last days should be gathered to the lands of their fathers inheritance it was needful that there should be a ruling presidency established and organized, or else my covenant and promises must fail: and none could be separated unto this holy calling only those who had proved their power to stand against the wiles and subtilties of the adversary. Therefore saith the Lord you having proved yourselves to the Lord your God that you could withstand all his seducing influences inasmuch as you had done it in the day of his greatest power over the whole world. I the Lord called upon you (I say called upon you, for there were none others left of all the people of the whole world on whom I the Lord your God could call. Every soul under the whole heavens had been overcome by the devil but yourselves.

At this gloomy period of the world, such an one as never existed before; I the Lord your God saw you as Obadiah saw you standing separate from all the rest of the world being the only ones on earth in whose hearts my word had rule.

It is my will saith the Lord that you should understand your position, for your Redeemer knows that you do not understand it and through you that all Zion may know it; not only your position but how you came to be there. I the Lord saw in your hearts a principle of honesty in your profession of religion before me that dwelt in the hearts of none others, Also a principle of integrity peculiar to yourselves and that my fear was the ruling power which held the dominion over you, & these were the causes which separated you from all the world.

Hence saith the Lord when the time came to begin my work for the restoration of my ancient covenant people. I the Lord called on you, for had the call been made on any others living, they would have treated my word with contempt as they had treated my former revelations lightly & with a neglect.
