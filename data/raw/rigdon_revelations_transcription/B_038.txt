## heading: Section 94th
## date: Oct 18th 1875

Sister Post. The revelation I send you, you will sign your name to it, and copy it so you will have it in the church at home and send the original to Br. Huffaker. may the Lord bless you & restore you to health is my prayer that you may see Zion built up according to the pattern the Lord has given.

## signed: P. Rigdon.

We received from Phineas the recorder in the court of the redeemed to say to you. Israel Huffaker made a covenant before the Lord between himself and the Lord above to the effect that for each dollar that all the rest of the saints would give for the sustaining of the department of the revelation he would give one himself that is he would give as much as all the rest.

We the members of the female department of the first presidency of Zion are required by Phineas one of the court of the redeemed that there is a covenant to that effect written to the fulfilment of which he Israel Huffaker the the first Bishop of Zion is required sacredly to see that he fulfils that covenant without failing, for he made it himself before the Lord above, And I Phineas as one of the recording angels, direct and require of you the female department of the first presidency that you notify him of that fact.

## signed: Attest Jane Post.
## signed: Phebe Rigdon

## heading: Section 95th
## date: Oct 26th 1875

The Lord to Israel Huffaker.

To my servant Israel Huffaker. I am God and beside me there is none else. I create and I destroy: from me goeth all power in heaven above and in earth. In me are treasures of wisdom, and from me wisdom goeth among the children of men. All things are present before me, and I comprehend all things. By my command man came up out of the dust & I endowed him with understanding, and made him capable of comprehending the works of my hands, and stamped my image upon him, so that he might learn of me, and be wise unto eternal life. I prepared all things for him, and bestowed them freely upon him; and he became capable of controling[?] all things that had life, which I the Lord had formed & created.
