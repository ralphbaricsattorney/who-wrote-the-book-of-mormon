## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## heading: Section 55th.
## date: June 28 1869

The word of the Lord to the first presidency of his Church. "Behold saith the Lord satan is raging with great fury and is struggling with mighty power to hurl Zion down at his feet and triumph over it that hell may rejoice in its ruin. He is availing himself of the corruptions in the hearts of those who have made great professions of faith in the laws and institutions ordained for the establishment of Zion, and for the salvation of my people. He is filling the hearts of those who were appointed unto high places in the kingdom of heaven for the purpose of redeeming the church of the living God from under the power and dominion of Satan and translating her sons and daughters into the light and liberty of the truth as it is in the bosom of the Father and the son, with pride and all its attendant evils. He is lifting up their spirits unto boasting in themselves to the uttering of great swelling words of vanity, and through the corruption of such Satan is trying to spread confusion amongst the Children of Zion, and through these means hurl Zion down into the chambers of darkness & secure its destruction.

And now verily saith the Lord to his saints if you will hear my voice, and obey my word and do in all things as I the Lord direct you; You shall overcome the Devil and triumph with Zion unto the eternal salvation of your own souls. and unto the redemption of the world, but if you will not obey my word, and do as I the Lord require, you shall be cut off and cast into the fire from whence there shall be no return, for none will be able to stand before me, the Lord your God, only such as do all things whatsoever I the Lord require of them, for your wills must be swallowed up in my will saith the Lord, or else where I am you cannot come.
