## heading: Section 44th Call to all priestds to preach
## date: Oct 1868

Br Post. I now write to you to say, the Lord is pressing by the Spirit the importance of all the authorities rising up & labouring diligently to push forward the cause of Zion. Let none excuse themselves but call upon all yea of every quorum 12, 70, or elders quorum say to every one of them in my name saith the Lord, to arise and shake themselves from the dust of Babylon & go out before the Lord and acquit themselves like those who fear God & delight in the works of righteousness.

It is my will saith the Lord that from this time forward the cause of truth should not languish nor lay silent in the hearts of those who have been called and ordained to bring Zion from the dead: but let the voice of their teachings be heard wherever there can be a door opened. And further let the authorities of my church saith the Lord purify their hearts before me that they may be blessed and have power in the Land to work for the salvation of both Jew and Gentile.

Behold saith the Lord this is my first calling unto the authorities of Zion, and those who arise at the voice of my command & go forth in hearty and faithful obedience to do my will shall by and bye be called together & be endowed to do a greater work yea the greatest of all works that ever was or ever will be done among the children of men. Who among you will then arise at my command, that I the Lord your God may glorify you with such a glory as has not been among men.

Behold saith the Lord to my servant John (whose surname is Forgeus) it is not for him to plead excuses for himself or others therefore let him cease doing so before the Lord: but let him call on all the quorum over which he presides without regard to their temporalities; & let them if any of them have excuses make them before the Lord their God. Let my servant William (whose surname is Swett) stir up his quorum to enter the field of labour and shew themselves workmen that needeth not to be ashamed & let the elders also be called on that all may go forth as one man for the day is approaching when great things must and will be done.

You of the presidency see that the will of the Lord as above written be obeyed.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon.
