## heading: Section. 45th On supporting S. Rigdon
## date: Oct. 1868

The word of the Lord to all Zion.

"Hear ye, hear ye O all ye children of Zion who have and are now coming forth before the Lord of the whole earth & who are uniting yourselves together before me saith the Lord of Sabaoth to be numbered with the children of the house of the Lord, even my Zion, which is now as one raised from the dead rising from the dust and awaking from her slumbers & presenting themselves before the powers and principalities above to have their names recorded in heaven that they may be partakers with the church of the first born who are waiting in heaven for the triumph of the house of David & for the redemption of the seed of Jacob through the conquest of Zion over all the powers of darkness through which captivity shall be led captive and eternal glory shall blaze around the redeemed of your God. I the Lord say unto you hear me, for I have somewhat to say. In order that you may reach the object of your faith even the salvation of your souls and make Zion a house of eternal blessing to your souls there are certain prominent duties which never can be dispensed with. For behold saith the Lord God, the Holy one of Israel, before the earth was, or dust fashioned into man[?] it was known in the heavens the damage there would be in having a priesthood at the head of Zion which would be employed in temporal concerns & have to devote his time to the obtaining of the necessary supplies for the support of himself & family. It is now understood among the powers that be on high that no such thing can exist & Where such a thing as that exists there Zion if it ever existed will cease to exist. And as Zion is now called upon to awake & go forth to call upon the inhabitants of the land to hear and obey the voice of the Lord their God, calling them to repentance, obedience & salvation. I the Lord call upon them to begin where Zion began & to prepare themselves to work according to the law of Zion, or else their labor will be in vain.

Behold saith the Lord the person is in existence and now in the land of the living through whom Zion has an existence & but for his prayers, his unceasing efforts, his long suffering, his endurance as seeing him who was invisible, his unflinching determination; through affliction, through persecutions, through want and necessity, without the comforts of life, without the pleasure of society, as an offcast in the world despised by all: as one unworthy of a place among the children of men, yea saith the Lord one whose name was cast out as evil in the lips of all men.
