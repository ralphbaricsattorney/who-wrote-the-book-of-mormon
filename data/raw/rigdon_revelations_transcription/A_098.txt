## heading: Section 60th. Rev. to the 12ve
## date: Aug. 3 1868

Thus saith the Lord to the Quorum of 12 belongs that order of priesthood to which the far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory is affixed. It belongs to that quorum to work out to themselves this glory:— but in order to do this they must abide the law through which this only can be done. That law is based on what Alma said to the Zoramites that the greater blessings belongs to those who receive the word of the Lord without signs. Those who obtain that glory must do it by abiding the law, for so it is written and I the Lord say that my word cannot pass away. Thus saith the Lord, behold I have found for Zion a first priesthood. but how was it he became such. It was by abiding the law of that order. He is the head of Zion on earth, & must forever be so, because he excelled all the world in comprehending the principles of my word. In these things he bounded far very far ahead of all others & thereby became the head of Zion. It was not by signs or marvellous wonders but by unceasing perseverance in studying my word. And this same law is for the 12ve, for no others can reach the glory which belongs to that order of priesthood. Thus saith the Lord had I at the holy Convocation have given them signs and wonders it would have overthrown all of them, for my word must have failed, because it was written and laid up for Zion hundreds of years before she came into existence that the highest blessings of Zions priesthood should be obtained by receiving the word of the Lord without being compelled by signs and power. Therefore saith the Lord, if signs had been given, & divine power manifested, it would have prevented the 12 from ever fulfilling the law of their calling, and if they had ever obtained any priesthood, it must have been a lower one & not the high and holy one whereunto they were called.

I the Lord further say concerning the calling of the 12 that their glory comes by achievement, and unless they are deeply read in the word of their salvation, they will never reach forward and seize upon their eternal glory. For their triumph as teachers depends upon their excelling in the knowledge of the revelations which they have, for if they do not this and let others excel them Ichabod will be written upon them, ("the glory has departed").
