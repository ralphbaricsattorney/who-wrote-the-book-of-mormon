Behold saith the Lord John in his last vision before he was separated from the world saw things pertaining to Zion which overwhelmed him with wonder and astonishment, the reason was there were great and glorious things belonging to Zion that had never entered into his heart notwithstanding his long apostolic course. And now saith the Lord I shew unto you an error which existed among those apostles yea all of them. They supposed that the order of things as established in the church through their ministration was the order under the rule of which the world would be delivered from the dominion of the powers of darkness & the reign of Christ be introduced. Under this false impression they taught and they wrote.

The prophets apostles and revelators who have gone before you never duly considered the true character of mans existence on the earth. They did not appreciate the great fact that it was by the afflictions and sufferings of woman that the earth was peopled, & that it must be through her exhaltation and glorification that the world would be delivered from the dominion of sin. Now I the Lord say to Zion & her Children that it was this eternal fact that John saw in his vision, known among you as his revelation that overwhelmed him with wonder and astonishment. Behold! woman clothed on with the royal apparel of the Holy priesthood. When that great and glorious wonder burst on his view he saw for the first time that he had cherished incorrect notions about the coming of Christ in his glory. Behold Christ could not come until the Holy Priesthood was bestowed on woman. John well knew that no such thing had place in the church he had assisted to organize and build up and consequently his entire order must pass away & give place to another. Thus he gazed with wonder and astonishment and here he closed his then career and went to his place appointed to wait till you made your appearance under whose administration woman was to be clothed with the Holiest priesthood ever bestowed on any living

Behold and be astonished O all ye children of Zion while I the Lord your God reveal unto you a fact a great and eternal fact concerning this vision of visions which John saw. He saw a wonderful event which will take place at the time of the great and last "resurrection" when the whole intelligent creation as resurrected shall come into Judgment. Then will come the August scene that John saw when the redeemed shall in full order, as organized in full quorums pass before the wicked & condemned in their royal apparel. Then & there
