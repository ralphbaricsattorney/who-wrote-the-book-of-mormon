so decreed from the begining[?]. If you fail you fail as transgressers[?] and such shall be cut off

Look at the old abomination and see where they are. lost in the Devils controversy about Polygamy and Monogamy As though as though either of these could save any body. But brethren and sisters go ye and learn the Lords system of salvation

In carrying out the whole law[?] of this cause[?] you will have days devoted to fasting and prayer which will consist of abstaining from food an intire[?] day and continuing in prayer You must see[?] that this is done at least one day out of each week so that the hearts of all may become purified and sanctified unto[?] the Lord that your organization may become an indwelling of the Holy Ghost.

It is your eternal salvation after which you are seeking and the Lord deigns to shew you how you can obtain it for it was on this wise that all the Angels of the pavillion obtained their situation and it is on this wise you will become heirs with them. If any should give out that started with you let them go, but let those who are determined continue till they overcome and the glory that others would have had will fall[?] to those who overcome

Brethren and sisters be faithful unto death and you will obtain a crown of life
## signed: Phebe Rigdon, Sidney Rigdon
