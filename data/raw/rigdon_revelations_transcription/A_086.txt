## note: The former Rev calls Evva Adams the Lords Handmaid Therefore we here insert the following.

## heading: Angel of the Lord's communication to Evva. March 1869

"I the Lord send my angel of light to talk with my handmaiden Evva. I the Lord hath owned her as my handmaid, and let the Children of Zion no longer doubt that this gift which I have bestowed upon her is not of God. Many of the Children of Zion have said that this is not of God, but wo be unto them for their Lord God sayeth they are in darkness. For I the angel of light warn you in the name of your Lord and Savior Jesus Christ that if each and every one who has said one word against my handmaid, if you do not go and make an humble confession, and ask her forgiveness, I the Lord will not bless you. Those who have asked her forgiveness of what they have said against her, I the Lord will bless them with my choicest blessings. The Lord loves all those who love one another, and who will keep his holy commandments, Many a plain and precious thing will come to you the Children of Zion from the Watch tower. this beautiful Message I the Lord who sits high upon my throne give to my handmaiden Eva that the Children of Zion may see where they stand. Now let not one of you doubt this which I the Lord giveth to my handmaid Evva for all good cometh from I the Lord even so Amen"

## heading: Extract
## date: Mar. 1859[?]

" The Lord tells me to say to his handmaiden Sarah that though she has sinned before him yet he has deep compassion on her & if she will now turn to him with her whole heart she shall be forgiven & shall be lifted on high".

" Perhaps it might be gratifying to our young sister Evva to know who the holy Messenger is that is ministering to her. The Lord told me that it was the Martyr Stephen; my prayer & desire before the Lord are that she may be able to acquit herself before the Lord as one on whom the Lord has bestowed such distinguished blessings"
## signed: Sidney Rigdon.
