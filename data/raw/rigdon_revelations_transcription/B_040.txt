Hence let the companion of that court here in the flesh, not be surprised to find their doings and sayings here written there. The law that was given at the beginning to the holy priesthood is the same now as it was then and always remains the same. For they were a chosen generation, a royal priesthood that they should shew forth the glory of him who had called them, and they were consecrated unto God for this purpose to continue before him Eternally. They are they who are the "elect" of God and unless they transgress they will continue in the court of the redeemed as their inheritance and none can dispossess them.

But should any of them fall by transgression it would be better for them that they had never been born. (Heb. 6th 4, 5. 6th) Of such it is written that they cannot serve two Masters, They cannot serve God and Mammon. Peter say of himself and of the 12 of the Apostles, that they had left all and followed Christ. Of this priesthood it is said they shall not lay up treasures on earth. At the time of their ordination all the property they have goes with them, and like themselves becomes the property of the kingdom of heaven; It cannot be lawfully used for an inheritance for any only those who are members of the church, and in that case no distinction can be made between those who are natural children more than others.

Let the priesthood then seek diligently to understand the duties which belong to their calling that they may approve themselves unto the Lord, lest through negligence they come short and fail, for should they do so their destruction is certain and their damnation sure.

Remember whom it is you serve lest he come at a day when ye think not, and an hour when you are not looking for him, and you are hurled down among hypocrites and unbelievers. Amen.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon, Phebe Rigdon
