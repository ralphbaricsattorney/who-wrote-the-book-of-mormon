## heading: Utah Section 61st Proclamation & to B Young
## date: Dec 6th 1870

The people of Utah who have professed to receive the Book of Mormon & to receive the work of the Lord in these last times.

"Know Ye then that by reason of your ignorance of the powers of the holy priesthood you attempted in the first place, to dictate it; finding you could not do that, you then thought to destroy it all of which you have failed to do. Instead of doing so, you by attempting it, have wrought out your own eternal destruction at its expense by struggling to take all its rights from it. In all ages of the world when the Lord had a priesthood on the earth, where the wicked tried for purposes of their own gratification, to destroy it; and seized upon the rights and prerogatives of that priesthood, the Lord gave to that priesthood, power and authority before him to doom such to destruction and he would execute it to the very uttermost.

In the exercise of this prerogative, We, in the name of Jesus Christ By the authority of the eternal Father, and in accordance with all the powers & principalities above doom you to an absolute & everlasting destruction. You shall be cut off by the hand of wicked men, your land shall be to you a desolation and your city shall be smitten with destruction: Your works shall perish in the overthrow, and your habitations shall be left desolate. In the hour of your calamities, you shall weep but none will pity; you shall cry for mercy, but none will hear. You shall be as the salt that has lost its savor fit only to be cast out and trodden under foot of man. And your judgment is not sleeping, neither is the wrath which will lay you waste, slumbering. you shall be cast into the fire from whence there is no return.

The time is drawing near when the authorities who have preserved themselves before the Lord in righteousness and truth, are to be called on for the gathering of Israel whereunto they were called from the beginning and for which great work they were required to qualify themselves before the Lord. This was to be done by coming before the Lord and receive his instruction until they were mighty to expound all scripture that such might know the certainty of all things pertaining to the kingdom of heaven on earth. In connection with this they were to make themselves acquainted with countries kingdoms, and nations with the knowledge of the laws of both God and man.

Being thus qualified they were to move in the great work of prepareing[?] the way for the coming of Christ and Elijah. And in one department of that work that the way may be cleared for its progress is the solemn and awful duty of notifying you of the ruin to which you are doomed and the wasting and desolation which are rolling upon you, and the blood and horror through which your calamities will be perfected.
