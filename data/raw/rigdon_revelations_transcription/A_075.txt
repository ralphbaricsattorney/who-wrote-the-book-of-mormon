attachment to Zion & for redemption. & know all ye children of Zion that it was on this wise that the voice of Zion has been sounded & is sounding in your ears for had he not maintained his integrity without failure Zion could not have been redeemed. Let it then be known in all the Land and be proclaimed in the ears of all who seek salvation that salvation cometh only through the ministration of my servant Sidney Rigdon for he alone maintained his integrity in the day when satan overwhelmed the first church with his corruption & took it to himself & he has failed not & fainted not until Zion is now beginning to move. Therefore saith the Lord the mighty God the everlasting Father & the prince of peace. His existence in Zion is as necessary to her existence as the spirit of holiness, for Zion cannot exist except both are there. And now saith the Lord the time has come that all the children of Zion must know and realize their dependance on him as well as on me (your Lord) for your salvation & therefore let all of the Children of Zion be assured that I the Lord hold him in the same light & with the same sacred regard that I do Zion, for without his welfare Zion can never prosper. Therefore saith the Lord I will deal with the Children of Zion as they deal with him. It is required of all the children of Zion that they see to it that all his temporal wants are supplied. The law under which he brought forth Zion forbid him to engage in worldly business & that law he maintained before me, and it is the law of his office and will remain so. In consequence of this he had to procure his subsistence & that of his family by the hardest means through which such things are obtained: but this state of things must cease forever. I the Lord will deal with the Children of Zion as they deal with my Servant Sidney Rigdon. If his wants are not supplied to the fulness I the Lord will not bless the Children of Zion. Those who do not their duty to him need not call on me saith the Lord for mercy in the day of trouble, for I will not hear them. When sickness afflicts their houses and they call on me I will not hear them, neither will I be entreated by others for them. I do not present his claim as an object of your charity but to whom you are under obligation above all others. It is of first importance that the Children of Zion should attend to this & see that there is no failure for if there is it will be fatal to those who cause it. The bishop of Zion is required
