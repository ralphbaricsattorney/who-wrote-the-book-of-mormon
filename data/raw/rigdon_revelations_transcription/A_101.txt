D.C. — But those who escape falling with the blasphemers of the Lord, unless they turn to the Lord with their whole heart, will perish with the world. Therefore let the priesthoods of Zion see to it, that the house of the Lord is put into order before him. Organize yourselves as speedily as possible, call your solemn assemblies and let all the priesthoods take their places both male and female. Let the house of the Lord become a house of prayer, a house of righteousness, a house for the understanding and teaching the word of the Lord. Let the priesthoods lips keep knowledge, and let all learn truth & righteousness at their mouth. The time has come when all slothfulness and idleness in these matters must pass away and when the whole priesthood as one individual must be found drawing near to the Lord through the knowledge of his word, for there is no possibility of drawing near to the Lord only by understanding and obeying his word.

Call those brethren who are a distance off to come and join in council with you that all may know and see alike. Call your brethren from Canada Page and Buhn. Let all understand the solemnity of the period we have reached. Though multitudes who once belonged with us are gone without seeing the day we see, it is our glorious lot to have endured until it is at our very doors, for had we failed our doom would have been to open our eyes in hell in torment. Know that the judgments of God which are now coming on to Utah for her destruction are the beginning of the judgments which shall continue till the world is overthrown. At least so says the revelation written by Joseph Smith, & they are only a continuation of what was written by all the holy prophets since the world began; and we can say in the language of one who has been gone for hundreds of years, "that on us the ends of the world has come. Seeing then we look, yea, more than that are doomed to see such things what manner of men ought we to be in holiness and godly conversation, looking for the awful day that prophet[?] and righteous men had testified of ever since the world began. Organize the Children of Zion so to have them taught these things Know there is no time to lose, In vain will it be for any to seek to lay up property to be had for their families, for their families will never get it, and all it can do is to be a moth to eat up their salvation. The time is at hand & doubtless many of us will live to see it and in that day it shall be as Paul said 1 Cor. 7c. 29, 30 & 31st verses read and understand. Be sure and see that all the priesthoods stand in their lot each one male and female discharging his or her duty in faithfulness & diligence. Amen.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon
