## heading: Section 27th
## date: Feb 1867

Behold saith the Lord, let all the Children of Zion hear the word of him from whom life & salvation flows that it may be well with them and that the work of righteousness may prosper in their hands, and that Zion may be established through their ministry never to be thrown down, for it is the will of him who rules in heaven, and who must and will rule on the earth that all those who profess to serve him may be enabled to do it in truth & in righteousness. Therefore saith the Lord your God give heed to the words which I say unto you. — Zion or the kingdom of heaven as now appearing is in fulfilment of the prophecies which have been spoken by all the holy prophets since the world began. It was concerning the kingdom of heaven which was to appear in the last times even as spoken of by Daniel the prophet, and the great things connected with its establishment and progress on the earth that inspired the holy prophets with their sublimest strains. To that period they looked as the time when the reign of wickedness should cease on the earth & the redemption of the world should come. They sought diligently before me saith the Lord that they might understand the things pertaining to the triumph of Zion which has now come unto you, yea saith the Lord they sought with such faith and perseverance before the Lord your God, as well as their God that they knew of your coming up on the earth and of your power to stand against the floods of corruption and all the devices of Satan & bring up the light of Zion out of the midst of darkness, & proclaim it to the world: and they prophecied of your appearing in the world thousands of years before you were born and they rejoiced over you as though you had existed in their day, and their times & sang praises to the Lord their God with exceeding great joy. They saw your day & the people thereof and they beheld that they were all given to idolatry, struggling one against another, seeking for riches and honor, and the fame of this world, and that the devil had power over all the earth. Among this number of prophets and righteous men was Obadiah who prophecied of your day and your times, for so great was his faith & his desire that nothing could be withheld from him. He beheld how entirely the Gentiles were given over to the works of darkness, and he looked till he saw the book of Mormon come forth, and he beheld that many of the Gentiles received it and gathered together to learn the things of God and he was glad; but he saw that after a season the devil began to get power over them also as well as other Gentiles. And he beheld that they began to be lifted up in the pride of their hearts and began to contend with one another, and he saw that they were overcome by the powers of darkness and were cast down as at this day. And when he saw it his heart sank within him and he fell upon his face and wept bitterly. Wailing before the Lord his God, fearing that all was lost forever.
