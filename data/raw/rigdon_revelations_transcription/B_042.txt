This greatest of all promises that I the Lord ever made to man I caused to be confirmed by the first female prophetess, Namely my hand Maiden Phebe Rigdon that I the Lord appointed in my church; a woman greatly beloved of God, in whose heart I the Lord have found no guile. That the saints of my kingdom may see the true character of this designation of the head authority in the Israel of our God, I the Lord testify that for many years while the powers of darkness were reigning his voice was the only one coming up into the ears of the Lord. All before me were running after their own imaginations. He was alone before the Lord seeking with tears and supplications for the salvation of Zion. His heart never turned away nor did his faithfulness fail, nor did his purpose change It is meet that I the Lord should give this testimony.

During this long period when all had failed, his whole soul was devoted to the understanding of my word, until he had risen in point of knowledge above all living in things pertaining to the revelations of my word. And thus he stands this day in my sight, and will eternally stand so.

From the beginning it was appointed that he who reached the priesthood of redemption should obtain it by overcoming the powers of darkness when they were let loose upon him in full force and conquer[?] his eternal crown against all the powers of earth & hell.

Now I the Lord present such an one to the kingdom of Jesus Christ. One who when an entire organization had been overcome by the powers of darkness. The head, the prophet, the seer & revelator had fallen before the artifices of Satan, and his whole organization had followed him into captivity of the devil, he, my servant Sidney whose surname is Rigdon stood steadfast and failed not. To such an one do I the Lord appoint as head of Zion. Because in these respects he has no equal. (sidney Rigdon)

The prophets of Ancient days had knowledge of such an order of things being established for the restoration of Israel for I the Lord authorized my servant Isaiah of the days of old to testify of such a condition of things in order for the gathering of Israel and hence he said as the days of a tree so shall be the days of my people &c.
