You will there find that Israel was not to be gathered among the Gentiles among whom the book of Mormon came forth but the priesthoods would go elsewhere to gather Israel: but let me not forestall you, but take the books before the Lord & you will find that the Lord had a place in his mind where Zion could and would become a great people. A place where, when sickness and death laid waste the old abomination "Zion" would be safe. These things would let you know where Zion could not be, when this nation was being laid waste, Zion you will find is to be safe. The fact here is settled that Zion will not be built in the bounds of this nation.

Zion will have to be situated in a country where access can be had to the Lamanites which cannot be had in this country. But let us bring this to a close. There is but one people in the world from the midst of whom Zion can come forth. It is alone in the Brittish possessions of this country where Zion can be established; no where else on the earth are Nephites found, here also are Lamanites, and here also is the colony of Blacks. Here then is a platform for gathering Israel that can be found no where else in the world. The duty is yours to see to it that the work is attended to that we may not be found among transgressors. The diligence and unceasing perseverance used by the first president to get the work where it is will bring it to a glorious triumph on your part. Sell all the property you have there because it can be of no use there for the work of the Lord will cease there altogether sell therefore and go your way as soon as you can. No more

## signed: Sidney Rigdon
