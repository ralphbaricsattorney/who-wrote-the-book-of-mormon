## note: a short covering note on a cut slip of ruled paper, in the same large, loose, looping hand as L_003 to L_005; no left margin and no margin labels

Bro Post— I send the word of the Lord just as soon as I could after the commandment came thinking it might reach you before you left for Canada You and br Hufaker[?] are required to see that it is attended to without delay that all may have an oppertunity[?] to obey without delay.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon
