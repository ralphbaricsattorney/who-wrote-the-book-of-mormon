know that the house of God is a house of order and cases which are sent up before the Lord must first be sent to my servants Stephen and Israel and they must decide on the case, whether it is such a case as ought to be sent up or not. No case can come before the Lord your God only in the way I have appointed: no individual can send any matter before the Lord. When it is sent to my servant Sidney it is sent to me for it is through him only that I the Lord receive questions for settlement.

Thus saith the Lord I have in this way arranged Zion for your salvation. I the Lord have said that Zion is in the world and I have now shewn you how it came here by the sufferings and sacrifices and unceasing perseverance of two individuals for they are as much the Father and Mother of Zion as was Abraham the father of his family or the father of the faithful.

And now I the Lord say to the Children of Zion that they shall be the objects of your special care. None of you high authorities would ever have existed to rule in my Zion saith the Lord had it not been for their devotion and undying perseverance before the Lord your God when you knew nothing of it for years after years in succession. I the Lord then require that you shall seek diligently to see that their temporal necessities are all supplied, and I the Lord say that I will supply to those who give of their substance to that end a rich reward; but those who withhold condemnation here and judgment hereafter.

Behold and lo! Verily, verily saith the Lord I require that the officers in my church yea all the priesthoods will see to it that these things shall be had and known in all the branches of Zion. Amen.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon, Phebe Rigdon

## date: Sept /72
Upon the case of presidency of Canada mission presented before the Lord He[?] said that Jesse Crosby should preside
## signed: S Rigdon

## heading: On Canada affairs.
## date: Oct 8 1872 to S. Post
I however wish to say that the presidency there as it is one of great consequence[?], the presiding officer ought to have two counsellors both ordained to the priesthood of the same grade of himself. & I will authorize you as my representative to choose them as they should be chosen by the first presidency according to the spirit of revelation.

Having called on my companion, the head and prophetess of the female priesthood for it is a case I trusted altogether unto you and her decision pertaining to the appointing and setting apart a female priesthood there. After the necessary consultation before the Lord, She says that you shall ordain sister Green to the office of the High priesthood and I add that she shall be identified with the quorum of the presidency.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon
