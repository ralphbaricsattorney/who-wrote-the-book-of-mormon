And also all the covenants made with & promises given to Lehi and Nephi and their seed can by this order be fulfilled. Hence the hearts of the fathers by it, yea all of them are turned to the children; Therefore need I the Lord tell you that the heavens rejoice over you, surely not, for had you not come forth and bowed to the requirements of the Most High, and held on to the principles of righteousness until you had obtained the holy priesthood of the restitution, the hearts of the fathers could not have been turned to the children because the covenants made with them could not be fulfilled.

And again I the Lord say unto my servant Joseph and handmaiden Sarah that inasmuch as you hold the first operative priesthood before the Lord, or the priesthood of the first things, it must needs be that your priesthood be put in order first. Therefore I the Lord require that your house be put in order before me, that you fail not in the discharge of the duties of your high calling for it is for you to have the oil that the lamps may be kept trimmed. Lift up your heads and rejoice while all the principalities & and powers above rejoice over you. Amen"

## signed: Sidney Rigdon Phebe Rigdon.

## heading: Section 17th
## date: Oct. 1864

The word of the Lord through his prophet & prophetess Sidney and Phebe to the first presidency of the operative priesthood his servant and handmaiden Joseph and Sarah. Thus saith the Lord to his servant and handmaiden Joseph and Sarah. It is necessary for the salvation of Zion that you should become acquainted with the high and holy callings of your priesthood; for on the faithful discharge of the duties thereof depends the salvation of Zion. I the Lord in a former revelation said unto you that it belonged to the duties of your presidency to see that the fountain of revelation should not be dried up: And though neither he who got the revelation nor you to whom it was sent understood the import of that requirement, I the Lord understanding the determination of satan and his power for evil and knowing what he would do in order to prevent the establishment of Zion, made that requirement of you, and made it the prominent and first duty of your calling before me saith the Lord.
