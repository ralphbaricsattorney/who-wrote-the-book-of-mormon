Therefore see to it that you fail not, but continue to pray and to intercede before the Lord as the poor widow before the unjust judge, till you do prevail. (See Luke 18th C 1st to 8th v. S.R.)

Let all the daughters of "Zion" also have their solemn assemblies of prayer before the Lord, that while the priesthoods are in the field of labor teaching and proclaiming, the sisters may be sending up their prayers and supplications into the pavillion for the success of those who are in the field of labor. — Thus let all engage and cease not until the people I the Lord have sent to you will bow to the word of eternal life. Let there be no faltering or ceasing till the truth is established indeed and in truth and in power. Let there be no contests about who shall preside for he who raises a quibble about this shall be cut off from my people saith the Lord. When you begin right as the Lord has directed, and continue to do so, you will not, you can not, labour in vain, for the Lord has so decreed from the beginning. If you fail you fail as transgressors and such shall be cut off.

Look at the old abomination & see where they are, lost in the Devils controversy about Polygamy and Monogamy. as though either of these could save any body. But brethren & sisters go ye and learn the Lords system of salvation. In carrying out the whole law of this case you will have days devoted to fasting and prayer, which will consist of abstaining from food an entire day, and continue in prayer. You must see that this is done at least one day out of each week, so that the hearts of all may become purified & sanctified unto the Lord that your organization may become an indwelling of the Holy Ghost. It is your eternal salvation after which you are seeking, and the Lord deigns to shew you how you can obtain it. For it was on this wise that all the angels of the pavilion obtained their situation and it is on this wise you will become heirs with them. If any should give out that started with you let them go but let those who are determined continue till they overcome and the glory that others would have had will fall to those who overcome. Brethren & sisters be faithful unto death and obtain a crown of life.

## signed: Phebe Rigdon, Sidney Rigdon
