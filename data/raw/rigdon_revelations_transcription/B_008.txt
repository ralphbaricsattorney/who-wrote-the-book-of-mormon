## heading: Section 2nd Extracts of a letter 80
## date: Oct 9 /73

Br. Post: I thought I would pen a few reflections about our situation. It is a matter of solemn gratification to understand "as Zion" that we have had a country placed before us where "Zion" can find a place for the soles of her feet, a something which has been denied her in the land of our nativity. x x x x x

But though in deep exile we doubled our diligence to search out the things of God. year in and year out the inquiry ceased not to agitate the mind.

Finally while alone before the Lord we got an answer and to our surprise it was in what is called the "new dominion" C.W. In answering an audible voice answered from heaven. On further enquiry I find that my ideas at first were too limited for the grant covers the whole country from the sea east to the sea west and from the Lakes to the Nephites. here then is the legitimate field for the Children of Zion to occupy in building of Zion,

It is in this large field of labor where Zion is to become terrible. It is here where their enemies are to be vanquished. But our soul says, when.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon

P.S. "As to the change it may commence at any time those desiring to go can do so as people go into other countries."
