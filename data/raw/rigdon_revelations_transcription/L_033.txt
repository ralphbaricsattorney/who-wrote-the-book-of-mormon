## heading: Revelation, Given May, 1868.

As the time appointed of the Lord for the begining of the organization of Zion is drawing near, it is of Great importence that the incoming Preisthood[?] Should understand the Relations[?] they bear to Zion, and to the Priesthood through which Zion is making her appearence in the world,—

I the Lord deign to Give to my people, the Children of Zion, the Knowledge of these things, as far as is wisdom in me the God of Zion to do it,—

—Behold, Saith the Lord, that it is the purpose of him who Claims Zion as his own, that in Establishing Righteousness on the Eaarth[?] which Can Only be done through the Rule and triumph of Zion, that a Complete and perfect Separation Should take place between Zion and any or all other Societies or associations of men, as spoken of by Nephi, when he said, there were but two Churches, one is the Church of the Lamb of God, and the other is the Church of the Devil,— Zion in her organization, Order and Rule, are as Separate from all others, as is the Church of the Lamb of God, Separate from that of the Devil,—

Behold and Lo Saith the Holy one of Israel, all that the Lord your God has done since He inspired Joseph Smith to bring from darkness and Obscurity the Book of Mormon, has been done in view of, and for the purpose of bringing to pass this Separation on the Earth, for whatever there were pertaining to the Lord, his truth and Righteousness on the Earth among men, were so Obscured by the works of darkness and the Subtility of the Devil, that they had lost all power to Salvation,—
