As I said so I the Lord say again the old church never planted the mustard seed, go where you will saith the Lord among a people who are acting under the influence of the old church, and my word saith the Lord is not there. If they had planted the mustard seed it would have been there for where the mustard is there is my word & where it is not planted there my word is not.

Behold now saith the Lord to his people of Zion. Look & consider, see Zion at the time the mustard seed was planted in the gloom of exile by one priesthood alone & then look at the vision Isaiah saw concerning it, & realize that the glory Isaiah saw was the plant that sprung from the mustard seed. Fix your faith saith the Lord upon the glory & triumph that is there set before you & shew yourselves valiant for the truth that you may push Zion to her triumph. For verily saith the Lord, if the Children of Zion will acquit themselves before me as did the first priesthood in order that the mustard seed might be planted, they shall see Zion in the glory of Isaiahs vision. For if they do that they will not have done as great a work before me as did the first priesthood do in order that the mustard seed might be planted saith the Lord your God. Amen.

## signed: S. Rigdon

## heading: Section 28th
## date: June 1867

The word of the Lord To the quorum of the first presidency of his kingdom as called and organized for the fulfilment of the covenants made to the house of Israel.

I the Lord having called and chosen you out of all the world to order arrange and establish my kingdom for the Salvation of Israel according to the covenant made with their fathers. It is needful that you should have my law, that you may act in righteousness before me in establishing my Zion amongst the children of men both Jew & Gentile for unto this work are you called.
