## heading: Section 31st To appoint assistants.
## date: Oct 1867

Behold I the Lord say unto my servant Stephen Post, inasmuch as you are the head of the Evangelical order of the priesthood, it belongs to your presidency when any are appointed to presiding offices in the department of the Evangelists and it should afterwards appear that those who were appointed had not power to discharge all the duties of such presidency. After sufficient time given to prove the fact to themselves and others, you shall appoint assistants as wisdom may direct to aid said president in the discharge of the duties of his calling. In the mean time taking the direct oversight of that presidency yourself.

Therefore I the Lord say unto you in the case of my servant John A. Forgeus you shall proceed without delay to appoint assistants of all or any of the priesthood under your presidency, & all who act as Evangelists are under your presidency to assist him in obtaining the quorum of 12 before me the chief head of Zion for the time has fully come that this work must be done, take therefore that department in the duty of the president of the 12ve. into your own hands calling upon my servant John A Forgeus, and any in whom you have confidence to give their power to the work until it is accomplished, and let there be no delay nor idleness that the work may be speedily done.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## heading: Section 32nd.
## date: Feb 1868

To the quorum of the first presidency.

Behold saith the Lord as the time is drawing near for the holy convocation of the priesthoods of my people I the Lord say unto you prepare ye, prepare ye, O ye Children of Zion for the solemnities of that day that you may come up before the Lord having your hearts cleansed from an evil conscience and your bodies washed in pure water according to the law established from days of old for the purifying of the people to stand before the Lord in high and holy callings.

I the Lord appoint unto you the 4th day of July 1868, that is the July of this year as the day when you shall appear before me the Lord your God in a holy convocation, to ordain and organize the quorum of 12ve according to the pattern you have received. The holy convocation shall be held with my people at Attica Marion Co. Iowa. And let all those who desire to be there on that solemn occasion take with them their own supplies for food and for lodging and for whatsoever they may need that my people at Attica be not burdened above what they are able to bear.

And again let those who go to the holy Convocation see, from the hour they receive this that they devote themselves to prayer & supplication, and to searching their own hearts before me saith the Lord that they may come up into the presence of the Lord acceptably
