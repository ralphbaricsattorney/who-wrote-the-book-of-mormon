## heading: Section 57th
## date: Dec 31, 1869

"The word of the Lord to his servants Stephen & Israel. It is required by me saith the Lord that my servant Stephen shall see that the mission to the coloured[?] colony in Canada is executed to perfection, that is to success, and the Lord his God lays it upon him that through him it shall be done. Let him therefore go himself at his earliest convenience, and labor with all diligence and faithfulness till the work is done. For the Lord his God will not hold him guiltless unless that work is accomplished.

For in order that my servant Stephen may approve himself unto me; For I the Lord will prove him to the extent of his abilities as they are before me, he must stand before me in uprightness of heart and obtain power with the heavens to do all things whatsoever is required at his hand It is required of such as my servant Stephen that they overcome and receive the far more & exceeding Eternal weight of glory, or else to be cut off & receive the greater condemnation.

And again saith the Lord let my servant Israel move forward in discharge of the duties of his calling and in the absence of my servant Stephen let him be faithful and diligent in observing all the duties of his calling for I the Lord require of him, that in the absence of my servant Stephen he shall stand in his office and discharge the duties of the first presidency in connection with my handmaiden Jane according to the law of their holy calling. For if they seek after wisdom and understanding they shall be given to them. Therefore let them council together that they also may approve themselves unto the Lord their God their Redeemer even the Holy one of Israel, Behold saith the Lord if the first presidency of Zion will walk in obedience to the law of their calling, they need fear no goings & comings of persons of unsteady minds if they keep their own hearts immoveable in righteousness they have the power of triumph in themselves. They have nothing to fear only corruption in their own hearts."

## signed: Sidney Rigdon.
