## heading: Section 5th 83
## date: Friendship Nov 6th 1873

Br Post: In reading your letter the Lord gave me his spirit to testify to your letter, that you understand how to go to work in prepareing for the building up Zion. I am gratified to see you firm and engaged in the great work of gathering Israel in these last days. May the Lord give you wisdom & understanding how to act under all circumstances; that you may be able to do his work in righteousness before him is my prayer.

From your sister in the Lord

## signed: Phebe Rigdon.

Br Post. As this communication is closing seeing the Lord has said as he did all I can say is the view you took of the whole case is what the Lord approves. Go then and do according to what was in your heart when you wrote that letter and the thing you will tend to is the building of Zion but nothing else will do it.

Such is the case as the Lord said to the female priesthood for to her he spoke and not to me. When I look to what he said I see it was what I write here.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon.

## heading: Section 6th 84
## date: 1873 Dec. 5th
