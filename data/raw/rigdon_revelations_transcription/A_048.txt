And again saith the Lord the character those prophets gave to the nations against which they pronounced the judgments of heaven were symbolical of the character of the present nations and peoples of the Gentiles. Their pride & haughtiness, Their thirst for riches & for fame one of another. They oppressed the poor to enrichen[?] themselves. Their daughters high and lifted up in the pride of their hearts mincing as they went, Instead of seeking to serve the Lord, they sought for marriages through which they could gratify their pride & sensuality. So it is before me with the Gentiles of this period. It can be said of them as the Isaiah said of the Jews, "The ox knoweth his owner & the ass his masters crib" but this people do not know they do not consider. And yet as did the Jews so do they, for the Jews after they had rejected and crucified me their Savior they sought to secure the favor of heaven by their offerings & sacrifices. They kept their alters smoking, their sacrifices bleeding & the fat thereof burning continually, thinking thereby to procure the favor of heaven; and yet they trampled the law of their God under foot and crucified him whom he had sent to them.

Truly did Isaiah say concerning their sacrifices that they were an abomination to the Lord. And so it is now with the nations of the Gentiles, they have rejected my word saith the Lord, and sought the destruction of those by whom it was sent, but they are trying to procure the favor of heaven by means of their own. They have their Missionary Societies, their bible Societies, their benevolent societies almost without number. These are their sacrifices with which they are trying to obtain the favor of the Lord. But saith the Lord these things are an abomination in my sight, seeing they have rejected the word of truth, and trampled under their feet the covenant of their salvation. And now saith the Lord it is needful that I should say something to you concerning Zion, and that you may understand what I say let me direct your attention to the 60th 61st & 62nd Chapters of Isaiahs prophecy and there see what the prophet saw in his vision concerning Zion. The power the influence & the glory which surrounded it. This prophecy belongs to you O ye children of Zion. There is but one Zion as there is but one Lord & one faith, and it was this Zion that the prophet beheld as there by him described. It is its destiny to rise to that grandeur, majesty & glory. And know ye children & priesthoods of Zion, that it belongs to you to push it to the glorious triumph there described. To this end are you called and to accomplish this work are you appointed. I the Lord your Redeemer direct your attention to the glorious triumph of the Zion to which you belong and to the great point on which to fix your faith for I say unto you as I did to the Nephites concerning Isaiah that he prophecied of all things pertaining to Zion or my kingdom, and I repeat to you what I said to them that all things that he prophecied shall be fulfilled.
