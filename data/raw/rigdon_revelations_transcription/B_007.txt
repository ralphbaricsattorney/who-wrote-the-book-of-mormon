## heading: Section 1st 79
## date: 1873 Oct 4th

Br. Post.

I will try and answer your letter. It is the strangest thing that has transpired since my career in life.

The questions I sent you did not come from the Lord but through the recording angel from the eternal circle which holds in obeyance the Lambs book of life. I am told that all the members of that circle entered into their position through sacrifice and you know what is said in the history of the spirits. "That all spokesman in Zion belong to that circle" Therefore before you proceed on your mission, and before your name is recorded for eternity. whether you remain in the flesh or whether you pass. The holy circle requires an expression of the Character of your sacrifice. When we report favorably, as we shall, in obedience to your sacrifice, "Stephen, Farewell Babylon, Yea Farewell world forever"!! The voice of the holy circle will reach you and when it does start instantly and ask no questions,

The change of the location of Zion will be from under the rule of this government to that of the Brittish. The lakes dividing us. When you get orders which you will before long send them over the lake and there let the priesthoods go to work in the cause of their holy calling and let them fail not. Zion will be fixed there till the time spoken of when she shall become very great. This great country for it is a very large one, is the one where the Children of Zion can be perfectly safe while this side the lakes the people shall be drinking their own blood as the old prophetic word has told us. Make no stir or commotion. Let the people rise and go home and leave Babylon to her own works and ways.

## signed: S. Rigdon

P.S. I now having reached the place of our future or home of Zion. Having done the work alone when you were all enjoying. I now say in the name and fear of God. Let not my house be destitute and empty for many days at a time but let it be supplied without regard to yourself or others by the law of "Tithing" as soon as it can be established.

## signed: S. Rigdon, Phebe Rigdon
