## heading: Section 88. Ans. to conference
## date: March 6th 1874

Br. Post: The Lord sends the following things to you as a response to your letter of the 1st of Mar.

"It is my will saith the Lord the God of Zion to let my people know the care that I have for them. As soon as they begin to shew themselves, I the Lord notify them that the heavens begin to prepare for their deliverance in the day of calamity which all the prophets have seen and have prophesied concerning, and left their testimony on record. Every day and every year is pushing the hour forward.

In view of that dread day the Lord your God gives you counsel, for there are no people who call on my name who can live here when the day of wrath comes. Therefore as a Father of mercy I the Lord have provided a place for you where you and yours can dwell when wrath is revealed in fury upon the heads of transgressors. It remains for you therefore to avail yourselves of the safety provided, and escape, or to take power to yourselves and run the risk of casting my counsel and mercy behind your backs
## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## heading: Section 89
## date: Sept 22d[?] 1874

To the Spokesman of Zion greeting:

Yours of the 16th was received last evening with the 5$[?] all safe It is to us the most important document ever received by us, as it settles the most important question there ever was in existence pertaining to Zion. "We say Zion" because Zion is with those whom the Lord appointed to go out before him, and point to the place where he, (the Lord) would commence to lay the foundation of the true Zion of his (the Lords) creation.
