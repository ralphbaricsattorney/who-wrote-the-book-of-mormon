## heading: Section 5th Rev. to Abraham Burtis:

"Thus saith the Lord to my servant Abraham Burtis, I have looked upon thee and seen thy works; thou wast with thine own consent ordained before me to a high and holy priesthood, and thou hast not up till this time honored[?] thy calling but hast treated it lightly and I have called upon thee now to arise and shake thyself from the dust of Babylon that thou may'st deliver thyself from her sins and her plagues. — I the Lord know thee, there is not a thought of thy heart but is present with me, you cannot deceive him with whom you have to do: For I the Lord your God am not man that he can be imposed upon; therefore I send this my word to you for the last time. — And verily, verily I say unto thee that if thou dost not awake from the sleep of spiritual death and do as I require I swear in my wrath thou shalt never enter into my rest. I will hurl thee down at a time when thou thinkest not, & thou shalt be cast out as salt which has lost its savor and I the Lord will shew thee no mercy in the day of thy calamity Neither will I hear thy prayers in the day when judgment and wrath shall overtake thee. Thy name shall be blotted out of the book of remembrance before me saith the Lord & thou shalt wail in tribulation, and lament in despair. I the Lord say this in mine anger, and unless thou hearest and comest forth with thine whole heart before me saith the Lord calling upon my name continually for grace to enable you to do my will, verily saith the Lord my wrath shall burn against thee until thou shalt raise thy voice in howling lamentation where hope has forever perished."
