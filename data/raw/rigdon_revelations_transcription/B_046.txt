The Lord appoints his servant Stephen Post judge and organizer of this court of the kingdom."

To us this is an entire new thing; for when the Lord said he would cut any one off from his kingdom and thrust them down to hell I supposed till now he would do so himself, but to us he gives a different law; to which we are bound to give heed. The sooner it is organized the better. The Court will decide whether" (those before them) "are obeying the Law[?] the Lord gave him or is making one to himself and obeying that. if the latter he is worthy of death & let the Court so decide & the Lord will see to the rest.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon.

## heading: Section 99 Warning to I.[?] H.
## date: May 20 1876

Br Huffaker:

With deep desire of Soul for Your Salvation I lift up a warning voice to you from my heavenly Father that you may see your situation before it is too late & repent and call upon your God for mercy that you may escape the awful sententence[?] that the Lord has said shall come upon you, if you did not obey the law he has given you. Repent before the Lord and call for mercy that you may escape the awful doom that awaits you."

The Lord has said that if you continue to pursue the course you are now pursueing the Lord will cast you down into the eternal damnation of Hell; therefore beware what you are doing."

## signed: Phebe Rigdon
