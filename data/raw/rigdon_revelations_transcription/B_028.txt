Neither of them had ever been there, but as commanded the servant started, and though he had to travel over hundreds of miles and pass into the government of another nation he Went Straight to the place and marked the country where Zion is in the first instance to place her feet.

The whole history of this case assures the priesthoods of Zion that they are right and cannot be wrong, so they have boldness towards God in going forth to place "Zion in the situation the Lord has chosen.

No people living or who ever will live, unless they gather with the children of Zion as the Lord has appointed will do or can do any thing, but work out their condemnation, let them do as they may, from these things there is no escape, for the Lord is not to be mocked, neither will he suffer his law to be rejected with impunity.

Zion is founded before the Lord according to the counsel of his own will through the sacrifice and offerings of those whom he had called; for it was by the sacrifice of his holy priesthood, of which none knew but the priesthood itself, nor ever will know. There were and are things pertaining to the first priesthood that is not known to any but those who were appointed thereunto. Consequently there are of the priesthood of Zion that will be eternally in advance of all others because these first fruits unto God were separated from all others, and in that separate state learned the deep things of Zion, while others were mingling freely with the Gentiles, till a voice from the priesthood thus separated unto God called to them, and they heard and began to enquire after the things pertaining to Salvation.

Thus it was, and is, that Zion began to move forward and to be discovered by a few, and they who got understanding united with the first priesthood and called upon others, and so it is that Zion has appeared.
