## heading: A Patriarchal blessing by Joseph Smith senr. Kirtland Ohio march 26. 1836 for Stephen Post who was born in Greenwich Washington Co N.Y. Jan 3d A.D. 1810.

In the name of Jesus I lay my hands upon thy head and seal upon thee a fathers blessing; thou hast no father to bless thee, but art an orphan as to this thing for he hath no power to bless thee: but thou shalt be blessed with much good, and thy blessings shall descend to thy posterity even to the end of the earth. I seal a blessing also for thy companion and children, and they shall be given unto thee and thou shalt raise them up for God, and they shall receive the ministry and be a great blessing unto thee: Thou must seek to keep the commandments & the Lord will make thee equal to thy brethren in power, in strength and activity in all things; thou shalt know all things, comprehend all wisdom & thou shalt save all thy friends in time or eternity. And if thou art called away before thy Redeemer comes thou shalt continue to minister, for thou shalt not finish thy ministry upon this ball, thou shalt preach to people of other planets, and thou shalt preach to spirits in prison: be faithful before God and all power both in heaven and under heaven shall be given unto thee, thou shalt be crowned with many sheaves, thou shalt see thy Redeemer and behold the prints of the nails in his hands and feet, and thou shalt weep when thou seest him, and seek to wash his feet with thy tears, and to wipe them with the hairs of thy head like Mary: he shall lay his hands upon thee and confirm thy ordination with his own voice, and seal all power upon thy head, and bless thee with all the feelings of a father, and then thy soul shall be satisfied. These blessings thou shalt receive, if thou wilt seek with all thy heart, for I now seal them for thee, and I seal thee up to eternal life in the name of Jesus Even so. Amen."

## signed: Sylvester Smith, Scribe,
