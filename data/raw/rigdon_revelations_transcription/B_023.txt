Now I the Lord say let the authorities who hold priesthoods before me hold a council or conference as soon as it can be done, and let my servant Stephen whose surname is Post appoint the time and place for the meeting and give notice thereof to all those who are desirous of uniting in it especially all such as have been ordained or desire to be, And let all go out & travel and preach the gospel, be not in a hurry but seek out such in every place where they go as are disposed to hear, and teach such in great patience staying themselves upon the Lord, with faith and humility and let them journey on as did my disciples in days of old: preaching before they get into Canada, and after they get in saith the Lord towards the place I the Lord have appointed for the centre of the dwellings of the children of Zion; and inasmuch as you find those who incline to receive the truth stay there and build a branch of Zion. Be in no hurry to get to the place for which you have started for only by patience and long suffering can you establish my Zion saith the Lord. But those who will have the eternal glory of bringing Zion forth will never tire in the work they must regard the work as too great to make no sacrifice for it. Though life itself should fail by reason of Satans influence, it is only laid down for a little season to receive it again in greater glory than they would ever had it, had not that have taken place.

Such as feel it in their hearts to stay in Canada let them do so and preach there and those who return let them preach as they came. There is no use in having a place without people to put into it.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon.
## note: In accordance with the above revelation I S. Post appointed a conference for the 14 & 15th of Feb. at Council Bluffs & attended it. Conference were[?] very unanimous in a joint resolution to carry out the revelation.
