It is the law of our holy priesthood that no one if he has received that place, if he fails his failure is eternal; eternal condemnation takes him as its prey and he is cast down to hell to rise no more but be overwhelmed with horrors, weeping, wailing, and lamenting everlastingly.

His hope perishes at the mouth of the yawning gulf & despair which will be his forever will stare him in the face with ghastly dismay. I who speak to you am Phineas one who occupied the priesthood with Moses in the wilderness after the death of our father, for he was such to both you and me. I therefore know the character & responsibility of the calling, the solemnity of its duties, & the strictness of its laws.

As a high priest in the Zion of God you cease to be self disposing, you are the Lords with all you have. The Lord claims the entire control of all things pertaining to you as a high priest in the house of God. The Lord requires that your confidence should be placed on him and that you take him as the man of your counsel, that you will do nothing only as he directs. When you go to do any thing inquire[?] if the thing you do is in fulfilment of the duties of your priesthood, if it is not it will be a damning sin to you because it was you[?] duty to have known what is of right according to the law of the priesthood. It is an indispensable duty to yourself, to your God and to Zion that you study the law of the priesthood that you are ordained to, and act in accordance thereunto To this end it is important that the priesthood should have stated meetings for the purpose of perfecting themselves in the law and duties of their calling. At these meetings each one should come as well prepared as he can to throw in his mite to the general fund of knowledge. Leaving every thing else seek the wisdom and knowledge which pertains to the priesthood. It is for you to know so that you can teach it to others the vanity and corruption of the things of this world. To lay up treasures of the things of this world is to hoard up damnation to your own soul. Remember O Israel and escape from it as you would from a raging pestilence or a burning flame, yea more an undying vengeance.
