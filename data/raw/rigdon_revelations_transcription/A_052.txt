Let those rise in the power of their faith & purity and soar on high till their voice shall be heard in the land as the voice of heavenly messengers. But if their aspirations look at the position you have obtained there it will stop, for they will have to say in their hearts before me saith the Lord, that when the door was open for them as well as for you, they could not reach it.

But now saith the Lord I speak to my distinguished servant Sidney. Behold saith the Lord I have given you a Joseph your first born in zion but not according to the desires of Joseph in Egypt who sought to gratify his pride by glorifying the name "Joseph" But I the Lord have given him to you as your first born, but still he is of the Loins of Joseph being of the same line of priesthood with yourself, for he is of the lineage of Samuel the prophet as well as you are. I the Lord have also given each of you a female companion in the holy priesthood standing equal with you, but they are not of the same lineage of which you are, but are of the royal lineage of David & they are sisters to "Mary" She who was the mother according to the flesh of him who speaketh to you even Jesus Christ the Savior of the world. And again saith the Lord I have given you another son in the truth even Stephen who is rightly named for he is of the lineage of Stephen who was the first martyr for the truths sake, after the kingdom of heaven appeared as organized by the 12 apostles at Jerusalem. Him have I the Lord chosen to be spokesman in Zion. I the Lord do this that the name of his progenitor "Stephen" may be had in honourable remembrance in Zion forever, for he was a mighty man, not only of great faith, but was mighty in word & deed. The devil hated him with perfect hatred & never ceased till he got his blood shed & his life taken from the earth. Stephen was not a prudent man, when the Jews opposed with great violence as they often did & when he saw the hardness of their hearts & of their contempt for the word of the Lord, he would get excited and use harsh language
