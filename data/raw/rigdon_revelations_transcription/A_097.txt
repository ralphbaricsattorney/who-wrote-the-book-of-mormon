Others are appointed directly by myself without any other influence governing my action, but is done in accordance with the prayers of the one appointed. Thus it was that I the Lord appointed my servant Stephen, he came before me and offered his prayers that Zion might no longer lay in the dust, but might arise that her light might shine, and he and my servant Joseph were the two first who thus presented themselves since the reign of the powers of darkness commenced. They covenanted with me that they would obey my word in all things & not do as others had done who had gone before them and I the Lord in accordance with their own prayers appointed them to presidencies in the operative priesthood of Zion. The responsibility of doing this I the Lord took upon myself based on the confidence that they authorized by their prayers. When any of my people come in this manner before me, I the Lord hold them bound by a covenant to do whatsoever I require and when they disobey my requirements, they are guilty of a breach of their covenant for they authorize their God to have confidence in them. Under such circumstances (& speaking after the manner of men) it could not enter into my heart that my servant Stephen would betray the confidence that he had authorized the Lord his God to repose in him. I the Lord call upon my servant Stephen when he receives this my word to open his heart and present himself before the heavens alone and take his pen and write down his answer to the following questions, and when he has done it, let him report the same to Zion that I the Lord may answer him. First; After consulting his own heart before me who searches the hearts & triest the reins of all who profess my name. Where did he find his authority in my word for making the proposition he did to the Children of Zion? Let him point out the place where I the Lord caused such a thing to be recorded. Second let him ask his heart before me, Can he set up the plea of ignorance before the Lord? Third. If he can let him say how that ignorance came: Was it or was it not by treating the word of the Lord lightly? Fourth: let him say has he or has he not cherished in his heart a spirit of dictation, designing in his heart to have things concerning Zion conformed to his ideas of things? To all of these I ask my servant Stephen to write an answer before me according to the knowledge his own spirit has of his heart, for a man cannot hide himself from his own spirit no more than he can from his maker, and as his own spirit sees him I the Lord see him. Again I require of my servant Stephen that he should point out how he expected his proposition if accorded to by the Children of Zion, would assist him in executing the commission that I the Lord had given him for I gave him but one. Let him do all these things as I have required."

## signed: from S Rigdon

## note: P.S. I answered the above & no further notice was taken of the matter. S Post
