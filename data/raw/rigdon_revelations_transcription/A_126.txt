None can be of this number but those who were valiant for the truth. Paul the great apostle to the Gentiles while in the flesh strove for this glory and did not account life itself dear to him that he might gain it. To gain the glory of the association with the recording Angel, Paul knew that every thing else must give way to it, nothing admitted into competition with it.

A spokesman in the Zion of God is placed where he must obtain that position or in the end be cast down to hell. All those in former ages who sought out the knowledge of God and never suffered themselves to follow after vanities till they reached this glory fulfilled the law of the priesthood in all particulars. Peter said to the Lord that he had forsaken all and followed after Christ. At that time the catalogue of sacrifices were called into review, Father, mother "wife" & Children" and then the Lord said that such in the regeneration should sit on 12 thrones and judge the 12 tribes of Israel. "That is" be members of or fill a seat in the high court of heaven. but to reach that all things had to give way.

James in speaking of it, that is the law by which this glory is obtained did not admit of a transgression in one point, for he who did so was judged guilty of the whole. Amen

## heading: Section 75 on above
## date: Sept 15 1873

In addition to what was written in the last great communication He (the Lord) said to me that the great and grand first order of things organized after the creation of man was known and has been known ever since with the holy order whose transcript was in their dialect of heaven as the "Lambs book of life," and when the Lambs book of life is mentioned or spoken of by any of the former revelators it is this of which they speak, when it is said that their names shall be blotted from the Lambs book of life it is the record of this high holy and first order of things that is spoken of.
