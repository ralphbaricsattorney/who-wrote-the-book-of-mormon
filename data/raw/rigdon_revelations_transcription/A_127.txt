## heading: Section 77 Ans. to above To the 1st presy in Zion
## date: Sept 28th 1873

I did not seek, think of or ask for the position I occupied, and I have often reflected on the matter seeing the Lord knew all my weaknesses and want of education, why he placed me there in so responsible a position. Did he foresee that there was such a heart within me as would do his will? Myself my time and all I possess are not a sacrifice adequate for the Lord, who knows better what I can do, and should do, than I do myself. Therefore I will endeavour to obey the voice of the Lord, and fill the pattern given in the 82nd section D.C. close of the second paragraph. To observe my "covenants by sacrifice, yea every sacrifice which the Lord shall command." And this whether I act as an officer or member in the Lords Zion wherever the Lord pleases to place me.

Your son in the gospel of truth

## signed: Stephen Post

## heading: Section 78 Warning
## date: Sept 8th 1873

Behold saith the Lord to his servants Stephen & Israel. So it is then when your services are called for they come not. If such is the case so it must be. And I the Lord will give power to my servant whom you have refused to obey to direct the "recording" Angel! to call a solemn assembly of the Council on high over which he presides to lay the case before them that they may cause your names to be "blotted out". From henceforth there shall be a correspondence had between that council on high and Zion on the earth. And thus I the Lord have given power to mine servant Sidney & mine handmaiden Phebe to open and hold such a correspondence with the powers above. I the Lord introduce this order as I am prepareing to take Zion from this rebellious people to whom it had been given and give it to another people and leave them to make their own priests and prophets, while I the Lord is with my Zion elsewhere. If you desire to be transferred with Zion prepare yourselves for it by getting ready without delay notifying Zion of your intention, and go to work and prepare your houses and hearts for it. If not your names will be called up by the Holy council — for they are there written. Once blotted out and there it ends.
