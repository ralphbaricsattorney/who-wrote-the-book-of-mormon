Again saith the Lord after giving the history of the first organization until it disappeared from the earth the record gives the history of the coming forth of Zion out of the ruins of the corrupted church; giving the names through whom it came both men and women, including the 12ve through whose faith and mighty works Zion shall be pushed to her triumph. It also gives their genealogy back from parent to parent till it reaches the recorded line through which they descended from Abraham & it follows Zion as thus redeemed to her eternal glory. For the satisfaction of the Children of Zion let them know that there are of the revelations that they have received some that are taken from that record. The vision of Obadiah is of that number which is recorded on the sealed plates in full. There are also of the prophecies of the former prophets such as Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel & other prophets transferred onto the sealed plates which were connected with the plates from whence the book of Mormon was translated such as relate to the affairs of the authorities of Zion as redeemed from the corruptions of the whole church & delivered directly to the Children of Zion & none others, separating those prophecies from all others so that Zion might have her own appropriated to herself. But behold & lo! saith the Lord the rise & progress of Zion to her perfection are a practical translation of the sealed plates so that Zion as she passes along practically translates the sealed plates. Verily verily saith the Lord I have hastened to give to the Children of Zion this knowledge of the sealed plates; I the Lord could not do it before, but as soon as there was a revelator to give it, and a people to receive it, it has been given to them. Therefore saith the Lord let the Children of Zion do all things as the Lord their God requires & all knowledge & understanding shall be given to them. Amen

## signed: Sidney Rigdon.

## heading: Section 43d From Phebe the prophs[?]
## date: Oct 1868

Dear Sister:

It is the will of the Lord that the quorum of the elect sisterhood should be engaged in the work of their holy calling the Lord says that all should arise and go forth and magnify their calling as the Lord has commanded, that the work of the Lord may be accomplished & go forth into the world. I hope our sisters will not be backward in their duty. Inasmuch as they will arise & do their duty the Lord will bless them. It is your duty to see that this is carried into effect that all may be done in order.

The Lord requires that Sister Post should arise and take her place as presiding officer of the quorum. It is her duty to see that each member of the quorum is walking according to the law of her holy calling.

## signed: P Rigdon.
