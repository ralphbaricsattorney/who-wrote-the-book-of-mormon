The work that is now moving forward is in the first instance designed to prepare the way before the priesthood which the Lord has chosen to stand between himself and Zion, that his word may be given to the children of Zion as their law: And while I now write the spirit is rolling on me saying, " Say to the priesthood of Zion

Verily verily thus saith the Lord the Redeemer of Zion, and the husband of Israel. I have placed between me and Zion a priesthood who like unto me your Lord has overcome, while of the people there were none to help. His own faith brought him salvation before me saith the Lord and in accordance with the will of the heavenly hosts, I have made him a father to Zion, and in that character shall he be known and reverenced in Zion. And I the Lord made it a law in Israel that when there was found a son in Israel who refused to obey the voice of his father he should be brought into the midst of the congregation & stoned to death. In like manner I the Lord make the law of Zion that those who do not reverence the Father shall be cut off from the midst of the children of Zion, and in my due time, he shall be trampled under foot of men as the salt that has lost its savor. For I the Lord have looked on that old church and I have seen her Abominations in trampling under foot my word which I the Lord had delivered to them, and running greedily after the imaginations of their own heart, that they might perish in the gainsayings of Cora. Therefore saith the Lord let the priesthood of Zion beware how they walk before me, for I the Lord sware by myself, because there is no greater, that if they do not obey my voice in reverencing him whom I have made a father to them, I will cut them off and cast them from me. And lo, and behold! saith the Lord I will take him and give him another people who will be children unto him indeed. For it is decreed among the priesthoods above, that as Abraham was, so shall he be to Israel. For as it was through Abraham that they came into the world & obtained the promises, so shall they through Sidney come to life from the dead by reason of the fulfilment of the promises made to them through Abraham."

I have a great and glorious subject I have been seeking out before the Lord until I have reached it. It is the Gog & Magog of the prophets but I cannot write it you must wait for it till we are settled. I close by saying that the[?] spirit presses on me the eternal fact that unless all those who come from the old church and act in a priesthood before the Lord do obey him in all things whatsoever he requires he (the Lord) will cut them off & consign them to the blackness of darkness forever, for the Lord will not suffer his word to be trifled with by them any more.
