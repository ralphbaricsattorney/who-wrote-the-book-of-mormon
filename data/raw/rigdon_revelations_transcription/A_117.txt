It was at this time and place my servant Sidney presented himself before the powers above in a point of light which distinguished him from all the rest: he cast himself upon me and sought council and wisdom of me and ceased not to do so by day and night by unceasing prayer and supplications and continued so year after year till the entire house built by Joseph Smith had corrupted its way before the Lord unto destruction. I the Lord let the smiths be smitten and drove the people whom they corrupted until there was nothing but iniquity before my face, out of the land of Zion and sent them away. [Sec. 21 par: 2[?]]

The heavens looked down on the abominations they saw among those to whom the gospel of eternal life had been sent in its purity and behold all was corrupt, they saw but one exception who turned not to the right hand or the left but by prayer and supplication sought to sustain himself in righteousness before the Lord his God.

I the Lord must say that great was his conflict and mighty his struggle for he had to sustain himself though he saw the seer and revelator fall before the powers of darkness and he who had been a prophet of the living God, and revelator to himself [My servant Sidney] become an emmissary of the devil, and yet he must stand steadfast and fall not or he could never secure the eternal priesthood.

Here he had a trial peculiar to himself. None who had gone before him had a case of this kind to encounter, but he maintained himself and pursued his course of solving all the laws and principles which pertain to the organization & government of the Zion which was prepareing for the gathering of Israel. It was through such a school as this saith the Holy One of Israel that I the Lord, he[?] to whom Zion belongs brought the Spokesman of Zion in order to qualify him for the calling of the ruler of my people. And now saith the Lord the Mighty One of Jacob, there was but one man in the world who could endure the scourgeing which he has suffered without failing for I the Lord called on all nations and this one only was found. Now I the Lord say unto the Children of Zion that I gave him no more advantages than I the Lord gave to others so that he might be made manifest in the eyes of men and angels, principalities and powers, in Heaven above as well as earth beneath, that he might stand forth in bold relief in time and eternity; which he will do for the heavens have decreed it.
