Therefore I the Lord will have no such law in Zion. I will hold the right in my own hands to dispose of that priesthood.

And again let the children of Zion well consider the relation they bear to the priesthood. It is through them that the salvation of Zion comes. It is through them that deliverance comes to the house of Joseph. It is through them that the fulness of the gospel shall be preached to the ends of the earth, and Israel be gathered. For I the Lord have manifested myself to that priesthood, And I will continue to do it until Zion is filled with the light & knowledge of the Lord their God. And now I speak to the children of Zion concerning their duty to their head, It is a duty I the Lord their God require them to discharge faithfully, to relieve the temporal necessities of the priesthood through which their salvation comes. I the Lord demand this at their hands. Satan in his attempts to overthrow it, swept their property away with fury as he did Jobs. And I the Lord have seen them toiling with their hands day and night to purchase a scanty subsistence for many years, until they have passed the age of man.

And during all that time by diligence, & by prayer & supplication, seeking for[?] wisdom and knowledge by their faith before me, while there were of those who are now living & made partakers of that knowledge, enjoying abundance and bestowing upon those who love not the truth. While those through whom their salvation alone can come were toiling day after day for a mere pittance to support life, and all that time without faltering, & laying up treasures of knowledge for the very persons of whom I speak. Let the Children of Zion know that their Lord and their God can look upon this no longer without his wrath being kindled. Let the money and means of Zion be used for the benefit of Zion, and not wasted on her enemies. I your Redeemer know the hearts of all living and I know what the Children of Zion can do & what they cannot do, and by that knowledge I will judge them. I do not ask the children of Zion to make their priesthood rich for that their hearts do not desire before me. But in this it shall be done as I the Lord will concerning them. It is wisdom in me that the Children of Zion should know how it was the first church fell under condemnation through Satans attempt to destroy the priesthood chosen with which to turn away ungodliness from Jacob; and this he did through the corruptions of woman kindling her envy into fury she threw her spirit
