He occupies his present position before me not by reason of appt. or designation by me, but by achievement. Though saith the Lord I designated him from the beginning as the one who would hold the office he now sustains before the Lord his God but that designation did not secure him the priesthood he now holds. nor could it have done it. By achievement he holds it, such has been his triumph over the powers of darkness in bringing to light the word of the Lord, so as to lay the foundation of Zion, that he would have attained the priesthood had he never been appointed or designated thereto. His designation to the office he holds was by reason of the foreknowledge of the appointing power. It is not possible saith the Lord that another living could hold the priesthood he holds. None other living was ever ordained to it, and none but himself has sought and obtained the knowledge before me saith the Lord whereby they could be qualified to discharge the duties pertaining to that high and holy calling.

I the Lord say this that the Children of Zion need not be imposed on by false pretenders, as I never appointed to my servant the right of appointing and ordaining another; neither have I the Lord said I would ever do so. It is the priesthood through whose guidance and direction Israel is to be gathered, and it is the priesthood through whose ministration the book of Mormon will be taken to the Lamanites, and all attempts by any others to do that will prove a failure.

Let the Children of Zion, for they are made judges of such things, look at the vain and wicked attempts to make prophets and priesthoods of their own by wicked and foolish men as if the Lord would ever give such power to take the book of Mormon to the Lamanites or to gather Israel. All such attempts will work out the damnation of those engaged in it, and this is what it will do.

And now behold saith the Lord for I speak to all who are Children of Zion or will hereafter become so, the time is drawing near when the arm of the Lord is to be made bare in the sight of the nations that he may do his work his strange work & bring to pass his act his strange act, to which end the day of the Lords power must come as spoken of by all the prophets since the world began. For all true prophets have spoken of those days which are coming on the world.
