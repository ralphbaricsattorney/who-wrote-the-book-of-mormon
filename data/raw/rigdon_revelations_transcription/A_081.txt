you fail in all things pertaining to your high & holy calling for unless in all these things you are one, you are nothing.

Behold the Lord your God giveth you this warning and unless you lay it to heart curseings[?] & not blessings await you for I the Lord will not be treated with neglect by you I am angry with you for not obeying my requirements with strictness & have not walked according to the law under which you were organized. Now I the Lord require of each of you individually that you neither say nor do any thing but what will tend to the maintainance of that perfect unity under which the quorum was organized. For behold saith the Lord your trangressions[?] of that law shall be marked against the one committing them, for I the Lord will both see & hear you do it, & wo be to you if you are found transgressors of that law of union, for there is no necessity of your doing so and should you be found transgressors before me I will enter into judgment against you, and I will hurl you down in my wrath and trample on you in my fury saith the Lord. When you pray I will not hear you. When you stretch forth your hand towards me, I will spurn you from me, no other person shall pray for you, you shall be unto me as the salt that lost its savor. I the Lord will laugh at your calamity, and mock when your fear cometh I will call on other authorities to do my work and I will make you a hissing and mock among your brethren, for I the Lord your God will not be treated lightly by you. That Zion may be built up it is required of such as you that your walk before the Lord your God shall be perfect.

Know ye then O ye presidents of my people saith the Lord your God that it will be required at your hand if Zion should get into confusion & her progress be stopped for it cannot so happen if you perfectly obey the law of unity as required of you.

And again saith the Lord I have not heard your prayers coming up into mine ears to give you wisdom and power to enable you to preserve yourselves in perfect unity of the spirit and I the Lord require of each of you that you enquire of the spirit which is in you have your prayers come up before the Lord at all times or have they not. This question each one answer to the others at your solemn meetings! "Amen."
