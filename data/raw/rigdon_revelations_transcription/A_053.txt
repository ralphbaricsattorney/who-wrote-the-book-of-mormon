And would cast upon them severe censures. He being spokesman for the apostles though not one of them, The apostles admonished him not to use language calculated to arouse the indignation of the Jews lest it might bring not only violent persecution on himself but on themselves also. But when he got excited he would not refrain. It was this trait in his character of which the devil took advantage being determined to take his life, for he feared his influence. He was enabled by the rashness of Stephen to stir up the Jews to furious rage against him until in their fury they stoned him to death. And thus it was saith the Lord that the first great persecution against the christians was hastened by the imprudence of one man for after the martyrdom of Stephen the Jews fell upon the saints in great wrath & fury & they had to flee before them in all directions to save their lives.

I the Lord say therefore to my servant Stephen and through him to all others; beware of imprudences, but bear the contradiction of sinners against yourself with patience and long suffering for the devil has great power over the hearts of men and greatly does he desire to get advantage over you. Therefore reason with mildness, and with soft words guard against the wrath of men. Bear their abuse as becometh a saint trusting in God. Now I the Lord give unto you the celestial law for by this you have to be governed. You have read in your books that it was there written that the time was coming that "the will of God should be done on earth as it was done in heaven". And as there were none on the earth but yourselves through whom the cause of Zion could be moved, and there were none through whom the work could begin to prepare the way for the fulfilment of the covenant made with the house of Israel. In like manner there are none through whom this work can begin but you. Therefore give heed to my word. Among you saith the Lord contention & confusion cannot dwell, the voice of strife can have no place, neither can disagreement exist. all you do and all you say must be done as with the heart and spirit of one what one says you must all say. What one does you must all do. I the Lord say this pertaining to the matters concerning the moving the cause of Zion. With you your yea & nay must be yea & nay only so perfectly must you secure each others confidence that nothing else will be required for confirmation but your yea and your nay for whatsoever more than this is required it cometh of evil & should it be so sin will have gotten into the seat of holiness in Zion: for I the Lord your God have appointed unto you that you shall perfect holiness before me.
