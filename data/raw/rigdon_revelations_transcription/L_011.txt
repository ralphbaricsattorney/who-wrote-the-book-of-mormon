him shall never inherit it I say to my servant[?] Israel as I say to all I the Lord your God require all you have. and are[?] to be used for the building of Zion There are those living through whose sacrifice of all they had brought the gospel out of darkness and it was and is proclaimed to you And had they not have done so you and your family would have been cast down to hell, and were it not now for their exertions you never can enter the "rest of God" Therefore seeing salvation has come to you and yours through their sacrifice— and such a sacrifice none living but themselves know— I the Lord require that you shall see that they are crowned with blessing at your hand, and that not sparingly but bountifully

Know O all ye children of Zion that to the heavens it is well known what you are doing and what you can do and I the Lord will have no excuses, I require obedience at your hand and if I the Lord cannot have this Behold and Lo! and fear and tremble! for I will take the priesthood away from you, and consign you to perish and be damned with the gentiles

Let every soul then who has received a priesthood according to the law of Zion arise trim their lamps and commence immediately to prepare to thrust in the sickle and reap lest judgment[?] overtakes you at an hour when you think not Amen
## signed: Sidney Rigdon
