Priesthood which was Separated from all the devices of the Devil, and Set free intirely[?] from his dominion, Zion Must Remain without power to Move,— And now Saith the Lord, out of all those who were Called from Among the Nations, One only was found who Could accomplish the work of Separation,— But no sooner was this done, than the fury of Satan with all his Host were let loose on him in fury, and have followed him till this day, He was marked Every where for a victim, all Opposers And Gainsayers Saught to distroy him, they followed him with Slanders,— They invented the most Shameless Calumnies that Corruption Could devise, and Ceased not until I the Lord took him into Exile, and there alone I Called upon him to perfect the work of Separation, which only Could be done by becoming acquainted with the true Character of Zion, as well as that of the Church of the Devil,— All of which Required a full understanding of all the Revelations I the Lord had Given him for the purpose of laying the foundation of Zion on the Basis of Eternal truth, which Required a long period of many years,

When the Prophet I the Lord had raised up to bring forth the Revelations Necessary to be had in order that my Servant Might perfect the work of his Calling, Got lifted up in the pride of his heart, and had identified himself with the Church of the Devil, instead of following on to perfect the work of Separation. He Bent his whole fury on my Servant, with all the influence that the position He had obtained Gave him,— He aroused Every feeling of predjudice against my Servant that was possible for him to do, No falsehood was too Corrupt for him to fabricate, and Even to Swear to in order to Effect his object,— Behold Saith the Lord, let the Children of Zion understand, that I the Lord have told them these things, that they Might understand the things pertaining to Zion, as they are before My face, that they may see that their Salvation, and the Salvation of all others, depends on the faithfulness, integrity, and determination of one man, Separated from the whole world, and all He had offered on the alter of Sacrifice, until He Stood alone intirely[?] Separated from the Church of the Devil,— and thus was his situation when I the Lord Called him to begin to move the Cause of Zion,— And thus it was that I the Lord Separated the Priesthood unto myself that Stands at the Head of Zion, and I the Lord say unto the Children of Zion, that there is no other man living Standing in this point of light before me,— And again I say to the Children of Zion, that not only does the Salvation of all who will be saved, depend on him, But all those who will be damned, will be damned at his Expence,— And verily the Children of Zion do see that when any turn away from the truth, they Excuse themselves by prefering Complaints against him,— And so it is that Salvation and damnation are with him,— This peculiar Situation belongs to him, and to him only on the Earth, the Condition of things with which He is thus Surrounded, was Created Because He was Separated intirely from the Church of the Devil, And become the Head of the Church of the Lamb of God,— By Reason of this fact, and this only, He has become the object of the Devils hatred, and He Stirs up Every Soul over whom he has influence against him,— Furthermore, that I the Lord may Show unto the Children of Zion the peculiar Condition under which He Exist, and has to Exist,— The Condition of things as here described does not belong to him only individually Considered, But they attach to his Companion also,—

All this the Devil through his Emisaries[?] falsely and Cruelly did,— the Devil and his Children here saught through these means to turn him away from the faith, But it belonged to him to Sustain the faith, or die in attempting to do so, and all the Children of Zion are witnesses of his triumph,—

— And now Saith the Lord, inasmuch as He and his family have been Marked out as victims, and the Devil because of his regard for the Cause of Righteousness, has Subjected them to all manner of abuse far beyond others,— It is for the Lord himself who Knew all these things would transpire from the begining, to manifest the things which were and are had in the Eternal world in Relation thereto,— Therefore let all the Children of Zion Know assuredly, and let them ponder it in their hearts, the Great Mystery which I the Lord now Reveal to them, Behold Saith the Lord, the principalities above Established a Law, and also Entered into a Covenant, in due time to be fulfilled on the Head
