## heading: Section 35th
## date: June 1st 1868

The word of the Lord to his hand maiden Sarah Newton.

Thus saith the Lord to his hand maiden Sarah. I the Lord have raised you up on high from among the daughters of my people, and lifted you on high that through you my power might be made known, & having appointed you a judge to separate unto myself through the election of grace a priesthood to bring unto me my Zion out of the midst of Babylon.

I the Lord now require of you that you should separate unto me out of the daughters of my people an elect sisterhood one out of each branch of Zion whereever there is a branch organized. Those who are distinguished for their love of the truth, and their devotedness to the cause of righteousness to be set apart to minister before the Lord in the holy things pertaining to Zion. Through your faith and obedience you shall have power to do this great thing And after you have got them distinguished and chosen, I the Lord appoint unto you the 4th day of Jan. 1869 to call them to Attica to a holy convocation where they shall be set apart by the presidency by an ordination to the office of an elder, and to assist in presiding over the branch in which they preside. It shall be your duty to have all the communications you have received and shall hereafter receive from mine handmaiden Phebe copied and sent to this elect sisterhood a copy to each one and it shall be their duty to see that the same are taught in the branches over which they preside. For I the Lord your God will not, suffer that the children of Zion shall be babblers, prating about things that they do not understand & wasting their time in vain and foolish conversation that does not minister grace to those who hear, but all shall have understanding in the things of the kingdom knowing what the will of the Lord concerning them is. In accomplishing this work you can call upon those who are spiritual to assist until the work is accomplished."

## signed: S Rigdon

## heading: Section 36th
## date: June 1st

The word of the Lord to the presidency of his church.

It is required of them that they take the book of doctrine & covenats & search it diligently so as to understand the duties of the quorum of the 12ve as they are there written & let them see that their reading is an exhaustive one, that every thing there written shall be brought out even to a minutia down to the thoughts of the heart where that is written so that they, the 12ve may understand their duty as well as know what manner of men they must be saith the Lord your God.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon
