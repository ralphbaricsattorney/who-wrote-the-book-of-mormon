## heading: Section 18th
## date: Sept 1st 1864

Br. Post: I send you this communication to give you the law pertaining to your mission (all the power I have in the premises is, to let you know what the will of the Lord is.) In founding Zion we all of the priesthood have one common law which is to govern us under all circumstances. That law is the law of the widow & the unjust judge. Zion can alone come forth through the obedience of the priesthood to that law. Every other means tried will fail; but under the rule of that law it will be established. This law knows no failure. It is the law given to insure success to those who obey it. You are appointed the head of a great mission even that of prepareing the way for the settlement of the priesthood of Zion. The Father & the Father of fathers will take nothing from your hand short of accomplishing the whole work for which you were sent. Should you fail it would be fatal to you. Do that thing you must. Should br. Stanley go with you, it will in no degree lessen your obligations. You are the head of the mission and the Lord will hold you responsible for the faithful discharge of its duties. It matters not what discouragements or opposition you may find in the way remember you have got to overcome them as the widow before the unjust judge, importune, and continue to importune until you prevail. Thus stands your relation to the father and priesthood. Zion can only be established by these means, for should you fail (a something not to be admitted, only to show the sacredness of your mission) another would have to take your place and do the same work by the same law, for it the only law given, or that will be given. Under this law Zion has to be established by the priesthood. Should br Stanley go with you, and after getting there his faith fail, it would not release you or if you should fail, it would not release him, he would have to do it or else lose his priesthood. By this you can see the sacredness of the mission.

To fulfil the mission, you must provide for my own use a comfortable house and a place where I can attend to the duties of my calling. The Lord would frown on me should I attempt to do such a thing myself, and he would shake the operative priesthood to the centre, if not to hurl them down to stand before him no more forever, should they put me to such necessity: for it is required of me to stay where I am till all things are prepared before me, and here I must and will stay till that is done, & you are the head of the mission through which it is to be done. In accomplishing this work you have the benefit of the authority of your appointment. As head of the mission you can call upon all those who have come forth and are connecting themselves with Zion, wherever they are scattered over the country to assist you.
