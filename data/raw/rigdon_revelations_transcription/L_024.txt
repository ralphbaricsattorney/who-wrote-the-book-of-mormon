## note: a rapid, rounder, upright hand, not the hand of the bound volumes; it appears to be the same hand as L_015-L_023 writing more hastily; no left margin with labels

The voice of the spirit to H H Deam in the field at Zarahemla Fayette Co Wis June 6th[?] 1854 in ans. to prayer Thus saith the spirit the work has been procrastinated arise out of obscurity and put on strength cleanse ye yourselves from all uncleanliness it must needs be by the waters of separation (baptism) that ye may cleanse your Fathers, but first set the priesthood in order, then redeem the dead that ye may be saviors in very deed even so Amen.

Again the voice of the spirit came to me June 12[?] 1854 in ans. to these words O Lord what dost thou mean why is it necessary to be baptized. Thus saith the Lord my people at Nauvoo was rejected of me as a church, for the transgression of my law, their bodies are defiled their priesthood prostituted their ordinances not accepted of me but I have reserved a scattered remnant who have not yielded to the temptation of the doctrines of Baal it is their right and my will saith God ye organize the church according to the order of the Holy priesthood called the organization of the remnant of the church of Christ organized on the 6th day of April 1830[?]

## heading: Zarahemla Oct 7th 1854 Rev to H. H. Deam

With my face prostrate to the earth the word of the Lord came to me concerning his people at Zarahemla

Thus saith the Lord in answer to your prayer which was pleasing to me, ye have lacked wisdom and relyd upon my promises, I am the same God I have not changed my promises are yea & Amen, I have spoken to this people many times but they have not performed saith God even Jesus Christ but there are a few in this place who have done my will and obeyed me nevertheless I have somewhat against them they have suffered the tempter to enter which has been the cause of darkness and unbelief, Know ye that Satan desires to sift you as wheat.
