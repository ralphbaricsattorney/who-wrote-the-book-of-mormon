The day is drawing near saith your God when the prepareing to fulfil the covenant to the house of Israel must commence. It is to begin this that all who believe are called on to organize that they may learn their duty perfectly in order that they may be the honoured instruments to do that work. Since the days of old the period now dawning upon you has been looked on as the day of the Lords power when he should shew signs in the heavens above and on the earth beneath; and when he the Lord should shew himself the God of Israel in deed and truth: And inasmuch as it is the day of the Lords power, it must be the day for mans achievement.

Behold saith the Lord there is no limits to my power wherewith I do my work, neither shall there be any limits to the power of the Children of Zion, for it is through them I the Lord will bring to pass my work. But the Children of Zion must secure unto themselves this power by achievement, they must not only say Lord, Lord, but they must do the will of the Lord who has called them to achievement and glory. And if the Children of Zion will do this in deed & in truth, the power of the Lord their God shall be with them and as is said in the book of Mormon that in doing that, in doing the work whereunto the Children of Zion are now called, that the servants worked with their might, and the Lord worked with them. (Zenos) and the whole work was accomplished; but to do this the people of God had to work with their might. Verily verily saith the Lord it was ordained from the beginning that Israel should be gathered according to the ruling of the book of Mormon. The teachings of that book must be understood and practiced by all those who will be preserved to participate in that most glorious work. It is appointed of the Lord your God that those who receive the teachings of the book of Mormon in their hearts & obey them in their lives shall be the only ones who shall be chosen to receive power for the great work which is to be accomplished by the Children of Zion. All others will be cut off let their pretentions be what they may. For this purpose saith the Lord I gave power to mine handmaiden Phebe whom I the Lord call the prophetess of Zion, and I say of her as I did of John the baptist that she is more than a prophetess, for she is the beloved of the Lord, and none other who stands before me is like unto her to expound the book of Mormon and apply its teachings to those to whom they belong so that the priesthoods of Zion might understand their duty and thereby qualify themselves for their official duties.
