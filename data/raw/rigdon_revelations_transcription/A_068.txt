## heading: Section 39th Newton to stand alone
## date: Aug 11th 1868

To the first presidency of Zion:

I the Lord say unto them that he who was my servant Joseph H. Newton has sinned in my sight; he has taken power to himself without consulting his brethren to teach what I the Lord had not authorized him to do, and has in this thing sinned grieviously against his brethren in my sight saith the Lord. And I the Lord have given him time to repent, but he has not repented. In doing as he did he gave the devil power over him so that his mind is estranged from the path of his duty, that he cannot act in righteousness before me saith the Lord in his holy calling which requires the whole heart to be in subjection to the will of the Lord.

Therefore I the Lord say unto you, that you shall not suffer him to be with you in your councils neither have any part with you in conducting the affairs of Zion but you shall separate him from you and he shall stand alone before the Lord his God, and before him shall he humble himself, and purge his soul from the pride of his heart, that he may learn to walk before the Lord with fear & trembling, for it is I saith the Lord who searcheth the hearts of the Children of Zion. And I the Lord say unto you of the first presidency of Zion that he shall stand separated from you until he so approves himself unto you that you will seek unto me through my servant Sidney to have him restored. And should he not have power to do that he must remain without the camp (or fold of Zion) I the Lord give you this not only as law in this case, but as the law by which the first presidency shall be dealt with before me saith the Lord.

And now saith the Lord to the authorities of his[?], let not this interfere with the progress of Zion, but let mine handmaiden Sarah haste the organization of the female quorums and let my servant Stephen see to it that all the authorities are organized without delay & let them see to it that there be no Idlers in Zion, but let all stand in their place & let Zion move forward. Let my servant Israel Huffaker be consecrated & ordained and let him take his place in the first presidency with as little delay as possible for his counsel & service are now needed.

And again let my servant Stephen take charge of the records of the church that are in the hands of him who was my servant Joseph H. Newton and see that all things are kept and recorded that are necessary to be recorded. Let the first presidency use all due diligence in doing my will saith the Lord your God. Amen.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon.

## note: P.S. ext

Fain fain would we have escaped this terrible thing —

Stephen & Sarah will you stand like those who fear God & not bring our gray hairs with sorrow down to the grave it is for you in this the time of our greatest trouble to stand like pillars in the kingdom of our God.

## signed: S.R.
