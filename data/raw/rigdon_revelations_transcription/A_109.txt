You know how you reached your present standing before the Lord by making one step in advance of the whole world — for such you have done — you have not only understood how to proclaim the fulness of the gospel and settle the question of the rights of priesthood, so that you can try those who say they are of the priesthood and are not and shew them to be liars. But you know what none others do know how to teach the righteousness of the kingdom. For the lack of this knowledge all who have gone before you have fallen, for without this knowledge they were blind leaders of the blind and all fell into the ditch together. Your way then is clear and there can be no darkness with you, let others be as they may. I the Lord your God say go on unto perfection in the course you are pursueing for by means of that you have presented Zion before the heavens for its blessing, which it shall have if its people fail not.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon, Phebe Rigdon

## heading: Section 65
## date: Feb 5th 1872

Verily verily saith the Lord to his servant Israel Huffaker, and his family whom I love saith the Lord and whose salvation I desire. Say to him in my name saith the Lord, make sale of all the property you can not take with you and pass over the Mississippi as early as you can, and make no delay that is unavoidable. For behold and tremble all ye rebels and sinners in this proud United States, for my sword is bathed in heaven for your destruction and everlasting overthrow and none can save. You have despised my offers of mercy, have trampled under foot my saints, and hissed with contempt at my word, and now destruction shall come on you as a whirlwind, and my fury as a raging tempest, and none shall deliver! blood shall follow you until you are utterly wasted.

Therefore my servant Israel save your family from destruction, and your house from desolation, Amen.
## signed: Sidney Rigdon
