It is wisdom in me that the children of Zion should know how it was the first church fell under condemnation through Satans attempt to distroy the priesthood chosen with which to turn away ungodliness from Jacob, and this he did through the corruptions of woman Kindling her envy into fury, she threw her spirit into her husband, and in this way it spread through the whole church, A spirit of Malice, of slander, of lying, yea, and of blood, I the Lord seeing this removed the priesthood out of its place, and I make it a Law in Zion that none of their decendents shall be admitted into Zion, unless they come confessing the sins of their Parents, for the same spirit dwells in the children that Satan put into the hearts of the parents,– Satan understands the Law by which the Heavens are governed, that those whose faith cannot be shaken but remain[?] steadfast cannot be overthrown for I their Redeemer is Pledged to deliver, though the mountains should melt with Blood, and the earth and Heavens be shaken, still they must and shall be saved, It was this knowledge that made him labour until he got the Priesthood at the Head of the church into his power, entertaining no doubt but that the other priesthood, faith would not stand when they saw the Prophet and his companion at the head of the church, and the one through whom the Revelations came, filled with lying, and slander, Malice, and fiendish outrage, but in this he was mistaken, as in the case of Job, And now I the Lord Jehovah will raise this priesthood on high, It shall be exalted, and be very high, It shall deal prudently, Kings shall shut their mouths at its presence, and consider what they have not seen, nor heard before–

Therefore let Zion take heed how they deal with it, for I their Redeemer and their husband have decreed that they shall not be tormented with the foolishness of ignorant and unlearned men.– Amen,

Again I say unto you my servent Abraham that after you recieve this, you will with as little delay as possible, take it to my servent Joseph H, Newton, and let him and the brethren with him sit in council with you, and take such measures in relation to it, as the spirit may direct, and you shall leave it with him, for I have appointed him to preserve the revelations I give, and let not the world see it, till you are directed to do so,–

Furthermore, It is my will that my servent Samuel M, Reeves should magnify his calling, in bearing testimony, whereever an oppertunity offers among his former brethren, and he should seek opportunities[?] to do so, and be faithful, and be prayerful before me,– Let the children of Zion labour with all their might, that I their King And their Ruler may labour with them, And let my servent Joseph H, Newton preside in your councils for I have appointed him a presiding officer in Zion, and to stand on the right hand of my servent Sidney, but let him beware lest he be lifted up, for satan if he does will take advantage of him and torment his soul.
