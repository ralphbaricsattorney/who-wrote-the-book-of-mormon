And again verily saith the Lord the vision of John as above written was originally[?] in it as John wrote it, but wicked men who used the scriptures for carnal and selfish purposes took it out thinking it would not be believed & that thereby their craft would be injured.

And I the Lord now say unto the daughters of Zion lift up your heads and rejoice and let your sorrow be turned into joy, for behold your deliverance draweth near and the times of your subjection swiftly passeth away.

But I the Lord now speak to my servant Israel the bishop of Zion for he must needs know his duty and obey it, for it belongeth to such not only to do the requirements of the Lord pertaining to themselves but to see that others also submit to the will of God & neglect not the duties of their respective callings. Behold saith the Lord to his servant Israel for I the Lord speak to you — ponder deeply in your heart, that you were called out of the thick darkness of Gentile corruption where you were buried under the rubbish of Babylonish ignorance & abomination & lost to yourself and all others forever, & had not a certain man who came from the loins of another tribe through whom you were called from darkness & obscurity into the glorious light of heaven, & into possession of the high & holy priesthood with the authority of which you are clothed you would have remained forever not knowing who you were nor from whom you had descended. I the Lord call your attention to the way by which this man had power before the heavens to have you separated from all others unto the work of the ministry whereunto you are called. Behold & lo! my servant Israel it was by reason of his faithfulness, diligence, patience in affliction, humility of soul before the Lord & unceasing exertions in studying the word of eternal truth, not regarding the riches and honors of this world but offering them all in sacrifice until in his old days he has nothing of this worlds goods, left nor has he a shelter he can call his own. He thus contended with the powers of darkness alone until he overcome & tore Zion out from under the power of the devil & then called upon the world to come and lay hold on eternal life.
