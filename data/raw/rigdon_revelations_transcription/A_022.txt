## heading: Section 14th.
## date: Sept 1864

Br Newton: I take this from your letter of the 18th inst to me I do not think I could go and leave him (Reeves) behind, & yet I should have to take or send all I have which cannot be a great deal to assist Br Post in making the purchase building houses &c. "In relation to the above the Lord by his spirit says to me" 'Say to my servant Joseph verily, verily saith the Lord by the spirit there is no duty required of you in relation to the work assigned to my servant Stephen except your prayers. I the Lord have chosen two presiding priesthoods whose duty before me is to attend faithfully to the respective requirements belonging to their callings. — To my servant Stephen I the Lord have required the duty of prepareing the way for the settlement of the priesthood in the place appointed, and it is not in accordance with my will saith the Lord that you my servant Joseph should leave the duties of your calling by interfearing with the duties belonging to his distinctively, you have been appointed to a presidency pertaining to the revelations. It is the business of your presidency to see that the fountain of revelation is not dried up for should that be done, it will be vain to build houses or settle priesthoods. Zion has come into existence by the revelations I the Lord have given, and it can only continue in the same way. Let the revelations cease and there is an end to Zion, & nothing left but a fearful looking for of judgment to come, & fiery indignation, that shall consume the Gentiles. Of necessity therefore your presidency stands first to see that Zion moves on by keeping the fountain of revelation open continually.

Should there be a failure in building houses or other things pertaining to the duties of the presidencies which I the Lord have chosen. Still Zion will move onward if the fountain of revelation is open but if that ceases Lo, and behold! Zion goes to another people, and Wo! wo! to the Gentiles. The Lord will receive no excuses from these presidencies, it matters not how plausibly framed: The fulfillment of the duties assigned, only will I the Lord accept. They have been chosen to high callings, and their respective duties must be discharged, in order that they may prove themselves sufficient for the duties of their stations. It is my will saith the Lord if they approve themselves worthy to give them a place in the first presidency of Zion, and among her children, but for this to be done they must prove themselves[?] to me saith the Lord in the place I have appointed them. Let them then each one see to the duties of their calling saith the Lord. Amen

## signed: Sidney Rigdon
