## heading: Section 7th.
## date: 1864 Jun. 26th.

The word of the Lord to Zion and her children.

Thus saith the Lord by the spirit of holiness, the time has come for the Children of Zion to begin to prepare to escape from the desolations of Babylon which is about to fall on the heads of those who dwell in the eastern lands. For I the Lord have decreed a consumption on all the region of this country laying between what you call the Atlantic on the east and the Mississippi on the west. And between the gulf of Mexico on the South and the great lakes on the North as it is named among you. Hear O ye Children of Zion; and give ear you who fear my name, for I the Lord have stretched out my hand against it and there are none who can turn it back, for the whole land shall be utterly laid waste: their cities towns and villages shall be left without inhabitant, and no flesh living shall be spared in this the day of my wrath; for my anger is kindled against them, and my fury shall go forth among them, & they shall be unto me like Sodom, and before my face as Gomorrah, for they shall utterly perish and their land be a desolation perpetually to them; and their generations shall cease to exist on the land of the living. Their rich and fruitful fields, which have caused them to abound in the abundance of the riches of the earth, shall be a waste without one to cultivate them; for the abundance of the things of the earth which were bestowed on them with a lavish hand, they have used to corrupt their way and debauch their souls before me saith the Lord.
