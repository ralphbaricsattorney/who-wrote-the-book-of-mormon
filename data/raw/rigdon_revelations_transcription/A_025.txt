Therefore when I the Lord say that your house is holy, the meaning is that you have been purified and stand before the councils of heaven as did your first parents in the day in which they were created before they sinned, being formed according to the pattern given at the creation. Therefore I the Lord say unto those whom I have purified, walk before me and be ye perfect, holding on steadfastly to the rod of iron, that through you Zion may awake and shake herself from the dust, and come forth in deed and in truth, and in power, and in great glory. And again I the Lord say unto you by this you may know that a house is holy before me, when my word is the rule of that house and where it is the basis of the faith and practice of those who dwell therein, I the Lord say of such house that it is holy. In this the children of Zion are distinguished from all others. And furthermore, I the Lord say unto mine handmaiden Sarah rise up and stand before me in your high calling, and when there is put into your heart a voice of warning to any of the priesthoods of Zion, or a voice of blessing, give forth that voice, and fail not, and if the one concerning whom it is given be away from you, then write the warning or blessing and send it to them. For thus I the Lord have ordained to bring forth Zion in holiness. before me saith the Lord. By this I the Lord give the pattern according to which the priesthoods of the council of the Ancient of days, or the ancient order of things, or the Adamitic order must be organized: For at the beginning there was a man and a woman. Verily saith the Lord this order is in accordance with the faith and prayers of the first man & first woman, and being the order of the beginning it must also be the order of the restitution, for it is through this order that cometh the restitution of all things. It is saith the Lord your Redeemer the order of Zion. It is through this order that cometh the salvation of Zion and of the Gentiles as many as will believe, and of the house of Joseph or the Lamanites, and lo & behold! of the whole house of Israel, yea of every son and daughter of Adam that will bow to the requirements of heaven. Therefore the first becomes last also.
