## heading: Section 96th
## date: 1875 Nov. 1

To the spokesman in Zion:

It seemeth unto me the Lord your God that your whole heart and soul should be alive to the great things which are prepareing to roll on to Zion. For from the beginning the days of Zion were to be the days of wonder: But those days could not come till I the Lord had prepared a priesthood to Lead Zion on to her triumph, a priesthood such as had not been seen among men; and that priesthood could not be made perfect only through sufferings such as none others knew nothing about. It took sufferings to perfect the Lord himself though he was a son.

In order to do this the priesthood now being perfected had to pass through trials such as none others could or would endure. For I the Lord had decreed from the beginning to put the whole world to the test to shew who it was that could stand the fiery trial and come out redeemed, having learned more of men & things and of the Lords manner of teaching the world than any other living. And those and those only who through severe chastisement and scourging would hold the faith steadfast without wavering to the end, and thus prove themselves worthy of the priesthood about to be conferred before the Angels of the pavillion, or seat of glory of the redeemed spirits in paradise.

I the Lord had previously declared the authority through and by whom Zion should be redeemed and Israel gathered; after all others had failed, "One" of my servants should be called on to go forth and redeem Zion. In regard to this notification and this servant I the Lord had designated him (sec 98 par. 7th D.C.[?]) And by two of those who had the patriarchate I the Lord had declared whom it was and how he should be qualified to do the work of his priesthood. Through the first patriarch of the church I the Lord said to my servant Sidney Rigdon that he should renew his age and become a young man. This patriarch was Joseph Smith Sen. then Several Years afterwards I the Lord renewed this promise to my servant "Sidney Rigdon" by my servant Carvil Rigdon another patriarch.
