To my servents[?] Stephen and Israel Behold saith the Lord I require that all those who have been ordained to holy offices in my Zion will arise and slumber no more but go forth in my name and preach my gospel where ever there is a door opened and I the Lord say that they shall seek diligently to obtain open doors where they can preach my gospel and if they do this faithfully I the Lord insure to them a reward, but if they do not this I will afflict them and scourge them untill they shall be laid waste and shall perish from the land of the living; for they will learn and shall know that the pestilence is in my hand and I will send it abroad for their distruction.
