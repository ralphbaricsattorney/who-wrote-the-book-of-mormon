## heading: Section 40th Newton's restoration
## date: Aug. 31[?] 1868

To the first presidency of Zion:

Behold saith the Lord by reason of a petition from mine handmaiden Sarah sent to my servant Sidney desiring him to seek mercies for Joseph H Newton who had fallen by transgression before me saith the Lord. And my servant Sidney having presented the sincere desire of his whole heart for the restoration of Joseph H. Newton to his former standing before me & before Zion. I the Lord feel bound to grant him his request. For behold & lo! saith the Lord there is no other person living who can bring his or her whole heart into my presence as he can saith the Lord. and when he does do it I the Lord will grant him his desire and more especially in this case for I the Lord did in consequence of Joseph H. Newton's having written to my servant an insolent & abusive letter which contained damning lies before me. That I the Lord ordered him to be expelled from Zion. He did this as Adam in the beginning did concerning Eve. he tried to put his sin on to her. So Joseph H Newton sought to put his sin on the head of my servant Sidney and for this wicked attempt I the Lord ordered him to be cast out of Zion. My servant Sidney knew this before he came before me to obtain mercies for him, and he who was attempted to be injured came before me to seek mercies for the one who was injuring him. And who am I saith the Lord, that I would not grant to him the desires of his heart. Therefore saith the Lord your God; yea, your Redeemer the Head of Zion, on whose shoulders the government rests let it be again My "Servant" Joseph; and let him take his place without delay and act efficiently before me saith the Lord, and let his transgressions cease forever. For I the Lord will not always bear with transgressers[?]. Let the Lord his God hear the ejaculations of his heart by day & by night ascending up on high that he may be protected against the wickedness and vanity of his heart, that Satan may not get advantage of him always and it shall yet go well with him.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon.

## heading: Section 41st On precy meetings

"Verily verily saith the Lord It is I who have made man and I know what is in man, and when man was made there was a spirit placed in him which had power to search out & understand all things that were in him. From this spirit that I the Lord had placed in man, it was not possible that man could hide himself. Every imagination of his heart & thought of his soul lay open to its search. And verily saith the Lord as this spirit that is placed in man knows him so do I the Lord know him Therefore saith the Lord there are evils before mine eyes in the hearts of those whom I have appointed to preside over my church & kingdom, evils which must be purged out or else judgement will of necessity overtake them.
