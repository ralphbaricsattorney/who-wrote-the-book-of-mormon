But no sooner was this done than the fury of satan with all his host were let loose on him in fury and have followed him till this day. He had however the glorious satisfaction of his wife uniting with him. He was marked every where for a victim all opposers & gainsayers sought to destroy him they followed him with slanders, They invented the most shameless calumnies that corruption could devise and ceased not until I the Lord took him into exile and there alone I called upon him to perfect the work of separation which only could be done by becoming acquainted with the true character of Zion as well as that of the church of the devil all of which required a full understanding of all the revelations I the Lord had given him for the purpose of laying the foundation of Zion on the basis of eternal truth which required a long period of many years.

When the first prophet & seer I the Lord had raised up to bring forth the revelations necessary to be had in order that my servant Sidney might perfect the work of his calling, got lifted up in the pride of his heart and had identified himself with the church of the Devil instead of following on to perfect the work of separation, he bent his whole fury on my Servant Sidney with all the influence that the position he had attained gave him. He aroused every feeling of prejudice against my servant Sidney that was possible for him to do. No falsehood was too corrupt for him to fabricate and even to swear to in order to effect his object. The church of the Devil also marshalled all their forces and in order to invalidate the book of Mormon said that my servant Sidney had made it. And they are to this day like the Jews who said that while the guards slept my disciples came & took me away. They all profess to believe it to this day.

Behold saith the Lord let the Children of Zion understand that I the Lord have told them these things, that they might understand the things pertaining to Zion as they are before my face. That they may see that their salvation, and the salvation of all others depends on the faithfulness integrity and determination of one man separated from the whole world and all he had offered on the altar of sacrifice until he stood alone entirely separate from the church of the Devil. And this was his situation when I the Lord called him to begin to move the cause of Zion. And thus it was that I the Lord separated the priesthood unto myself that stands at the head of Zion. & I the Lord say unto the Children of Zion that there is no other man living standing in this point of light before me. And again I say to the Children of Zion that not only does the salvation of all who will be saved depend on him but all those who will be damned will be damned at his expense.
