He (the Lord) took a way to make the fact of his having chosen the place designated so that all might know it, First he told to one of his servants that he had chosen such a place in Canada to commence Zion. The servant to whom he gave the communication had never been there but the Lord gave him a description of it, and directed him to tell another of his servants to go and find the place he (the Lord) had described. The servant to whom this calling was made had never been there no more than the first. The second servant took the directions and started and after travelling hundreds of miles through districts he had never seen before, One night memorable in the history of this great commission, like an ancient prophet the visions of his mind told him to go to a certain place; he was now in a strange land. He rose in the morning and thither he went and behold the Lord was there!! He gazed on the platt of the Land, and made such enquiries as he thought necessary so as to be able to give counsel to the saints about going there and then returned.

A more perfect settling of a question never took place in the world than the one pertaining to the Lords choice where he would commence the foundation of Zion. From the days of Joseph Smith a great question was sprung on the children of Zion. Smith had left it on record that in case the saints were driven from their inheritance in Missouri so as to require it to be taken by blood, this could not be done until the saints had become a great Army. Now the question arose, where would the children of Zion get a place where they could thus be multiplied.
