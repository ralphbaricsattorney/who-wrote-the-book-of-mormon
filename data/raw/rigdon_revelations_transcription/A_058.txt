## heading: Section 30th Calling of 12. & 70
## date: Feb 27th[?] 1867

Br Post. I send you this notification as a case on which action on the part of yourself, br. & sister Newton is required. We say sister Newton for in all cases she by her faith steadfastness and love of the truth has obtained before the Lord, the same position you have obtained and right for her voice to be heard and respected as well as yours and br. N.s[?] in all matters pertaining to the progress & welfare of Zion.

The case we present is will it be in accordance with your minds and the spirit that is in you that br John A Forgeus be appointed a president over 12 to fill up the quorum before the Lord as the spirit of the Lord may direct him thus making him responsible before the Lord for what he does on the premises.

Through the spirit of truth we are informed that the time has come to move in this order for the purpose of completing as soon as practicable the full organization of Zion. Should it be approved of by you that J. A. F. be appointed to this high & holy position you will notify him of the fact and ascertain from him if he will accept it with a ready mind and a willing heart. and should he do so you will let us know it so that his papers can be made out and sent to you for your signature & from you be sent to him.

There is another president to be appointed but we submit that to you to make the nomination as we are not sufficiently acquainted with the priesthoods there to do it and it is but meet that you should incur a part of the responsibility in the organization now commencing. It is a president over 70 to be selected & filled up from time to time until the quorum of 70 is full. These two president[?] duties are to teach the members of their respective quorums their duties and to see that they are well instructed in the knowledge of the word of the Lord; as they are all to be travelling priesthoods. It will be the duty of these presiding priesthoods to call their quorums together & impress upon them the duties of their calling, and see that they are using proper diligence in understanding the knowledge of the things pertaining to Zion so that they may be able to teach the truth in righteousness. They will doubtless travel far off to carry the truth to those who are in darkness.

The whole of this matter will be under your supervision and it is expected of you that you will in this matter as well as all others see that the will of the Lord is done. After you have made your appointment of a presiding priesthood for the 70 you will forward the name of the appointee to us that his papers may be made out. It will be necessary that the names of those chosen by those presidents with which to fill their quorums be forwarded to you that they may be registered.

Up to this time we have not been able to put the great revelation on paper but will do so as soon as we can. The requirement to appoint the presidents was received within the last 48 hours.

## signed: As ever Sidney Rigdon
