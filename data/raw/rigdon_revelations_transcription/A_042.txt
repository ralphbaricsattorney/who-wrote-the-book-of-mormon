It is written in your books that the Lord your God would raise up and qualify a spokesman so that he could be a spokesman indeed being able to expound all scriptures, and to know the certainty of all things pertaining to the kingdom of heaven on earth, & that the priesthood of that spokesman should through the revelations put into his hands perfect his priesthood. Now saith the Lord to Zion and to the children thereof, has not this promise been fulfilled, judge ye; You also read in your books that when this spokesman with his priesthood perfected should make his appearance and go forth in my name that it should be for the "salvation of Zion" and of the "Gentiles" as many as would believe, and also for the "house of Israel" and was not that spokesman called by name. All these I the Lord did that there need be no mistake, and that none need be deceived, and if any were deceived that the sin might be on their own head.

Through the administration of this priesthood and none other was there Salvation promised to Zion, or any other organized body. Read and find out if there were any such made saith the Lord.

Let none mistake my word I do not say that individuals in all ages when I the Lord had an order for salvation established in the world could not obtain salvation and eternal life through obedience to my laws required in that order, though the order itself afterwards went to ruin through the transgression of those who were at its head, for many did do so. This Salvation of Zion in her organized form is what was set forth in my word. Now behold saith the Lord this salvation has come to the Children of Zion without labor or money on their part. But it was not so with the priesthood through which it came, it cost them both labor and money, yea they had to sell all they had and buy the field where the treasure was hid. I the Lord when I was in the flesh spake the parable referred to in relation to the kingdom as prophesied of by Daniel as also many others of my parables, my disciples who were with me understood this. And now behold I the Lord say to Zion and her children that the good things of the world have not been bestowed on this priesthood. I the Lord have placed it at the head of Zion in the condition in which its toils its struggles, its privations & its sacrifices to bring the salvation of Zion to you, left it: Because saith the Lord I have a duty for the Children of Zion to perform in relation to it. Before you came you were using your money for that which was not bread & your labour for that which profited not, but now through it saith the Lord you can use your temporal things for salvation, and I the Lord require this at your hand, that the priesthood delivered from cares and anxieties may attend constantly to the work unto which they are called otherwise the Children of Zion will stand condemned before me.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon.
