## heading: Section 68th. Rev. to Samuel E. Hughes.

The Lord required us in his name to say you had treated him the Holy one of Israel with contempt and his word with neglect and had taken satans emmissaries as your counsellors. The Lord says that when he gives a high office to those who profess belief in his word he requires of them strict regard for his word. When any do not that as you did at the Assembly follow after the teachings of one whom the devil had sent there and then trouble your brethren with your ignorant and corrupt letters you let all see where you are & what you have been at. Did I the Lord authorize you to receive the teachings of any only those whom I the Lord had chosen? Why did you not take your[?] place in the assembly and stand by your brethren instead of listening to the voice of the devil? I the Lord say to you in mine anger go to the next assembly and there confess your sins, and if you do not this let your[?] name be blotted from the book of remembrance from before my face, and let you be doomed to the blackness of darkness forever. I the Lord am not to be mocked by man.

## heading: Section 69th. On Representative
## date: July 29, 1867[?]

I the Lord say to the Children of Zion that if they have a perfected priesthood at their head they know not the toils the afflictions, the sorrows and the sacrifices through which it was perfected. I the Lord had that priesthood Perfected to be my representative on earth and to be in Zion as myself and this that the prophecies might be fulfilled which were spoken concerning me. When You read the 11th chap. of Isa. it is there said of me that there should be a rod come out of the stem of Jesse & a branch from his roots &c And then it is said what he should do, and among other things, the tribes of Jacob were to be gathered and righteousness and peace were to dwell on the earth. — Now how was all this to take place? It is spoken concerning myself. I the Lord could not do it personally myself, for I am according to the prophets, not to come the second time, till after Israel is gathered, therefore this could only be done by perfecting a priesthood that could represent me on earth. And again the same prophet in speaking of me as written in the 53d. of Isa. where he describes my humiliation and death; he then says, "That the Lord shall prolong his days and the work of the Lord shall prosper in his hands."
