## heading: Section 8th.
## date: Aug 4th 1864
## note: Item of Rev. from or via J. H. N.)

"I am directed by Father Rigdon to write to you, as he says he does not know your whereabouts It appears that the Lord has given you the high calling to go to the state of Iowa as the agent for the priesthood to purchase land and prepare a home for the priesthood. The promise originally was given to Br. Stanley to go alone on this business but as he lags behind because of his much business[?] I fear he will lose his crown and glory. But as Father Rigdon writes

"They may do as seemeth them good till events dreadful & fearful become their teacher, inasmuch as they will not receive any other But let them do as they may, it does not change our obligations to our Father above. If they are disposed to transfer the burdens of Zion on the heads of others, with these burdens they will transfer the glory also; for where the burdens rest there will the glory rest, I say then according to all the light which I can get of the spirit let Br. Post be the agent for the priesthood to obtain the place for it where they can come together. This duty among others belongs to his calling".
