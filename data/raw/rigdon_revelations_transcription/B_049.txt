## heading: Messenger & Advocate Extra.

¶1. Behold my servant Joseph Smith hath been a true and faithful prophet, seer and revelator to my people, and hath suffered many and grievous persecutions, afflictions and sorrows, and he shall in no case lose his reward. And he shall be a great king and imperial primate over all Israel.

But the time will come when he shall be gathered unto his fathers and another shall take his office.

Therefore thus saith the Lord God: Behold my servants Hyrum Smith & Sidney Rigdon shall be prophets seers and revelators, and first presidents unto my people the church of Jesus Christ of Latter day Saints: But they shall not officiate or usurp authority, while Joseph yet liveth. Yet they shall be with him in the triumvirate, & shall be viceroys in the executive dominion.

¶2 One of them shall survive Joseph, and shall be as Joseph unto my people. Yea he shall be the imperial primate to all Israel and over all authorities and ecclesiastical powers, and his councillers[?] shall be viceroys as in the reign of Joseph. And should I take the other to myself what is that to thee. It is meet that the first presidency thus constituted (The first president being imperial primate & his councillors viceroys; & the three composing the triumvirate) should govern my church and people with all and singular the plenary, temporal, and spiritual powers, prerogatives & immunities[?], which appertain to their high and honorable[?] calling, to act as the executive of my church and people.

¶3. Joseph Hyrum and Sidney are fruitful vines and in great wisdom shall they establish the saints in a perpetual foundation and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it or them.

¶4. For as the key of doctrine has been given unto Joseph So shall the key of conquest be given unto Sidney, And the great key of the Father shall be given and by them shall the saints prevail, and riches and honour and power, and dominion and great glory shall accrue to them and to their posterity after them for ever and ever. Harken unto me then my servant
