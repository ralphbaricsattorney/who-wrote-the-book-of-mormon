I fashioned him that he might be my companion eternally. To this end I numbered the hairs of his head that one could not fall without the notice of my eye. I made the thoughts of his heart audible sounds in mine ear, so that I the Lord could hear them, as man can hear the voice of his fellow man: When therefore man thinks, the Lord hears. The things pertaining to man with his whole objects, pursuits & purposes, are all present before me the Lord his God. He cannot deceive me. When he talks to me, or to his companions, I the Lord sees his heart and there can be no deception in it, but what I the Lord sees it, and when man is brought into judgment all these things will come up before the Lord, and all will shine before the assembled creation so clearly that none can fail to see. There every idle word and work will be brought into view, and the whole creations will say "Amen" to all that the Lord has done.

When I the Lord call any of my saints into a high & holy office, it will be in accordance with the eternal facts pertaining to the unchangeable laws of that calling; And if such do not lay it to heart to seek day and night to have their hearts conformed in "all" things to the duties and character of the calling whereunto they are called, wo! unto them for their end will be condemnation and not salvation. And instead of their working out their salvation they will heap up unto themselves "wrath" against the general day of "wrath" & righteous judgment of the Lord. Let none suppose after they have been called of the Lord, and accepted the call and are ordained to the duties of the calling, that from henceforth they are as they were before. Behold saith the Lord that from that time & forever they and all they have are mine unless they fall by transgression and are cast down to hell.

They cease to be self disposing and they and all they have are subject to the will of God.
