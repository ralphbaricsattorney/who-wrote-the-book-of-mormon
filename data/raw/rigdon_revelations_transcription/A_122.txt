## heading: Section 72nd. On Robt. Buhn
O Lord my heavenly Father in the name of Jesus I appeal to thee to be protected against Robert Buhn whom thou hast exhalted[?] on high by confering[?] on him a high and holy priesthood, who has used that priesthood to cast contempt on thine handmaiden by assuming to himself the powers which belong to the priesthood of thine handmaiden and none others in ordaining a woman without calling first on thine handmaiden that she might present the case to thee to know whether it was thy will or not to have it done. This is casting contempt on the priesthood of thine handmaiden. To whom can I look for protection but unto thee, and to thee O Lord I present my humble supplication. Amen
## signed: P. Rigdon.

In consequence of this awful and heaven daring abomination, the word of the Lord came to his servant Sidney, saying— "Verily, verily saith the Lord the everlasting God. Let the priesthood I the Lord bestowed on Robert Buhn depart from him forever, and let his name cease to exist in Zion. For I the Lord will not suffer that this holy innocent and pure priesthood which before me is but the impersonation of humility meekness, and holiness; should be insulted by any priesthood in Zion, from the revelator through every order of the priesthood in Zion. Amen."
## signed: Sidney Rigdon.

## heading: Section 73d Lesson 1st
## date: Dec. 28th 1872.
To the heads of the operative priesthood in Zion.

Behold saith the Lord Zion draggeth but does not walk. She is in the world but has no life imparted to her by which she can shew herself. Now saith the Lord that the condition of these things in Zion I the Lord your God charge on your heads before me. I your Redeemer have chosen you to see that the cause of Zion is moved before me. To this intent I the Lord hath caused[?] that the priesthood, which stands at your head, should in age & infirmity, & great weakness bring my words out of obscurity, and out of darkness, where it had laid buried for unnumbered ages past and placed it within the reach of the minds[?] of[?] those[?] who desire understanding pertaining to salvation. To you this source of eternal truth has been committed that through you it might make its way to the ends of the earth, and cease not till Zion triumphs over Babylon. Such is the greatness and grandeur of your Holy and glorious calling. It is designed of the Redeemer
