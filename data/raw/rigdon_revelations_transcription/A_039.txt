stand in the midst of this great conflict. The tears that they shed are bottled up in heaven, and their groans of affliction are written on the records on high. They ceased not day nor night to implore the guidance of the holy spirit, even when at labour & toil to procure enough to sustain life, their prayers and entreaties ascended up out of their hearts to the courts above saying O Lord deliver Zion, deliver Zion, and bring her out from the dust & place her on high. They besieged the heavens as the widow the unjust judge and would not cease day or night. They sought of the Lord their God to shew the evil of their own hearts that they might turn away from iniquity and be wholly the Lords. And at times when afflictions bore heavily upon them they would cry to the Lord their God & say to their Father; Though O Lord every feeling of our nature rises up and revolts at our deep and to us almost insupportable sufferings; yet would they say; Thou O Lord only knowest what are necessary for our salvation. Be not Thou therefore O Lord our heavenly Father governed by these our natural feelings, but if Thou seest that these afflictions or even greater are necessary for our salvation stay not thy hand in afflicting, for it is our salvation we seek. During these scenes of suffering and great affliction, they never ceased studying the books of revelation, praying continually before me for the holy spirit to enlighten their understandings, so that they might understand my will perfectly, and they never ceased doing so till I the Lord called them before me to recon with them. There were no sufferings, no reproaches, no privations, no shame, no disgrace, no hissings, no revilings could change the purposes of their hearts. Yea they brought all they had and laid it down at the foot of the cross. Behold, and lo! saith the Lord it was in this wise that the priesthood was perfected which I the God of righteousness have placed at the head of Zion. And now verily saith the Lord the Children of Zion owes it a debt of gratitude to them that they never can pay, for through them their salvation has come to them. They have thus laboured for their salvation till they have passed the age of man. Will not therefore the Children of Zion smooth down the path of life in old age. The Lord of light and truth has caused this to be written and not man that[?] the priesthoods as well as the Children of Zion may see and understand the relation they bear to the head priesthood. I the Lord said in a former revelation that I had raised up a pattern priesthood for Zion & now I have shewn you the pattern.

But behold saith the Lord I am not unjust that I can forget the work of faith and labour of love of my servant
