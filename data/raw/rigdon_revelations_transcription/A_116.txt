raised up fiends of hell no better than devils to steal his property till he was left without house or home. The devil through J. Smith having got all his followers who are now at Utah, to run greedily after pernicious ways turned them all on the spokesman. They published lies wrote lying pamphlets and circulated all manner of lies in every way they could invent. The devil to accomplish his destruction stirred to its foundation the wrath of earth and hell.

Such was the situation that the devil through his agent Joseph Smith on earth had got the work of the Lord completely destroyed: and last of all had got Brigham Young instead of teaching the word of the Lord presiding over a house of polygamy: And the habitation of holiness that the Lord had established entirely gone and the devil ruling the world with a stronger arm than before the book of Mormon came. I the Lord here give to the Children of Zion a short account of the condition of the Zion of their God when my spokesman which I had chosen began to build the Zion which had been spoken of by all who had ever spoken in the name of the Lord by authority since the world began; with the history of one of the most heaven-daring apostates that ever lived. I the Lord will now give you an account of the spokesman during this awful reign of corruption. It was ordained from the beginning with the powers and principalities above that the one that should obtain the priesthood of the eternal kingdom should by his own unceasing exertions and superior ascension in the knowledge of the truth and in understanding of the things pertaining to the house of the Lord, and the things thereof would by these lawful means secure the glory to himself.

Now as the kingdom which was coming into existence was universal in its character belonging to no particular nation but common to all. I the Lord after I had brought from the dust the book of Mormon, I caused it to be sent among all nations, I the Lord hastened it and rushed it through the world & brought together thousands of people. I the Lord your Redeemer did this to give an equal chance to the nations & give all a chance to shew from whence he should come, that the Holy One of Israel should appoint as ruler over all his goods. Of course to try all and prove all Satan was let loose among them, and instead of their seeking wisdom and understanding from me [the Lord] through the revelations they had received; they fell an easy prey to the devices and subtleties of the adversary and he led them captive at his will until they all became corrupted
