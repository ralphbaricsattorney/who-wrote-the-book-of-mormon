I can see by the spirit that satan is about to sow the seeds of discord among the Children of Zion just before the convocation so they will not be able to receive the blessings. I hope he will see his error and humble himself before the Lord and all the Children of Zion be united in heart & in mind and walk uprightly before the Lord that our heavenly Father will pour out a great blessing upon you. It grieves me to hear of any trouble among the Children of Zion. I feel anxious for the prosperity of Zion that it may continue to roll on until the world is filled with the knowledge of the gospel of Jesus Christ

## signed: From your sister in the Lord P Rigdon

## date: May 1st[?] 1868
