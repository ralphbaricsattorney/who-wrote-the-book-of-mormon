## heading: Minutes of a conference of the church of "Jesus Christ of the Children of Zion" held at the house of Stephen Post. Province of Manitoba Canada Ont. Oct 8th 1876

Conference met according to previous notice.

Stephen Post member of the 1st presidency and spokesman to the children of Zion presided and opened the meeting by reading the last page of the 12th chap. of 2nd Nephi then singing then prayer by the prest. President Post then stated the business of the conference that we commence a more perfect organization by expressing ourselves in relation to the official members of the church. It was moved and seconded that the church sustain Stephen Post in his office as a member of the 1st presidency & as spokesman & Counsellor to the Children of Zion Voted Unanimously. There were present besides the president Two members of the female priesthood (Jane Post & Louisa Sellers one high priest & member of High council (Thomas Sellers) one elder & member of the Quorum of 70 (Orson Hildreth) one deacon (Norman Sturdivant) & Four members (Sarah Hinkle, Elisabeth Hinkle & Susan Hinkle. & one priest Viz. (Andrew J Hinkle It was moved & seconded that the church sustain Thomas Sellers in the office of a High Priest voted unanimously It was moved & seconded that we sustain Orson Hildreth as a member of the Quorum of 70 Elders Voted Unanimously. It was moved & seconded that we sustain Jane Post in her office & Louisa Sellers as an Elder. Voted & carried Unanimously. It was Moved & seconded that we sustain Andrew J Hinkle in the office of A priest - Carried Unanimously, It was Moved and seconded that we sustain Norman Sturdivant in the Office of a deacon, Voted & carried Unanimously

It was Moved & seconded that this conference & members thereof extend the hand of fellowship & sustain all those officers of the church who have been ordained (except they have been disfellowshipped for transgression) inasmuch as they magnify their calling before the Lord. Vote unanimous.

On discussion relating to our next general Conference, It was moved & seconded that it be held at the house of Stephen Post in the province of Manitoba Canada N.[?] Ont. commencing on July 4th[?] 1877 Vote Unanimous. Moved that we adjourn, adjourned. Members of Conference present 2 members of 1st presidency Male & female one high priest one elder of female quorum one member of 70s one priest one deacon & 5 members
