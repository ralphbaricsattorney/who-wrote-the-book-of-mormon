Hence saith the Lord there was a covenant established in heaven that he and his seeds not seed as of one — but seeds as of many meaning his seeds according to the flesh should be heirs of the eternal glory pertaining to Zion which was brought forth by reason of his faith & sacrifices before the Lord.

I the Lord have looked on the family of my servant Sidney & inasmuch as he has dedicated them to me both he & his companion with all their heart, might mind and strength, because the persecutions lay so heavily on him though[?] they could not direct them as others had the privilege of doing, and they called on the name of the Lord their God both day and night in behalf of their children that I the Lord would take them & dispose of them. They did this in the time of their greatest affliction & the Lord their God must hear their prayers for so had he covenanted to do. In relation to this matter their prayers and supplications were as the woman before the unjust judge & they must prevail.

Therefore I the Lord claim them as my own to be disposed of according to my own will. For as I said in a former revelation, that it should happen unto children as seemed me good that I the Lord might require great things of their parents. So has it been with my servant & handmaiden Sidney & Phebe and in this instance as in others I have made them a sample to my people.

Let the Children of Zion even all of them have their children in remembrance before me saith the Lord & let them cease not day nor night to seek mercies for their Children, for it is the will of the Lord that all those who belong to Zion, when they come to rejoice in the covenant of triumph shall have their offspring with them. Amen.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon.

## heading: Section 38th Apt of Bishop Huffaker. July 13[?] 1868

Behold saith the Lord to the members of the quorum of Judges in Zion concerning my servant and your brother Israel Huffaker that he is a descendant from Aaron who held the priesthood in Israel & in the line of the first born & to him belongs the bishopric of Zion by right of covenant & none can deprive him of it. He can demand it & Zion must yield to the demand let him therefore take the office which has been secured to him by covenant with his original progenitor, And I the Lord require of him that he regards the duties of that office above his family his property yea even his life. If they all go but the law of this priesthood Must be before his eyes day and night or he will be found wanting in the great & terrible day saith the Lord Jehovah when I shall recon with the world and call my saints home to their eternal rest You will find the law governing the situation of this bishop in the D.C.
