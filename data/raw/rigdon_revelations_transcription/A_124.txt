Remembering that it is your own salvation you are working out, and unless you do all things as you are commanded you will fail to do it. The Father gives the law of "your" calling to you with this impressive attestation "That to those to whom much is given much will be required." Seeing then that the highest place in the Zion of our God is conferred on you the Lord gives you the law of "that" calling and to you it belongs. Let others do as they may, and let their callings vary according to circumstances yours remains the same during your life times without change.

You will also inquire diligently into how all others are discharging their duties and see that those who profess to be of Zion obey the law of their calling. Forbid all Idling among those who have been ordained. Either let them go out as the spirit directs you, or let their commissions be taken from them.

Those who have been ordained to the Female priesthood if they are negligent in making themselves acquainted with the lessons they have received, cause such to be admonished; and should they then fail in their duty let their names be blotted out.

Arise ye servants of the Lord arise and see that his house is put in order before him.

You will see the[?] Lord has called you to be separate from the world leave it, having no more to do with it. Amen

## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## heading: Section 74 Communication on spirits
## date: Aug. 1873

Br Post: I commence writing to you because the Lord so requires in order that your attention may be directed more profoundly to the things of God than ever before.

In order that I might have a more extensive acquaintance with the things of God pertaining to Zion. I have been at different times seeking for many years to understand the mysteries pertaining to the world of spirits. I mean to that world where the disembodied spirits dwell after they are separated from the body. But never till yesterday did the veil rend so as to let the divine light of heaven shine forth.
