To the presidency of Zion a requisition of the Lord

The work in which you are engaged is the redemption of "Zion". The time has now fully come for the moving of the cause of "Zion". When you search the word of the Lord, you will there learn how "Zion" can be brought forth out of darkness and out of obscurity. It is not every work of man that will do that, however devout he may appear to be.

The Lord has given us direct rules & counsel by an obedience to which we can bring "Zion" out of obscurity.
