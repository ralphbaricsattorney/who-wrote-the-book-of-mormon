## heading: Section 24th
## date: Dec 1866

"Verily verily saith the Lord God of Zion to his servant Sidney behold if your brethren the priesthoods of Zion are as faithful in keeping the commandments they have received and will hereafter receive through your administration, as you were before me in the days of your greatest affliction, I the Lord your God swear by myself because there is none greater, that they shall never fall neither shall Zion perish in their hands but they shall triumph gloriously and Zion with them. Therefore let the Children of Zion rejoice before me, for their Maker is their husband, and their Redeemer is the holy one of Zion and through them shall he be called the God of the whole earth."

## signed: S. Rigdon.

## heading: Section 25th
## date: Dec. 1866
## note: Rev. on promises.

"Thus saith the Lord, When high and holy promises are made to men stretching forth to things within the veil: These promises with me saith the Lord your God are not yea and nay but yea & amen. And when those to whom they were given by their disregard of the laws of the celestial kingdom, and their want of obedience to my word, place themselves before the Lord their God in a situation in which I the Lord cannot fulfil the promises that have been given to them the promises are not withdrawn from them but they remain before their face as tormentors to rack their souls forever and forever.

And again behold saith the Lord the heart of my servant Sidney is sending up prayers and supplications to me with great intensity pleading with me continually that I the Lord would forgive his brother Joseph his transgression saying that he is willing to suffer all the reproach that he may have to endure therefrom. Therefore I the Lord say unto my servant Joseph go forth in the power of your high and holy calling knowing that your trespass is remembered no more, but be faithful in the trust committed to your charge and sin no more & your promise shall be sure & reward certain, Amen.
