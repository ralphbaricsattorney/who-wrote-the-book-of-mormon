The army of the Lord were to be mighty and powerful, when it marched to hurl down the powers who had usurped dominion over them; but where they were to be situated in order to gain this strength and power were not made known till now. But it is now in possession and this much we have obtained above all others, for none others but ourselves, know any thing about it, but without this knowledge none can be saved.

The Father has held this awful fact from all living but has now made it known to those whom he had chosen. It is as much an essential to salvation now as believing is, to obtain and possess the place the Lord has chosen where he will establish Zion. In the first instance the place the Lord showed to you, is the place where you shall begin as a kind of Centre. But in the whole country to the Pacific you can place the people of the Lord as convenience may suit.

Zion goes to this land as a first home or earthly inheritance. I surely need not say to you that the Smiths failed most awfully in giving Zion an existence at all, When they were cut off what was left? nothing but a rabble of liars & slanderers, having corrupted the people who followed them, till they were much more abominable than were the rest of the Gentile world, fulfilling all the books of revelation that the last end of such a people is worse than the first.

The Smiths themselves had reached a depth in iniquity that could not be found elsewhere this side of hell. Zion perished in their hands as perfectly as it did in the hands of the devil in the spirit world. The two cases are perfectly similar only the devil before he fell had gained a larger fold than had the Smiths, but the triump of evil in both cases was the same.
