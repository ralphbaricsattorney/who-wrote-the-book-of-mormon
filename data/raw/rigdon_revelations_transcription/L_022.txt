Paul speaking of the resurrection[?] says in 1st Cor. 15: 36[?] & 37 That thou sowest, is not quickened, except it die; and that which thou soweth, thou sowest not that body that shall be but bare grain it[?] may chance of wheat or of some other grain. The grain lies in the earth in a state of death & dying while a germ springs forth for the resurrection. Man lives alone on the products of a resurrection. All things pertaining to life comes to him through a resurrection. Whether he sows or whether he plants he symbolizes death, he puts all into the ground as into a grave and covers it up or buries it From thence by a resurrection he looks for the fruit of his labor.

Not only his bread but the water he drinks comes to him by a resurrection. The rains descend from above on the earth and sink[?] [illegible] into[?] it[?] till[?] they reach a grave[?], [illegible] has there prepared for them into which they enter, & from thence as by a resurrection they come to him for his use, or else he digs to the grave where they are and raises them himself.

So man eats, and so man drinks by the symbolization of death and the resurrection, and cannot get food and drink in any other way. Symbolize death & the resurrection he must or perish he will. O man look at yourself and receive instruction, for why will you be in ignorance, while all nature is teeming with intelligence for your salvation.
