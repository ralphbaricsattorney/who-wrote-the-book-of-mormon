## heading: Section 85
## date: 1873 Dec 24th

The Lord now deigns to answer the request concerning the tithing. Behold saith the Lord it is my will that the Children of Zion should pay tithes of all they possess and I require that my servant Israel Huffaker should set the example to the rest of the Children of Zion in offering his tithes of all he has and then as Bishop of Zion call on all the rest to do likewise. O Israel marvel not for the day and time has come for revealing hidden things and behold they of your forefathers are standing here & they know of your position in Zion and watch you with deep solicitude lest their race should be disgraced by your disobedience. Phineas whose zeal for the salvation of Israel was such that the overthrow of their enemies at Baal Peor came by his single arm will be had in eternal remembrance; he says to me saith the Lord your God "tell my son Israel a name continually revered by all the righteous ones of the holy family whether on earth or in heaven have their eyes on you and fear lest there should be any failure on your part of honouring the family. We all rejoiced when you reached the place you now occupy at the very dawn of Zions existence, and we wait to receive you when you have finished your work on earth. But should you fail where we are you never can come but you shall be cast down into an eternal hell so deeply that your groans and wailings will never be heard by us to disturb our joy and peace in our everlasting rest.
