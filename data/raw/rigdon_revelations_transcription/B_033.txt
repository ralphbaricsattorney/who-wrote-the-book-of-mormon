revelation for the coming forth of Zion.

That he might be qualified I the Lord required that he should become acquainted with the history of nations, peoples, countries and kingdoms, with the laws of God and man, as also to be mighty to expound all scriptures, and that he might understand all things pertaining to the kingdom of God on the earth: And in the ancient prophesies[?] it was said of him that the Lord would give him the tongue of the learned. Such is the character that I the Lord your God present before you in the person of the head of the second dispensation under the ruling of the book of Mormon.

The perfecting of the ministry was a work of great labor before me saith the Lord; for upwards of 25 years my servant Sidney whose surname is Rigdon laboured before me the Lord his God while villains were stealing his property and ceased not until they had left him without house or home, having stolen all he had until he was left as I said to my disciples of myself "the Redeemer" "The foxes have holes and the birds of the air nests but I have not where to lay my head." The two scoundrels who did this work was George W. Robinson who had married his daughter, and a connection of his own with him whose name was John Olney. I the Lord cause their names to be recorded that they may be had in a time to come when the workers of iniquity shall be called into judgment. I the Lord will say that no two men were more corrupt and heaven daring since the days of Cain than they are.

Having given the character of the priesthood I the Lord your God will give you the reason why he was called to suffer, and through suffering to perfect the priesthood. From days of old it had been appointed that the house of Israel should be gathered in the last days, & established never to be thrown down, and I had said to you that it could not be done only by power, and after I the Lord had designated so clearly that rejection of that priesthood would procure the damnation of all who did it, and had set forth how that priesthood had to be perfected for the work whereunto it was called. I the Lord caused it to be written that the arm of the Lord should be stretched out in power for the salvation of Israel.
