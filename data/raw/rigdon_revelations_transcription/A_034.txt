## heading: Section 21st
## date: Oct. 16th 1865

The word of the Lord came to me saying; Say to my servant Stephen Post and my servant Joseph H. Newton to arise & prepare themselves to go forth in my name saith the Lord, & in the power of the calling wherewith I the Lord have called them & be diligent & faithful before me the Lord your Redeemer in teaching my word to those who have professed my name in all that region of country, & call upon all such who are connected with you to go also and let none who are capable & chosen remain in idleness while thousands are perishing for lack of knowledge. Let this be done and fail not, that there may be a people prepared to receive my saints in the day when destruction & desolation shall come on the Gentiles to the very uttermost. For I the Lord have stretched forth my hand for the overthrow of the nations which have rejected my word & none can turn it back. For to the intent that my word saith the Lord might be made known to the people who have professed my name, have I the Lord planted you in the place that I had chosen And the time has now fully come for you to go forth & call on those who are connected with you to go also. For I the Lord say unto you that the people who profess my name are before me as were the disciples of John in the day when I was with them, they need to be taught the way of the Lord more perfectly.

I the Lord your God have placed the lives of your families and friends whose deliverance you desire into your hands and their escape in the day of vengeance, depends on your faithfulness & diligence in doing and ceasing not to do as I require. Amen.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## heading: Section 22nd
## date: Mar. 27th 1866

Br. Post: Yours of the 17th from Glenwood came to hand yesterday & this morning I commence to answer it, not of myself nor by myself but by the requirement of my God & your God And though it places me in a most peculiar situation to one whom I acknowledged to be a prophet of the living God, indeed I knew him to be such. And did I not know that he fell by transgression, it seems to me I should faint under the responsibility that is now laid upon me; But as I am writing for & to one of the Lords servants, and for his use and not mine, I must give him the word of the Lord as the Lord gives it to me, for the Lord by his spirit says to me, that if the questions his servant Stephen has sent to him through me are not answered his servant Stephen will not be able to act in good faith before him pertaining to these things.
