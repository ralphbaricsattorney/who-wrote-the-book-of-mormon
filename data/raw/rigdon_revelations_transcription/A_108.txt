Behold! saith the Lord to this end you were called into a solemn assembly that as many of you as had come to understanding might unite with zeal and energy to disseminate the understanding you had on this subject of the righteousness of the kingdom to others.

When you assembled I the Lord came also, for I was there, and as was always the case ever since there were a people of God on the earth, when they came together, Satan came also, he was there. This was permitted that those who were the Children of Zion might have an opportunity to shew themselves as such in the presence of the Devil. I the Lord your God was looking on and hearing, and verily, verily I beheld and heard that there were Children of Zion on the earth, and I the Lord took a record of it and had it given to the angels round the Throne and they are rejoicing over it. In order that Zion might exist it was indispensable the priesthoods both Male & female should be found standing in their place boldly though mildly rebuking Satan and nothing daunted at his fury. I the Lord was pleased yea greatly delighted at the manifestations made by my handmaiden Jane. I the Lord say this because she was the first who sustained her calling in a manner worthy of its dignity.

My Young hand maiden Evva has deported herself before me saith the Lord as becometh one in her calling. The devil was permitted to attack her in his usual way of merciless abuse but she has overcome nobly and now stands approved in the dignity of her priesthood.

Inasmuch as she has youth on her side let her apply herself to the study of the word of the Lord that her profiting may be manifest for many years to come that she may be able to hold the holy priesthood in righteousness and great ability whereunto she has been called with a high and holy calling.

Having brought Zion into existence by your faith and perseverance, your salvation and glory depends on perfecting the work of righteousness. Your way is clear before you. You have reached your present position saith the Lord by a strict attention to the book of Mormon and the former commandments you had received. A perseverance in the same course will bear you off triumphant and give you a place with the church of the first born whose names are written
