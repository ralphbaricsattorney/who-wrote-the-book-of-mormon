## heading: To the presidency of Zion a requisition of the Lord

The work in which you are egaged[?] is the redemtion[?] of "Zion" The time has now fully come for the moving of the cause of "Zion" When you search the word of the Lord you will there learn how "Zion" can be brought forth out of darkness and out of obscurity It is not every work of man that will[?] do this[?] however devout he may appear to be The Lord has given us direct rules and counsel by an obedience to which we can bring "Zion" out of abscurity[?].

The revelations which the Lord had given a long time ago tells us expressly that former attempts had not brought "Zion" into existence what other things it had done thereefore[?] he caused to be written by the same revelater[?] through whom he caused the book of Mormon to come forth how "Zion" should come forth after the floods of corruption had spent their force and Satan had accomplished all he could accomplish to prevent it. This the Lord shews us could not be done by merely gathering many people together for this had been done and proved a failure

After all these abominations all could see, had wasted themselves and only fitted the actors therein, for the perdition of ungodly men the few who had withstood the whole flood
