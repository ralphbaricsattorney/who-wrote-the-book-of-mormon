They saw them coming from the east the west the North & the South their sons coming from far and their daughters from the ends of the earth, flocking like doves to their windows & when they saw they were astonished with great amazement & said of Israel I am a widow and sit desolate, having lost my children, and these from whence have they come for more are the children of the desolate than of the married wife. So greatly they saw had Israel increased in their dispersed situation. They saw the messengers from Zion hunting Isral[?] & fishing them from the dens and caves of the earth whither they had been driven in the days of their calamity. They beheld them triumphing over their enemies; the Gentiles bowing to them and the kings of the earth submitting to their rule and saying our fathers inherited lies and things in which there was no profit. So great was this work of gathering Israel that after it was accomplished that it never should be said again in Israel: That the Lord liveth who brought the children of Israel out of Egypt, but the Lord liveth who brought them out of the North country & from all the countries whither they had been scattered. They did not only see the gathering of Israel but they saw it was done by power with a high hand, a stretched out arm & wrath revealed as they were brought out of Egypt at the first. All things written in books, concerning the gathering of Israel, the salvation of Zion, & the redemption of the world belong to the priesthoods & to the Children of Zion. Therefore saith the Lord let the children of Zion yea all of them read all things that have been put in their hands by the spirit of Revelation that they may see the field of enterprise achievement and glory which lays before them.

In order that the Children of Zion may come to understanding saith the Lord it is of importance that they should be made acquainted with the manner of the Jewish prophets in writing and their peculiar form of expression. They called Zion a mountain; the mountain of the Lords house &c And Esau they also called a mountain & spake of the mountain of Esau. These forms of expression were based upon a law given by Moses, which was that the authorities of Israel when they pronounced blessings should do it on mount Gerizzim.
