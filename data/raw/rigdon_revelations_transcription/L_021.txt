It is those represented by the filth of whom your distinguished revelater said, "He that is filthy let him be filthy still." (Rev 22c. 11th v.) This saying of the apostle had reference when he called these castaways filthy to the filth we cast into the sink. Man[?] is always[?] representing[?] death and the resurrection. All things which pertain to his temporal existence decays & has to be renewed, and this is unceasing through his entire life, and says to him in a language he need not mistake, "You, like all things pertaining to you, are decaying and will have to be renewed. As you can now see upon what principles & by what means you have your temporal existence let the spirit of Holiness give you understanding in relation to your spiritual existence.
