And now saith the God of Zion: I appoint my servant Stephen Post to be spokesman to my servant Sidney and to the children of Zion and my servant Sidney shall be revelator & expounder to him. And I the Lord will give unto my servant Stephen great wisdom if he will adorn that wisdom with humility & prudence, And his voice shall be heard in Zion as the voice of a mighty Counsellor, and he shall be clothed with righteousness as with a garment, and he shall be great in mine eyes, for I the Lord his God will give him power to withstand all the enemies of the truth & overcome them if he will walk before me & be perfect, but let him beware of pride which bringeth condemnation.

And I the Lord say unto my servant Joseph H. Newton let him attend to the calling wherewith I have called him, and let his voice also be heard teaching the things pertaining to the Zion of his God: But his mission is more immediately to the Gentiles wherever a door is opened, preaching the gospel to them, & expounding my word, and I the Lord will stand by him and give him power to confound them Let him also where occasion offers enforce my word upon them of the old church & if they reject you and my word it will prove to their condemnation. And the Lord your God command you that you stand by my servant Sidney & forsake him not, minister to his necessities that he also may rejoice with you in your success. Finally walk before me in all righteousness & faith be ensamples to the children of Zion in all holiness of heart & godly conversation that your profiting may appear to all & I the Lord your God will give you great power and a glorious triumph.
