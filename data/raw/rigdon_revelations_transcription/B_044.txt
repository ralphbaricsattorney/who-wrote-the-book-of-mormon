Let all the people of God that now or hereafter may exist know this saith the Lord your God. For from the voice of the earliest prophet there never was but one spoken of to come in the last days to gather Israel; and I the Lord gave notice of this fact by the prophet through which the book of Mormon was brought. I the Lord said by him when speaking or revealing the gathering of Israel that I would call on "one" of My servants. Here is the closing of former prophecies in the case. The great thing now laying immediately before those[?] of the children of God, is, to prepare for that great event when your leader will come to you clothed with his proper authority and qualified for the accomplishment of his high & Holy commission[?] For unless I the Lord had sifted the nations till such an one was found, proved & qualified the book of Mormon would have been sent into the world in vain.

Let each one then awake to the sense of his & her calling, for behold! my priesthood cometh.

## signed: Yours as ever Sidney Rigdon.

## heading: Section 97. Br. Huffaker

Your 8$[?] has been received I am required of the Lord to say to you that your whole transaction in this case is an abomination in his sight In making your covenant as you did not with me saith the Lord but with yourself. You took the whole case into your own hands to direct & do as you pleased. I the Lord have something to do with this matter. indeed I have all to do with it. You are only my agent to do just as I direct; But instead of your doing so you took power and authority to yourself to say to one I will do so, if you will do so; You have attempted to do this until the anger of the Lord is kindled against you and you are nigh unto cursing awful & eternal.
