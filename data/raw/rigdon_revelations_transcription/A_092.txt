## heading: Section 56th. Rev. To Ermina Page.
## date: Feb 12 1865[?]

"Now I the Lord say to mine handmaiden Ermina awake Ermina awake cast off the works of Babylon & shake the dust of her corruption from your garments, and lift up your head and rejoice for behold the day of your deliverance draws near and the day of joy and gladness to the daughters of Zion. A jubilee of release a time of breaking of bands that the subjected may go free. For I the Lord appoint unto Zion and her children a quorum of female prophetesses that shall sit in the councils of the most high, and their voice shall be heard in the celestial order which I the Lord organize for the ruling of Zion, that the daughters of Zion may have security and deliverance from oppression under their sacred protection, and fear and sorrow depart from their hearts. For Zion is not based on any covenant upon which my people have been founded which have gone before you: but upon the new covenant which was promised to the house of Judah in the last days under which covenant iniquities were to be pardoned & sins remembered no more. Under all former covenants there was a remembrance of sins and the daughters of my people were subjected to the sons of my people, because woman was the first in the transgression thus having a remembrance of sins: but under the new covenant sins were to be remembered no more, And where sins are not remembered the consequenses of sin must cease.
