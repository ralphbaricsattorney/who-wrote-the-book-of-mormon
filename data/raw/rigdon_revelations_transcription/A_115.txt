again I the Lord would shew unto my people why Joseph Smith was chosen when all must know that the Lord your God well understood that he would fall and be cut off before he was called. The Lord your Redeemer he who now speaks to you will shew unto you how it came to pass. Joseph who was in Egypt by reason of his great power in the world and knowing that in the character he was sustaining in the world that he represented one who in the last days would redeem Zion, for Joseph was a man who understood the things of God. Getting lifted up in the pride of his heart, desired of me his Redeemer that as the name Joseph was glorified above all the names in the world. So he desired of me, that when the time came for the final redemption of the world that the Seers name who should bring to light that work should be Joseph because he wanted the name Joseph to be glorified. I the Lord gratified him in his desire for it was a principle with me your Redeemer, then as now that when my servants desire a thing at my hand I grant it though I know it will turn to evil instead of good. But though I the Lord granted him his request I made provision for a spokesman in the covenant that when he failed the spokesman might accomplish the gathering of Israel for it was for this cause the book of Mormon was brought forth and not for the purpose of building Gentile Churches, All which was introduced through the transgression of Joseph Smith. When the devil had him fairly under his influence he [Smith] began to give revelations for building Gentile Churches and to this end came the revelation of polygamy which ignores the gathering of Israel altogether. Puts at defiance the book of Mormon, and casts off the doctrines & covenants & substitutes polygamy for all of them.

Hence the devil made a subtle attempt through him that had been chosen, to bring forth the work of the Lord to entirely prevent it and divert its whole operations into a channel to accomplish worldly purposes & gratify sensual pleasures. Add to this the attempt of Satan through him to destroy the spokesman I the Lord had caused to be provided for in the covenant. No spirit of darkness which had sallied out of the habitation of fiends could have fallen upon one whom the devil dreaded with more fury than this fiend of hell, for such he had become, did on my servant Sidney. The devil fully believing that if he could get him out of the way he could then effect the destruction of the whole church.
