Now I the Lord call on you most solemnly, and by the authority I the Lord God Omnipotent have in Zion, to hear, and not only hear, but devoutly attend to the things the Lord your God say unto you. For behold there have none stood before me in the Holy office you hold who have made less effort to qualify themselves to discharge the duties of your calling than you have. All others have held their meetings of prayer and fasting before the Lord, seeking both knowledge and preparation of heart to prepare them for the duty of going out to build the kingdom of heaven. And that the spirit of holiness, which is the spirit of your priesthood might dwell in your hearts. Those meetings have been before me your Redeemer Continually with other priesthoods which have held the same office before you. Great exertions were made by them that they might come to understanding so as to see eye to eye. Hence they met frequently in the room they had provided for this purpose, and in view of this duty the Father has directed you to prepare a house for yourselves where you can hold your meetings alone when you desire. The time has now come when if you desire salvation you must close up all other business retire to your room and prepare for the work of redeeming Zion in earnest; and as opertunity[?] offers preach the gospel in season and out of season, and cease not from henceforth to send the gospel of salvation to all who will hear, and devote yourselves unceasingly to the work of the ministry whereunto you are called. Cause your voices to be heard in every place where your voices can be heard and seek diligently for places where you can find those who will hear. In doing the will of him who sent you, read the history of those Nephites who left all and went every where to get those who would hear the truth, and they would never yield the conflict until they found such.

Let no earthly consideration interfere with the duties of your ministry. Let others sow and reap and gather into barns, and apply the tools: but let none of these trouble you. Instead thereof, when you are not preaching, be before the Lord studying his word, and seeking wisdom and counsel at his hand; for you must give yourselves to reading and much study.
