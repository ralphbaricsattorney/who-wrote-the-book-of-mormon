What I the Lord send to one people, I send to "all" people of every tongue, language, and kindred under heaven; and at all periods in the history of mankind; whether to your father Adam or to you the proclamation and promises are the same. Adam was the first to whom it was given, and you are the last. It is through[?] the same gospel testified to Adam by the voice of Angels, even those who sang together and shouted for joy when the symbol of the resurrection burst upon their eyes when light was commanded to appear and did appear around the new created earth which till then was covered with thick darkness, that I the Lord have caused to be proclaimed to you in this my last dispensation. For as the world after the transgression at the first, was preserved in existence that the descendants of Adam might avail themselves of the blessings of the gospel which had been prepared before the foundation of the world. So it is in accordance with the Law of the Gospel that it should be sent forth now for the last time, in order to wind up the whole affairs of the world. Now saith the Lord Behold I have given this greatest of heavens gifts to the Children of Zion that through them I the Lord may establish righteousness on the earth and bring about the redemption of the world, and I have deigned to give to them the evidences of their high calling and their relation to me the head of Zion. Therefore the promises connected with the gospel are being fulfilled in your midst. I the Lord your God gave to my handmaiden Evva the vision through which her eyes were opened to see things which to the natural eye are invisible that all of the Children of Zion might know that Zion is mine saith the Lord your God. Verily, verily, saith the Lord from henceforth let the Children of Zion beware how they conduct themselves before the Lord. For since the signs of the gospel has confirmed the truth before their eyes they can have no excuse for their sin. The gospel has been proclaimed to them with signs following, and I the Lord designed to have it so. There are no other people on earth to whom the truth has been confirmed as it is among the Children of Zion therefore from henceforth you may know with as much certainty as you know the difference between day and night that Zion is with you and none others. Amen
## signed: Sidney Rigdon, Phebe Rigdon
