## heading: Section 26th
## date: Dec. 1866

To the Zion of our God.

"Behold saith the Lord it is my will that the Children of Zion should understand the character of the salvation which has come to them through the ministration of those whom I the Lord have chosen. Therefore I the Lord say to all the Children of Zion that I the Lord their God chose my servant Sidney to be the head of Zion even before he was born and he was mine from his mothers womb. I the Lord his God watched over him from his cradle to the present moment. The integrity of his heart I knew. The honesty of his soul was always before my eyes long before I sent him the fulness of my gospel. For him only did I behold among the sons of men whose intention of soul & purposes of heart were such as to warrant the Lord your God in chosing him for this high and holy calling. I the Lord made him mighty in the earth among the great ones thereof from the beginning. Even in his youthful days I made him one to be wondered at. As he advanced in life I made him a terror to those who opposed him, until his name became famous in the midst of the great ones of the earth. I the Lord also chose his companion for him one the innocency of whose soul, and the guileless character of whose heart adapted her to the position in which I the Lord placed her. Hid from the eyes of all living did I the God of Zion prepare a priesthood to stand at her head and through which I the Lord will rule her destinies. but it was but mete that I the Lord should take this my priesthood and learn them obedience through the things which they suffered, that in all things they might be perfect before me. Therefore I the Lord let satan drag them through the furnace of affliction, waste their substance, hurl on them his vengeance in fury, overwhelm them in sorrow & grief such as few have ever endured before the Lord your God, Those for whose salvation they were suffering were many of them assisting the devil to destroy them, and none were sympathizing with them, though I the Lord had pointed out in my word the priesthood which I had chosen, with clearness so that all might see and understand: but my word was cast behind their backs, and they ran greedily after the imaginations of their own hearts, and mine anointed priesthood was left alone to bear the violence of the devil. with none to help.
