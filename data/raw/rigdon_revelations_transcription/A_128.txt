I the Lord know that you have treated the Lord without the regard that was due to him from you, for you are indebted to him for all you know or will ever know about the things of eternal life for all the knowledge there is in the "world" pertaining to eternal life has come through him. Amen. Again I the Lord say unto you that apart from his teachings every thing you have and do will work out destruction to "itself" & damnation to you. All of the world that heareth not neither obeyeth the voice of his instruction goeth down to hell. Therefore while it is today lend an ear to the voice of your salvation that you may escape the impending vengeance.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon, Phebe Rigdon

## heading: Book of revelations Through the Holy Ghost to Sidney Rigdon who was appointed and ordained through Joseph Smith Jr who was called by the voice of God to be a prophet seer & revelator.

## heading: Revelation Given March 17th 1856

Verily, verily Thus saith the Lord unto my servant Stephen Post I have looked upon thee and seen thy works and thy desires to understand my revelations, and I have heard thy prayer. And now I the Lord say unto thee that I call to a great work in assisting my servant Sidney Rigdon in prepareing the way before me, and Elijah which should come, And I say unto thee, that as my servant Sidney Rigdon assisted my servant Joseph Smith with all his might mind and strength that my word might be brought forth to the children of men for their salvation and for the salvation of the house of Israel mine elect; So have I called thee to assist my servant Sidney Rigdon to send forth my word in deed, and in truth, and in power for the salvation of mine elect, and for the redemption of Zion. And if thou wilt receive instruction from my servant Sidney thou shalt have the desires of thy heart for thou shalt understand all my words; not only those which I have brought forth in these last times, but also those which thou hast had from thy youth; And thine heart shall be full of light, and I will give unto thee wisdom and understanding such as is not common to man. Thine eyes shall see mine elect gathered, and Zion redeemed, and thou shalt shout hosannahs in the midst of my people while Babylon shall shake and tremble, and the inhabitants thereof shall quake with fear, and howl, and weep, and mourn for anguish of soul; even so, amen.
