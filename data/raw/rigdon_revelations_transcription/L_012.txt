Let the children of Zion know that their Lord and their God can look upon this no longer without his wrath being kindeled. Let the money and means of Zion be used for the benefit of Zion, and not wasted on her Enemies— I your Redeemer know the hearts of all living, and I know what the children of Zion can do, and what they can not, and by that knowledge I will judge them, I do not ask the children of Zion to make their priesthood rich,
