## heading: Section 54.
## date: April 1869
## note: Recd May 5th

To my servant Stephen Post saith the Lord whom as spokesman to the first great priesthood of Zion, and the head thereof, as the agent of the Lord the great God the Everlasting Father, and the prince of peace.

Behold I the Lord tried to organize Zion under the Celestial law, which was the most perfect law ever delivered to man, Under this law the quorum of the first presidency was brought together. The celestial law required that each individual of the quorum should stand individually for him and herself so that they need not teach one another but each one should individually know the Lord for him or herself and thus move before Zion in perfect harmony. But instead of this I the Lord beheld a spirit, an aspiring spirit get into the hearts of some of the members of that quorum, a desire after supremacy, and a thirst for authority and power, seeking to be great in the world. I the Lord saw this and knew it, and saw this foul spirit destroy the harmony of the quorum.

But verily saith the Lord I was dealing with man with the Children of Zion, and it was let go until it would reach a place where it would manifest itself so clearly that all the Children of Zion could see it, that when I the Lord acted that all the Children of Zion might see the cause and the necessity therefor, Now behold saith the Lord there were prayers, supplications and entreaties coming up before me continually from some of the Children of Zion that I the Lord would fulfil the promise made from days of old as well as in these days, that spiritual gifts should be bestowed on those who obeyed the gospel. I the Lord since the coming forth of Zion began to fulfil the promise: But Lo & behold! what do the Children of Zion see and hear, nothing less than the members of the first, (or some of them at least) presidency crying the devil, and throwing Zion into awful confusion with their cry. Thus surely the Children of Zion can but see that there had got a spirit into the hearts of the first presidency that called the Lord their God the Devil to his face.
