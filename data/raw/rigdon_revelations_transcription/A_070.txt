## heading: Section 42nd On sealed plates.
## date: Oct 1868

To the first presidency of Zion

Thus saith the Lord the God of Zion, knowing the feelings of the hearts of all living and looking at the hearts of the Children of Zion as well as that of others & seeing their anxiety to grasp after unrevealed things, when they know there are matters of great consequence in reserve to be brought to light in due time, and seeing their inclination to form conjectures in relaton to the character of those things and form opinions concerning them which are not correct, All of which are calculated to lead them astray. And seeing as I the Lord do that such things are now among the Children of Zion in relation to the sealed plates which will eventually lead to discord unless the things sought after are placed in a situation to obviate all difficulty & darkness from the face of the subject. Inasmuch as I the Lord desire to have peace and unity among the Children of Zion, I will as far as wisdom in me requires give to my people an understanding of the character of the things which are engraven on the sealed plates. Let the Children of Zion therefore know that the record spoken of differs from the book of mormon in its being historical instead of doctrinal.
