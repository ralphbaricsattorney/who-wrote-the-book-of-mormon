## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## heading: Section 46 To the Children of Zion.
## date: Nov. 1868

Let all the Children of Zion hear saith he who holds the keys of David (or of power) who openeth and none can shut and shutteth & none can open who giveth understanding to those who obey him & wisdom to them who will receive it. For from the beginning it was decreed and none could disannul it nor stop the gates of revelation, that to those who received the word of truth should the voice of revelation be heard, giving truth and light with grace and knowledge so that they might be lifted on high above all the peoples of the earth. For it is the wisdom that descends from on high above all the peoples of the earth. For it is the wisdom that descends from on high which exhalts[?] a people & makes them truly great.

Therefore verily saith the "I Am" (The Lord Jehovah) it is my will that you should understand things yea great things which are hid now and were hid from the ages and generations past and are now making known to you.
