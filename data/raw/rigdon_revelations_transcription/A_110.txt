## heading: Section 67
## date: Apr. 6th 1872

As directed by the spirit we inform the operative priesthood that the notification by which Br. Huffaker was warned to sell and pass on to the west side of the Mississippi was not intended for him alone; But it was in addition the commencement of the present order pertaining to the Children of Zion, All are to be notified as fast as any case may transpire, that in connection with their faith they should pass over to the west side of the Mississippi, and there where they can most conveniently do it get a home and settle for the present, keeping out of Missouri and Kansas. The faith of none in the salvation of Zion (— and there is no salvation apart from Zion —) will be available unless they strictly obey this counsel. For destruction cometh and none can escape unless they flee before it. For as a whirlwind it will come upon the inhabitants of the earth (sec. 104 P 9th D.C.) The destruction testified of here is the great destruction spoken of by all the holy prophets since the world began. And now since the Lord has acknowledged Zion as founded, he notifies her priesthood to give warning to all those who are the children thereof or who through their ministry may become such, to escape to a place of safety. That you may see the entire force of what the Lord says in relation to the people of Zion, (see sec 98 Ps 6 & 7) D. & C.) and you will not only see the importance of attending to the admonition but the awful consequences of neglecting it as manifesting itself concerning those to whom it was first spoken. Put all together and it places a most important matter pertaining to Zion in a forceible point of light, that of the watchman on the tower. Since the Lord has thus early introduced it in Zion.

Without that there can be no Zion, men can build what they please but they cannot build Zion; this the Lord alone can do, and he has so arranged the case of Zion that none but himself can do it, for none others let them do what they may, can qualify a watchman. This arrangement will protect the Elect so that they will not be deceived; though the devil deceives all the rest, he cannot deceive the elect, for when they come to look for the watchman, behold he is not there & where he is there is Zion: You will cause this to be read in the solemn Assemblies.
## signed: Phebe Rigdon, Sidney Rigdon
