I the Lord did this not only to supply the wants of my servant and handmaiden, but as an expression of my Anger to you. Who are you that casts contempt on the Lords servant? Did you think you were dispensing penny to a beggar when you sent your 16$ I the Lord whose eyes are open saw you at that & my anger rose & beware lest it kindles into indignation and you fall among the damned to rise no more, for if you fall from where you now stand you will sink to hell to rise no more.

I the Lord now give you a law concerning my Holy priesthood on the fulfulment of which depends your Salvation.

You shall cause to be paid to my priesthood of the revelations 50$ every three month. I the Lord require this of you because I the Lord know that by proper diligence you can do that. On it hangs the issues of you life & death, Salvation or condemnation.

The above is the copy of a letter that the Lord required me to send to I.[?] Huffaker & also to send a copy to you.

## signed: Sidney Rigdon

I have done as the Lord required the case is between you and your God

## heading: Section 98.
## date: Apr. 27 1876

To the Spokesman of Zion.

This Morning the Lord requires of Me to say to you Call a council of five High priests and if that number is not there make up the deficiency by ordination and through that court devote — to the eternal damnation of Hell for slandering Jehovah & failing to do his will & that the Lord through his mouth piece on earth has thus required.
