## heading: Section 100. Copy of a letter from Phebe Rigdon
## date: June 15th 1876

Br Post: I am under the necessity to write you a few lines at this time to inform you of the calamity that has befallen my husband he fell and got badly hurt, he was sitting in his chair he got up on his feet and fell down on his back. he is not able to move himself it takes 2 strong men to lift him in and out of bed. He appears to be parallyzed[?], he has no use of his hips to move or take a step I do not know what will be the result. I cannot let myself think for a moment that our Heavenly Father is going to take him away at this time. I think of all the promises that has been given to him. If you have faith I pray you will all raise your hearts to the Lord for his recovery I am alone in the faith the only one to say a word to is my husband. He is not able to converse much at present O May your faith be strong that you may call upon him for the recovery of his servant

## signed: Your Sister in the Lord Phebe Rigdon

## note: Sidney Rigdon died July 14th 1876 Aged 83[?] years 4 months & 25 days.

## heading: Friendship July 20th 1876[?] Br Post:

I received your letter but had not time to write my husband lay very sick he had a shock of paralysis, he was helpless not able to get out and into bed; it took two men to lift him out of bed. The most of the time he was deranged, he complained of his head, a dull heavy feeling. He was sick six weeks, he lay on his back all the time, He was patient[?] through all his sickness.

The Lord has taken him to that world where parting is no more. I feel myself alone we lived together 56 years. You must think I feel his loss he has been very weak and feeble for 6 or 7 years he has been like a child he could do but little for himself but he is gone, I feel his loss greatly. I have my children all around me except one she lives in Pittsburg my children are kind, we have lived with our daughter and her husband" (Wyngate[?]) "for 7 years. I held on to the promises almost to the last of his life. I saw he must go my heavenly Father has taken him to himself O may we all be faithful & live humble before the Lord that we may meet him where parting is no more. He died the 14th. of July, he was in his 84th year I hope you will remember me in my situation before the Lord it is my desire to be faithful & walk humbly before the Lord.

## signed: From your sister in the Lord Phebe Rigdon
