I the Lord your God now call you to come forth in the power of your calling and of your ministry and join your brethren in causing the appeal which is my testimony to your former brethren to be sent forth among them that they may have the opportunity to recover themselves from the condemnation which is resting upon them for I the Lord deign to give them through the priesthood of Zion by my testimony in their hands this last opportunity to cease their rebellion, and turn to me & be saved: But if they will not hear, neither your testimony nor mine saith the Lord, I hereby give to the priesthood of Zion power to seal the condemnation resting upon them upon their heads, that the Lord may cut them off by the judgments of the Gentiles.

It is therefore my will that when you have taken my testimony and yours to any society of those who are organized, and they wholly reject you or any portion of them do it, you shall return alone to the nearest running stream, and shall there wash your feet as a testimony against them; and then lift your voice to heaven, testifying to the powers above that you have discharged your whole duty to them, and they have rejected both your testimony and mine. Then will I their Lord and their God seal upon their head the condemnation. under which they are resting: But all must be done in a meek and quiet and humble spirit. And again I the Lord your Redeemer say unto you that I have put at the head of Zion my servant Sidney Rigdon as the organ through which I shall speak to you in all matters pertaining to Zion, and none other shall you receive; for him only have I proven before my face, by tribulations and afflictions, buffetings & scourgings. For Satan stood before me and claimed the right to scourge him in a similar way as he did Job, believing that he could overthrow him. And I the Lord knowing that when the heart of Satan was once set on overthrowing one of the servants of the Lord, that he would do so if he could and no power could prevent him unless he was annihilated.

Satan believed that his situation at the time he made this demand, was such, as would enable him to do it. he had seen in my servant Sidney a high & lofty spirit which he had inherited among the Gentiles.
