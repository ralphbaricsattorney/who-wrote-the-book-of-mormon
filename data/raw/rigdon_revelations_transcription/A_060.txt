that all things may be done as becometh the house of the Lord Now saith the Lord unto my judges whom I the Lord have chosen out of all the world to judge of all things pertaining to Zion:

It is required that you should be prepared in all things on that great day to stand in your holy callings in all righteousness and dignity. To this end let my servant Joseph take my servant Stephen and let him be washed in clean Water before me saith the Lord; and then let him anoint him with the "holy oil" of consecration; And inasmuch as my servant Stephen has magnified the first priesthood he received from me.

After the anointing let my servant Joseph and mine handmaiden Sarah lay their hands upon his head and ordain him to the holy calling of Prophet priest & king before me saith the Lord.

And then let my hand maiden Sarah bless him in my name according to the power of her holy calling. Thus putting the quorum of the first presidency in perfect order before the Lord their God. There is but one ordination higher than this in the callings of God, and there are none on this earth who have that priesthood, save him who stands at your head. One other had it, but he has fallen by transgression.

Let my servants Joseph & Stephen see that the 12ve are also washed and anointed with the Holy Oil of consecration before they are presented for ordination & organization. In doing so in righteousness with prayer yea much prayer & supplication before the Lord you prepare the way for your triumph. For it is my will saith the Lord that this quorum of 12ve that I the Lord have chosen out of the whole world should be the mightiest quorum of 12ve that were ever on the earth

## signed: Sidney Rigdon

## heading: Section 33d. On J. H. Ns teaching wrong
## date: May 1868

"Verily, verily, saith the Lord let no person holding a priesthood as from me throw themselves thus before the "car" of my queenly priesthood of holiness through which I the Lord am myself teaching Zion & her children. And again I say concerning my servant Joseph that he has suffered evil to get in his heart before me & in consequence of that the Devil through his deceitful arts was moving on him that through him he might sow the seeds of discord among the Children of Zion and thus hinder the progress of Zion. Let my servant Joseph therefore saith the Lord, search out the evil in his heart before me and repent thereof that it may be well with him."

I warn br Newton in the name of my heavenly Father, to stand in the place the Lord has called him. It is the principles of obedience is to be taught in the book of Mormon & the other books the Lord has required. I say beware Br Newton
