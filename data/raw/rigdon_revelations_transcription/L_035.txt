Bro Robinson, I Copy from the Record the above Revelation, and I Am Sure Your heart will Rejoice when you Read and Behold the profound wisdom of our God in Separating the Rightious[?] from the wicked, before Zion is founded in Everlasting Strength, for how Could this be done without a Separation, seeing that Zion is the pure in heart, Even the Holy ones,— I think it was nearly one year before this Revelation was Given, that I dreamed a dream, And Could I see you and tell of it, you would see the Connection perfectly,— However from that time I testified to all, that before Zion is Redeemed there must be a Separation of the Clean from the unclean, the pure from the impure,— And I saw only about 6 that was Righteous out of quite a multitude of people,— And they seemed to Gnash their teeth with raging anger at me and those with me, But they had no power over us to do us any harm— And now Dear Bro, you may imagine My joy and Gratitude to my God when this Revelation was Received, which was on the 3rd of July the day before our Convocation, when we met and organized that Kingdom which according to the word of the Lord to J Smith Should be desolved, and truly it was desolved the very day it was set up, or the Separation Commenced that day, and Consummated the next March, by the Devil introducing his Spiritualism in our midst,—

## date: June 7th 73
## signed: Jos. H. Newton
