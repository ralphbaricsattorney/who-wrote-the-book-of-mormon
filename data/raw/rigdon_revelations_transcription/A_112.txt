But let the Children of Zion read and they will see that all such prophecies could only be fulfilled by a representative. — But the prophecies of Isa. refered to were spoken concerning myself, for it was a rod out of Jesse, and a branch of his roots that was to do the work there spoken of, thus making it refer to me your Redeemer, for I am of Jesse; But my servant is not of Jesse, nor of Judah, but of another tribe even Ephraim. I the Lord direct the attention of the Children of Zion to these things that they may come to understand all the prophecies of this character.

If fulfilled it must be done by an agent who can represent me your Redeemer in the world. For this cause I the Lord have perfected a priesthood to represent myself. It is I your Redeemer who is doing the work as testified of by the prophets but I do it by a representative, — a priesthood I have perfected for that purpose, therefore let all the Children of Zion know when they say or write any thing to my representative that they are saying it or writing it to me, and if he is troubled with questions or Opinions of men, it is I who is troubled

## heading: Section 70 On Canada presicy[?]
## date: Oct. 1872
The word of the Lord to his servant Jesse Crosby whom I the Lord have chosen according to the election of Grace and now calls to preside over the department of Zion in Canada. Behold saith the Lord thou hast been separated unto the great work of the Lord thy God out of obscurity and this because the Lord thy God judgeth not as men judges but according to righteousness. The Lord looks at the heart for he knows the hearts of all living. None can practice hypocrisy before him but he sees it. None can lie and he not know it: None can dissemble & he not see it. None can practice deception and he not perceive it. It is not in the power of man to conceal one thought of his heart from him with whom you have to do. A man need not say before the Lord I cannot do, when he can; for the Lord knows he can do it. Behold and Lo! saith the Lord, consider the character of him with whom you have to do, and fear & tremble in his sight; for you will be brought for every departure from the law of your holy calling into judgment. Verily saith the Lord many have fallen, after having received the Holy priesthood, because they did not regard the strictness of the law, and satan took advantage of their transgression, and carried them away at his pleasure and cast them down from whence they never can recover.
