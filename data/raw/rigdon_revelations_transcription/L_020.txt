It is through this resurrection the flocks mature their fleecy robes to be transferred to man for his comfort and ornament. Thus it is that man eats & drinks & wears by reason of the symbolization of death and the resurrection.– Man lays down to rest at night representing death, he rises in the morning typifying the resurrection.– Man can exist by no other means, only by the figure of death & the resurrection

So completely is he surrounded and environed with it, that there is not, nor cannot be an act[?] of his life for good or evil, but what is in accordance with it. It is before him, round about him; It besets his path go where he will, or stay where he may; all that is in his power to do foreshadows death and the resurrection.– Behold, & lo! saith the spirit of holiness I shew unto you a great mystery in relation to man, for he is a living and walking symbol of death and the resurrection & eternal judgment.
