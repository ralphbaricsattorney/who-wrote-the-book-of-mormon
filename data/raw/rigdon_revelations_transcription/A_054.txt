You know saith the Lord that you do nothing nor say any thing, nor think any thing but your Father in heaven knoweth it, so do you deal with each other in all things pertaining to the cause of Zion. Be as open to each other as you are open to your Father in heaven, so that you may see each other as you are seen, and know each other as you are known. Finally let your love be without dissimulation, your intercourse without hypocrisy & your union without discord for so is the will of God done in heaven. As there were none others through whom the Lord could establish righteousness and holiness on the earth, you must needs walk in holiness before me, and no unholy thing can come into your counsels, lest satan trouble you. I the Lord have made you judges between the clean and the unclean, the holy and the unholy from days of old you were known as the judges of the mountain of Esau, and it is for you to judge of all things pertaining to it.

When complaints are prefered[?] before you, it is for you to judge whether they are holy or unholy, and if you see they are unholy you shall refuse to hear them, for the devil will try to get you to judge of matters of his own creation in order if possible to pollute the head of holiness in Zion. When therefore complaints are prefered[?] before you by your brethren one against another it is for you to decide whether the complaint is holy or not. If on examination you should discover that the complainant or the complainants, have some personal feeling to gratify, some sinister ends to accomplish some desires of their own heart to gratify, or something else besides the welfare of Zion is in their hearts I the Lord pronounce that complaint unholy, and you shall not hear or entertain it for a moment but shall judge the complainants themselves instead of the one or ones complained of and rebuke them for not having preserved themselves in righteousness before the Lord, but had given place to the devil so that he could use them to throw corruption into Zion.

I the Lord give you a test by which you can try every case which comes before you. When complaints are brought before you you shall require of the complainant or complainants to testify in your presence and before the Lord that the case they have presented to you they had had before the Lord and by devout prayer & supplication had sought of the Lord his mercy to be extended not only to themselves but to the person or persons complained of that the Lord would have mercy on them & convince them of their error and bestow his spirit upon them so that they might be powerful in fulfilling the duties of their calling and that the Lord would sustain them
