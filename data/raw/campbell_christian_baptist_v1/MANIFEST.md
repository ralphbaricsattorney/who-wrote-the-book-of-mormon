# Alexander Campbell (ed.), *The Christian Baptist*, vol. 1 (1823–1824)

- **Source:** https://archive.org/details/cbv-1-sipdf ("The Christian Baptist, Volume 1 (1823–1824)").
- **Fetched:** 2026-11-12 from archive.org (metadata API + `_djvu.txt` OCR download) via `scripts/ia_get.sh` (or direct curl for items whose OCR filename contains spaces). Generic client.
- **Cleaning:** `*.clean.txt` made by `scripts/build_cowdery_and_ocr.py`: de-hyphenation across line breaks and joining of wrapped lines only. OCR errors, running heads, page numbers and library stamps are NOT removed. Raw OCR kept as `*.djvu.txt`.
- **Files:** `cbv-1-sipdf.djvu.txt` (~172,000 words), `.clean.txt` (~170,000 words), `.metadata.json`.
- **Edition caveat:** the scan is the **1955 Gospel Advocate Co. (Nashville) reprint** of the 1827 Buffaloe, Va. edition of vol. 1 — i.e. the reset text, not a facsimile of the 1823–24 issues; the reprint adds a note on its title page.
- **Authorship caveat:** monthly periodical *edited* by Campbell; most essays are his (e.g., "A Restoration of the Ancient Order of Things" begins in vol. 2), but the volume also contains letters/essays by correspondents (signed e.g. "Paulinus", "Philip", "Timothy") and reprints. Segment by signature before using as a pure Campbell sample.
