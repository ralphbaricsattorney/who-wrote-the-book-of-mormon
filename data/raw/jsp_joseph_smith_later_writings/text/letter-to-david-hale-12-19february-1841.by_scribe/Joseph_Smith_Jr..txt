Mr. David HaleAs Lorezo and Emma have givean me space for a few lines, I gladly imbrace it, to send to you and yours my sincere and heart felt respects, and I do honestly think if you will come to this country, and settle it would be much to your advantage. I [will] do what Lorenzo has stated and even more I will help you to improve the land; also, [we] want to see you all very much well settled in this country.
[Yours respectfully, Joseph Smith].
