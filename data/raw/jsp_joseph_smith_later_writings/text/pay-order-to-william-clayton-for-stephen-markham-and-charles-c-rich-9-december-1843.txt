Nauvoo City De[r]. 9th 1843Mr. William Clayton Treasury for the City of Nauvoo you will Please pay out of the City funds in your hands Sixty dollers to be paid to the Commandants of Cohorts to be used in case of defenceJoseph SmithMayor of said City
The commandant of the 1st. and 2nd. cohorts is, of the 1st. Col. Stephen Markham in the absence of his Senr. Officers, and Genl. Charles C. Rich of the 2nd..
Wilson Law
Major. Gel.
Nauvoo Legion
