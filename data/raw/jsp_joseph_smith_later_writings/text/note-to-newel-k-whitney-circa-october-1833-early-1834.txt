Brother Newel K. Whitney I write this because I forgot to tell you of some things that you ought to know Doctor Philastus Hurlbut is commenccing an unjust suit against Brother Hyram [Hyrum Smith] to git the property of this farm which belongs to the firm Brother Hyram or father has not got any property here but one cow each I have a bill for all the rest made over to me more than one year ago for Books and what they owed me and it will involve me or the firm if we let them take this property which you may rest asured belongs to us a word to the wise is sufficientJoseph Smith Jr
