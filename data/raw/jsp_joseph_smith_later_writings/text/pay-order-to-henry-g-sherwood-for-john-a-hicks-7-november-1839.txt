Springfield November 7th. 1839
Mr. Henry. G. Sherwood
you will please to pay John. A. Hicks fifty Dollars and charge the same— to me Yours &c
Joseph Smith JrThe Above order is for mony to bear our expences on the road he will take a lot or land if you have any to please him if not you can sell the lot that Br— Jonathan Hampton was to have I have seen him hes paid me the damage &c &cJoseph Smith Jr
Estate
Estate of Jos. Smith decd
Filed 27 May 1845
David Greenleaf PJP [probate justice of the peace]
