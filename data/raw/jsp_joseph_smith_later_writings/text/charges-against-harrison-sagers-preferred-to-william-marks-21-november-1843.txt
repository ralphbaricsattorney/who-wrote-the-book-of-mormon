Nauvoo City November 21st 1843Br William Marks Dear Sir I hereby prefer the following Charges against Elder Harrison Sagers namely——1st for trying to Seduce a young girl living at his house by the name of Phebe Madison——2d for useing my name in a blasphemous maner by Saying that I tollerated Such things in which thing her is guilty of Lying &c &cJoseph Smith
