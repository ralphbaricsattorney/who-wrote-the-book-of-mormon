To the Rev. Mr. Hewett John Hewitt.
Sir, In consequence of your not returning to us as we understood you at your introduction to us. It was resolved and approved in council, on the evening of the 14th Inst. that the bearer Oliver Cowdery, one of the presiding Elders of our church, should proceed to Fairport &c. and ascertain, if possible, the cause of your delay. and this is done as one reason. that we feel an anxious desire, for the Salvation of the souls of men, and to satisfy your inquiries concerning the religion we Profess. If at Fairport it is the sincere desire of the council that Mr. Hewet return, that we may satisfy him; concerning our Religion, and we satisfy us concerning his, for we feel as great a desire for the welfare of his people as he can have for ours.
With respect & esteem
I am, Sir, your friend
William W. Phelps Clerk
Kirtland. June 14 1835
