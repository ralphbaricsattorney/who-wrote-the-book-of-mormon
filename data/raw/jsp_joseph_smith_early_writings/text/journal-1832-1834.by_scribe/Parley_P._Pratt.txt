Journal of Parley P. Pratt and JS
4–6 March 1834 • Tuesday–Thursday
March 4th, took our journey from Westfield accompanied by Brother [John Gould] . Rode 33 miles, arrived in . Stayed all night with a Brother [Reuben] McBride . Next morning went 4 [miles] to Brother [Freeman Nickerson ]’s. Found him and
his household full of faith and of the Holy Spirit. We called the church together and related unto them what had hapened to our brethren in Zion . Opened to them the prophecies and revelations concerning the order of the gathering to Zion and the means of her redemption. And Brother Joseph prophesied to them, and the Spirit of the Lord came mightily upon them, and with all readiness the young and middle aged volunteered for Zion.
Same evening held 2 meetings 3 or 4 miles apart . Next day, March 6th, held another meeting at Brother Nickerson ’s. The few unbelievers that attended were outrageous, and the meeting ended in complete confusion.
7 March 1834 • Friday
Started on our journey accompanied by Brother [Freeman Nickerson] , leaving Brothers [John Gould] and Mathews to prepare and gather up the companies in the churches in that region and meet us in Ohio, ready for Zion, the first of May . We arrived after dark to the
county seat of Cattaraugus, called [Ellicottville] . Tried every tavern in the place. But being court time, we found no room but were compelled to ride on in a dark, muddy, rainy night . We found shelter in riding 1 mile. Paid higher for our fare than tavern price.
8 March 1834 • Saturday
Continued our journey . Came to [Farmersville] to the house of Elder [Marcellus McCown] . Were invited to go to Esq. Walker ’s to spend the evening.
We found them very friendly and somewhat believing. Tarried all night.
9–12 March 1834 • Sunday–Wednesday
Sunday the 9th, held meeting in a schoolhouse . Had great attention. Found a few disciples who were firm in faith and after meeting found many believing and could hardly get away from them . We appointed a meeting in Freedom for Monday the 10th and are now at Mr. [Warren Cowdery ]’s in the full enjoyment of all the blessings both temporal and spiritual
of which we stand in need or are found worthy to receive. Held meeting on Monday . Preached to crowded congregation . At evening preached again to a house crowded full to overflowing . After meeting I proposed if any wished to obey, if they would make it manifest, we would stay to administer at another meeting . A young man of the Methodist order arose and testified his faith in the fulness of the gospel and desired to be baptized. We appointed another meeting and the next
day, Tuesday the 11th, held meeting and baptized Heman [Hyde], after which we rode 9 [miles]. Put up with . Next day rode 36 miles to Father Edmund Bosley’s.
13 March 1834 • Thursday
Held meeting. I preached.
14 March 1834 • Friday
In Father Alvah Beaman’s.
15 March 1834 • Saturday
At Father [Alvah] Beaman’s, and Brothers Sidney Rigdon and Lyman [Wight] arrived at his house to the joy of our souls in [Livonia].
16 March 1834 • Sunday
Brother [Sidney Rigdon] preached to a very large congregation in Geneseo.
