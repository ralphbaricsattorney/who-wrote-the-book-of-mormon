Continued at Springfield until Tuesday the 8th. Journeyed that day to Brother Shadrach Roundy ’s at Elk Creek . Tarried there overnight, came the next day to a tavern. The next day, Thursday the 10th, we arrived at Brother Job Lewis ’s at Westfield. The brethren, by a previous appointment, met there for meeting . We spoke to them as the Spirit gave
utterance. They were greatly gratified. They appeared to be strong in the faith. Left there Friday the 11th and came to the house of an infidel by the name of Nash. Reasoned with him but to no effect. Came Saturday the 12th [to] the house of Father [Freeman Nickerson]. Monday evening arrived at Lodi . Had an appointment, preached to a small congregation . Made an appointment for Tuesday at 10 o’clock the 15th . The meeting was appointed to be held in the Presbyterian meetinghouse,
but when the hour arrived the man who kept the key of the house refused to open the door . The meeting was thus prevented . We came immediately away and left the people in great confusion . Journeyed until Friday the 18th. Arrived at [Eleazer] Freeman Nickerson’s in Upper Canada, having after we came into Canada passed through a very fine country and well cultivated and had many peculiar feelings in relation to both the country and people . We were kindly received at [Eleazer] Freeman Nickerson ’s.
20–25 October 1833 • Sunday–Friday
Sunday the 20th. Held meeting at Brantford on Sunday at 10 o’clock to a very attentive congregation . At candle lighting the same evening held meeting at Mount [Pleasant], where [Eleazer] Freeman Nickerson lived, to a very large congregation which gave good heed to the things which were spoken . What may be the result we cannot tell, but the prospect is flattering . This morning, Monday the 21st , enjoy pretty good
health with good prospects of doing good. Calculate to stay in Canada until the Monday of next week, then, the Lord willing, will start for home. Left Mount Pleasant Tuesday and arrived at the village of [Colborne] . Held meeting at candle lighting . The evening was very bad, snowing vehemently . We were publicly opposed by a Wesleyan Methodist . He was very tumultuous but destitute of reason or knowledge. He would not
give us an opportunity to reply . This was on the 22nd . We find that conviction is resting on the minds of some . We hope that great good may yet be done in Canada , which, O Lord, grant for thy name’s sake. During our stay at Mount Pleasant , we had an interview with a Mr. Wilkeson of the Methodist order, being a leader in that sect . He could not stand against our words . Whether he will receive the truth the Lord only knows . He seemed to be honest.
Written at Colborne , Wednesday morning the 23rd, at the house of a Mr. Bemer. Left Mr. Bemer ’s on Thursday the 24th , came to [Waterford] . Held meeting at 1 o’clock. Spoke to a small congregation, being a very wet day . After meeting returned to Mount Pleasant and held meeting at candle lighting to a large congregation . One man, Eleazer Freeman Nickerson, declared his full belief in the truth of the work and is with his wife, who is also convinced, to be baptized on Sunday . Great excitement
prevails in every place where we have been . The result we leave in the hand of God. Written at the house of Eleazer Freeman Nickerson in Mount Pleasant on Friday morning the [25th]. Sunday the 27th. Held a meeting in Mount [Pleasant] to a large congregation . Twelve came forward and were baptized, and many more were deeply impressed . Appointed a meeting for this day, Monday the [28]th, at the request of some who desire to be baptized . At candle lighting held a meeting for confirmation . We broke bread, laid on hands for the gift of the Holy Spirit. Had a good
meeting . The Spirit was given in great power to some, and the rest had great peace . May God carry on his work in this place until all shall know him. Amen. Held meeting yesterday at 10 o’clock. After meeting two came forward and were baptized . Confirmed them at the water’s edge . Held meeting last evening. Ordained Brother Eleazer Freeman Nickerson to the office of elder . Had a good meeting . One of the sisters got the
gift of tongues which made the Saints rejoice . May God increase the gifts among them for his Son’s sake. This morning we bend our course for home . May the Lord prosper our journey. Amen.
29 October 1833 • Tuesday
Left Mount Pleasant for home.
30–31 October 1833 • Wednesday–Thursday
Continued on our journey Wednesday, and on Thursday the 31st arrived at Buffalo.
