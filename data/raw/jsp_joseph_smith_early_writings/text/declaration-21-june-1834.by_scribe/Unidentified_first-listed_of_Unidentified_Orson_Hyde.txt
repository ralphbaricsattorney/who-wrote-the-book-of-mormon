Being a Citizen of Clay County and Knowing that there is considerable excitement amongst the people thereof and also Knowing that diferent reports are arriving almost hourly and being requested by the Hon John F Ryland to meet the Mormon army and obtain from the leaders thereof the Correctness of the various reports in Circulation & the true intent and meaning of their present movement and their views generally. regarding the dificulties existing betwen them and the people of Jackson County I did in Company with other gentlemen Call upon the said leaders of the mormons at their Camp in Clay County. And now give to the people of Clay County the substance of what passed between us. Cornelius Gilliam
