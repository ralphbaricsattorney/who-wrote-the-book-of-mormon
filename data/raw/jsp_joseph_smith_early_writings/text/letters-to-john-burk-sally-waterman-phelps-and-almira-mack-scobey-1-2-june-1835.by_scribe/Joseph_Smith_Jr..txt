Joseph Smith, Jr.Cousin Almyra, Scoby [Almira Mack Scobey]
 Brother W. W phelps has left a little space for me to occupy and I gladly improve it, I would be glad to see the children of Zion and deliver the word of Eternal life to them from my own mouth but cannot this year nevertheless the day will come that I shall enjoy this privileege I trust. and we all shall receive an inheritance in the land of refuge which is so much to be desired seeing it is under the direction of the Allmighty therefore let us live faithful before the Lord and it shall be well with us I feel for all the Chilldren of Zion and pray for them in all my prayrs peace be multiplyed unto their redeemtion and favor from God AmenJoseph Smith Jr
