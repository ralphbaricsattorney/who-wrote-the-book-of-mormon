To Moses Daley
(An extract of a letter sent by me to the Florence church and vicinity— ‘I have inquired at the hand of the Lord concerning this matter’ ‘and have engaged our bro. Wm. E. McLellin to visit you who will teach you concerning the duty of the churches relative to Zion. I want therefore that you should receive him in the name of Lord, and uphold him by your prayr of Faith, and in the name of the Lord of truth he will do you much good— Joseph Smith Jr)
