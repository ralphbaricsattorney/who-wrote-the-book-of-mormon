Kirtland, June 22, 1835.
To the Editor of the Painesville Telegraph:
Sir:—In a late number of your paper the fact was noticed of my being bound over to the Court of Common Pleas, to keep the peace, for an assault upon the person of my brother-in-law: Since my honorable acquital before said court, last week, there being no evidence to prove the same, I believe you will do me the justice to make the last as public as the former, and oblige
Your ob’t serv’t,
JOSEPH SMITH, Jr.
